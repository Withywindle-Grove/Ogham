# Changelog

What's new in Grimoire, newest first, in plain words. Each milestone gets an entry when its gate passes.

## M5 — into the dungeon (November 1, 2026)

- **Sessions and the clock.** The GM starts a session and an expedition; Next turn moves time on for everyone at once, and the strip shows the mode, the turn, the day and the time. End a session in the middle of a dungeon and the next one picks up where you stopped ("Resumed: the place, turn 6").
- **Light.** Light a torch or a lantern, watch it burn down turn by turn, put it out and keep the time left, refill a lantern from a flask. When a group has no light, a reminder says so and offers to light one.
- **Reminders and checks.** Wandering checks and your own reminders (rations every day, a check every 2 turns) come up on the turn they're due, with Hit and Miss and a roll on the right table. Results stay secret from players until the GM reveals them.
- **Marching order and splitting up.** Arrange who walks where, save orders you use often, split the party into groups in different places, and join up again when they meet.
- **Statuses.** Put a status on a character for some rounds, turns, hours or days; it counts down by itself, and some stack.
- **Rest.** The app says when rest is due; rest, or push on and take the penalty.
- **Weather and the calendar.** Players roll the weather, and its effects show; the date and season move with the clock.
- **Places.** The GM keeps notes on each place and shows players its description when they arrive; split groups each see only their own place.
- **Messages from the GM**, and **escape rolls** when leaving a dungeon, for settings that use them.
- **Safer and steadier.** Signing out on one device no longer signs you out on the others. Pop-ups open over the whole screen on an iPad turned sideways. Players can't reach another group's hidden place or the GM's weather table.
- **Still to come.** The table told us a lot: M5b brings guided character creation back with more help on every step, a reference screen for the GM, simpler messaging, rest as a shared prompt, and a calmer players' screen.

## M4b — every class, automatically (October 28, 2026)

- **Guided character creation.** Make a character step by step: how to roll, ability scores, race and class from your setting (with what each needs and allows), hit points, starting money and languages. Or go freeform and fill the sheet yourself.
- **Your setting fills things in.** Values the setting works out (saves, attack, movement, literacy and more) fill themselves in, and tapping one says how it's worked out.
- **Starting gear and kits.** Shop for starting gear, or take a ready-made kit: armour goes on, a weapon goes in hand, and the rest goes in the backpack.
- **Drafts and the GM's review.** A new character stays private until you send it. The GM sees what's waiting, can approve it or send it back with a note, and you can fix and resend.
- **Equip warnings.** Wearing armour your class can't use is allowed, but it says so plainly (for example, it stops casting), and Undo is one tap away.
- **The Player screen.** HP with a damage pad (Damage or Heal, then one tap), AC and attack at the top, then light, spells, abilities, what's at hand, skills, saves and coin.
- **Spells and ability uses.** Prepare spells from your class's list, shown by level; tap dots to mark an ability's uses.
- **Companies.** Characters can found a company together and invite others.
- **Giving part of a stack.** Give some torches and keep the rest. If you undo it later, whatever is left comes back.
- **Calmer menus.** Each ⋯ menu has symbols, the device settings at the bottom, and Sign out last.
- **Still to come.** Character creation and spells get redone in M7, with the lessons from this milestone's test.

## M4 — inventory and encumbrance (October 24, 2026)

- **The app is Nemeton.** New name, new look for the name on the sign-in screen, and sign-in emails come from Ogham, the app's voice.
- **Your Pack.** Every character has a Pack tab: what's worn and in hand, what's in each bag, what's carried. Add items from your setting's shop list (grouped by kind, with a search), say whether you bought them or got them free, and put them where they go. Tap an item for its details, then give it, move it, wear it or drop it in a tap or two.
- **How much you carry.** A bar at the top shows your load and how fast you move, using your setting's own rules: by armour and treasure, by weight in coins, or by slots. Crossing into a slower pace shows a short note and goes in the log.
- **The Party stash.** One shared pile for the whole group, at the bottom of everyone's Pack. Anyone can put things in or take them out.
- **Make your own items.** Not on the list? Make a one-off item; the GM approves it, and can add it to the campaign's homebrew so it's on the list from then on.
- **Wands and charges.** Items with charges count down as you use them. Some settings keep the count secret: then only the GM knows how many are left.
- **Secrets on items.** The GM sees what's hidden about an item (a curse, a true bonus) and can reveal it to whoever holds it.
- **At hand.** Your Player screen shows what's in your hands and anything you pin there, so it's always one glance away.
- **Arrange your screen.** Show, hide and reorder the sections of your Player screen.
- **Bigger text.** Pick Standard, Large or Larger text for each device.
- **Page references.** Rules popups say which book and page they come from.
- **One top row.** Every screen has the same slim bar with the way back, the log and ⋯, and it stays put as you scroll.

## M3 — rules libraries and setting rules (October 23, 2026)

- **Your rules library.** Everyone has a library of their own under **⋯ → Rules library**. Import a setting-rules pack (a JSON or Markdown file) and see what's in it before anything is saved: classes, races, items, spells, monsters, tables and more. Big packs with over a thousand entries load fine.
- **Updating a pack.** Import a newer file over an old one and Grimoire shows what will be added, changed and removed first. The campaign log notes the update, with a list of what changed.
- **About this file.** A short note in plain words on who made a pack, when, and whether it's open content or private.
- **Setting rules for a campaign.** When you start a campaign, pick which packs it uses and which optional rules are on; each optional rule has an ⓘ with a short summary. If a pack comes with a sheet layout, you can use it for your characters with one tick.
- **Homebrew.** The GM can copy any entry and change it, or make new ones, without touching the original pack. Changes are logged and can be undone. GM-only details stay hidden from players.
- **Convert a book.** "Convert a book with an AI…" gives you instructions to paste into an AI chat of your own, to turn your rulebook into a pack file. Grimoire itself never calls an AI.
- **Simpler deleting.** Deleting a character or a pack asks a plain "Are you sure?" instead of making you type.
- **Stays up to date.** The installed app picks up new versions by itself when you come back to it, without interrupting what you're doing.

## M2 — the log (October 21, 2026)

- **Everything is written down.** Every change in your campaign is recorded in a log: who did what, and when, in plain words ("Sam set Wren's HP to 9 (was 11)."). Players read it on the **Events** tab; the GM has a **Log** section. New lines appear on everyone's screen within seconds.
- **Simple or verbose.** The log shows what matters by default; tick **Verbose** to see every small change, like each HP tick.
- **Undo.** Tap a line you made and press **Undo** to put things back. The original line stays, marked "undone", so nothing is ever erased. The GM can undo anyone's change.
- **Notes.** Write a note for the group, optionally about a character, or tap any line to add a note to it. You can edit your own notes; the old wording stays in the log.
- **GM rulings.** When the GM changes a player's character, it's marked as a ruling with a seal, and the GM can give a reason.
- **Private stays private.** You only ever see log lines, notes and updates about things you're allowed to see.

## M1 — accounts and campaigns (October 20, 2026)

- **Sign in with a code.** Type your email, get a 6-digit code, and you're in. The first time, Grimoire asks what to call you.
- **Campaigns.** Start a campaign as GM, or join one from an invite link or code. Your campaigns are listed with your role and the character you're playing. The GM can rename the campaign and give it a sigil.
- **Make your character.** Name, starting level, hit points, backstory, a portrait, and any details your GM asks for. Your GM approves it, or sends it back with a note so you can change it and send it again.
- **Several characters.** Make more than one and choose which you're playing. Remove one you no longer use (download a copy first if you like); your GM can bring it back.
- **The Player screen.** Your character's name and hit points, with big − and + buttons, and your full character sheet a tap away. On a tablet held sideways, the sheet sits right beside it.
- **Your party.** See every approved character in the campaign, who plays them, and which company they're in. Your GM sets up companies.
- **For the GM.** Invite players, approve characters, set up the details every character records, organise companies, and open any character's sheet.
- **Works on tablets and phones,** in light or dark, and can be installed on the home screen.
