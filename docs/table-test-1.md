# Table test 1: the whole group (updated and ruled 2026-10-25)

First real use of the app by the group. Runs after the M8b gate and Pre-test hardening. The session runs the opening of the chosen published adventure (`valley-town-adventure`) on the `classic-advanced` rules, from the private packs. Updated for the rulings of 2026-10-24/25 (S16, S17, M5, sheet, M4b gate, UI conventions, journeys, offline). Changes from the 2026-10-23 version are marked **[new]** or **[changed]**.

## Ready to run when
1. M8b gate passed and Pre-test hardening done.
2. **[changed]** Packs ready: `classic-advanced` (current version) and `valley-town-adventure` pass the app's checker and the converter's adventure check; all 22 classes produce a legal 1st-level character; fill and warn hooks run; the copying check finds no copied notes.
3. **[new]** Automated checks green: component gallery screenshot tests, contrast and target tests, and all 10 journey tests within budget.
4. Backup taken, then the wipe script removes everything, accounts included.
5. GM signs up fresh, imports both packs, creates the campaign (**[new]** sets "Rules for this campaign": start level, monster AC learned in play, show each other's HP), sends invites, and preps the adventure's opening locations in prep mode.
6. One run-through on a phone (one player may use a phone; everyone else uses tablets). **[new]** At least one player uses the Large text size.
7. Paper sheets and rulebooks on the table as backup.
8. Picture approvals are back on the 2026-11-05 limits since pre-test hardening began (`docs/testing.md`, The component gallery).

## Before the session (at home, a few days ahead, about 5 minutes each)
Open the invite, sign in with the emailed code, add the app to the home screen. Success: everyone is in without calling the GM.

## At the table, part 1: setup (45 minutes maximum)
- Each player creates a **new** character with guided creation (**[new]** list-and-detail picking, the review screen, the kit card); the GM approves, and **[new]** sends one volunteer's character back with a note to test the sent-back flow.
- The group forms a company, writes the charter and share rules, ratifies it by vote, and picks a Treasurer **and an Archivist in the same vote [new]**.
- If it passes 45 minutes, finish on paper. Running over is a finding.

## At the table, part 2a: scripted first room (15 minutes) **[new]**
The party's first room runs as a short, announced script covering checks 8–12 below (exploration turns, AC on a hit, Give a stack, the offline test on a volunteer's or the GM's spare tablet, End expedition with the Archivist recap). Then free play begins.

## At the table, part 2b: free play (rest of the session)
Play normally with quiet mode on. Checks 1–7 are tallied only if they happen naturally. The app handles sheets, HP, inventory, time and light, exploration, combat, XP, and Split Loot at the end. No player checklist. The GM keeps a tally: times someone needed help, times paper beat the app, moments heads stayed down over screens.

Specific checks (1–7 during free play if they come up; 8–12 in the scripted first room):
1. **[changed]** Equip something the class can't use → blocked, with the reason one tap away.
2. Damage with a quick chip, then Undo from the toast → both in the log. **[new]**
3. **[changed]** Light running low → its ring turns the warning colour with one info line; at burnout, "Light another?".
4. Cast a memorised spell → it greys out; a spell with a duration starts a timed effect on the target. **[changed]**
5. On one player's tablet, look for another player's purse, a monster's HP, and an unidentified item's true name → none visible.
6. **[changed]** GM sends a picture or text to one chosen character → only they get it.
7. Split Loot → shares follow the charter.
8. **[new]** Exploration: at least 6 dungeon turns with the hour track, one wandering check with a modifier, one secret listen (**[changed]** 2026-10-30: the GM opens the GM screen's listening rule and rolls), and a marching-order change by hold-to-drag.
9. **[new]** A hit on a monster reveals its AC to the party; it stays known.
10. **[new]** Give a stack: how-many, then Undo from the toast.
11. **[new]** Offline: the GM switches a volunteer's (or the spare) tablet to airplane mode for one minute while they change their HP and give an item; back online, the changes sync or are explained, nothing lost.
12. **[new]** End the expedition: summary, full-rest offer, and the Archivist edits and publishes the recap draft.

## Wrap-up (10 minutes, said out loud, GM takes notes)
1. From 1 to 5, did it get in the way of play?
2. What confused you?
3. What would you miss if we went back to paper?
4. **[new]** Was any text too small, or anything hard to see in the room's light?
5. Keep this campaign, or start fresh? ◈ Decided after the test.

## Passes if
- Everyone created a character in 15 minutes or less, with at most one question to the GM each.
- No hidden data leaked and nothing was lost (including during the offline check). **[changed]**
- Average "got in the way" answer is 2 or lower.
- No switch to paper for more than 10 minutes.
- **[new]** No one needed help with a toast, Undo, or the offline pill.

## After
Every bug and comment gets sorted into "fix before R1" or "R2". Then one design session in the Project to adjust M9 and M10. **[new]** Wording and visual findings are checked against the words guide and UI conventions before any rule changes.
