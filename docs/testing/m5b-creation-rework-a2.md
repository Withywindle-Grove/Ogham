# Creation rework, slice A2: Scores, the pick steps, kept picks (cw-11, cw-13; mockups creation/12 and 13)

Any campaign with guided creation (the fixture "Seed · fae-woodland · defaults" works; with classic-advanced, Strength, Intelligence and so on). A new player: Create your character, then Guided.

1. **iPad, landscape, Scores.** Three tiles across, each with the full name (Muscle, Nimbleness…, or Strength, Intelligence…) with a round ⓘ, centred above − number +, the caption under it ("+1 melee", or "No change"). The step sits in one centred column, the same width as Class later. The title is large (as on Who they are). The app's top row shows no "‹ <campaign>"; Messages, the log and ⋯ are still there.
2. Tap a name (Muscle): a pop-up with what the ability is, then "At 9, nothing changes in play." (or "At 13: +1 melee."), then the rules note in small grey. ✕ closes it.
3. Type 13 into a box, then tap − and +: the number changes and the caption follows.
4. Empty one box: the foot's status line reads "▲ <name> is missing" in the warning colour, at the left; tap Next: it says so and stays.
5. **Kindred and Class.** Each name has an ⓘ; tapping the name opens its entry; Pick picks. On Class with a kindred that rules a class out: open "Not open to …" (the + square): the row shows "▲ Not open to <kindred>" and no Pick; tap it: the entry says "Not open to you" and why, with "Got it" and no Pick.
6. **Leave keeps the picks (cw-13).** With scores typed and a kindred picked, on Class tap **Leave**: a toast "Kept on this device. Pick it up again from Create." Tap Create your character: **A new character**, with "Continue where you left off · Scores · <kindred>" and "Start again", neither red. Tap Continue: you are on Class; Back shows the kindred picked and the scores as typed.
7. Pick a class and go on to Who they are, then Leave and Create again: no Continue row (the draft is in Your characters now).
8. Leave again before Who they are, then **Start again**: the usual Guided / Freeform choice, nothing kept.
9. **Another player on the same iPad:** after 6, sign out (the kept picks go), sign in as another player of the campaign, Create: no Continue row.
10. **Phone, upright (375 wide).** Scores: one row per ability, the name and the stepper on one line, the caption under. A long name wraps beside its ⓘ. Scroll to the bottom: the status line sits left of Back and Next (or above them, left-aligned, when long); Next wholly on screen.
11. **New campaign (GM):** unchanged: its ‹ at the top left, the top row's "‹ <campaign>" link where it was.
