# Manual checks: dec13 C12 (the Full character sheet's foot)

1. Open a character's Player screen on a tablet held sideways. Under "Full character sheet" (right column, last) you should see short rows with a chevron: Weapon skills (if picked), Abilities, Traits, Notes, each with a line under the name.
2. Tap Notes: the Notes sheet opens. Tap Weapon skills: the pack's rule opens. Tap Traits: the old section's rows open in a pop-up.
3. On a phone (375 px), the last row reads "Full character sheet" with a line naming what it holds; tapping it opens the full sheet as before.
4. Give a character a weapon outside their class (a GM-approved exception) and open the Player screen on a tablet: the Weapon skills row's line says "(not your class's weapon, usable at −2 to hit)" after that weapon; tap the row and the pop-up says it again.

Pictures expected to change: the layout run's `player-column-*` screens (the foot).
