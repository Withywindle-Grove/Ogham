# m5b-gate-bugs-3: the side menu's long name; Rules tab indent and row actions

1. **iPad, landscape (1024 or 1180 wide), as the GM.** Open a campaign, tap the name at the top of the side menu, rename it to something about 60 characters long ("The Lanterns of the Seven Drowned Marches and Their Kin"), Save. Expect: the name takes two lines at most and ends in "…"; Scene, Players and Rules sit right below it. Tap the name again: the rename form opens with the full name.
2. **Same screen, a short name.** Expect it on one line, no "…".
3. **iPad, Rules tab, as the GM.** Open a campaign and tap Rules. Open "Optional rules · <pack>" and one other group (e.g. Character fields). Expect: the arrow and title of every group start at the same left edge.
4. **Same tab, a pack's row under "Setting rules".** Expect "What's in it", "Optional rules", "Update from a new file", "Sheet layout…" and "Remove…" in the same text size (small, body size), each still an easy tap.
5. **iPhone (375 px).** Repeat 3 and 4: nothing wraps badly, nothing sideways, the campaign's own heading shows the whole name.
