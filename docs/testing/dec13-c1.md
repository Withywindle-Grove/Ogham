# Manual checks: dec13-c1, the Player screen's two columns

Branch `dec13-c1-two-columns`, into the held branch `dec-13-player-stack` (plan `docs/plans/dec-13-the-player-screen.md`, row C1; frames P1 and P2, approved ps-9 on 2026-11-14).

1. **On the iPad held sideways** (or a window 1024 px wide or more), open your campaign and your character. **The left column**, top to bottom: the portrait and your character's line, the statuses, hit points, armour class and attack, the lit light (if any), At hand, then **Level** with the experience bar, then **Details and backstory**. **The right column**: Scores, Saves, Movement (if your setting has speeds or a load), Traits with their Use buttons, Skills, Languages, then "Full character sheet" with Edit and the rest of the sheet under it.
2. **Nothing shows twice**: one Saves, one Skills, one Scores on the screen.
3. **No party list and no marching order** on the Player screen. (They are on Scene.)
4. Tap a score, a save, a skill, Level, a language: each opens its sheet as before; close it with ✕.
5. Tap **Edit** at the top of "Full character sheet": the editor opens in the right column; save or cancel brings the sheet back.
6. Tap **+** on hit points: the hit points change; the right column still reads right.
7. **On a phone** (or a window 375 px wide): one column. **Saves sit directly under hit points and armour class**, then At hand, Level, Details, then Scores, Movement, Traits, Skills, Languages, and the row "Full character sheet" last, which opens the full sheet page.
8. **The iPad upright** (834 px): the phone's order, in one column.
9. Switch to **Magic** (a caster) on the iPad sideways: the full sheet still sits beside it, as before.
10. Switch to **Pack** and **Scene** and back to Player: the columns come back in the same order.
