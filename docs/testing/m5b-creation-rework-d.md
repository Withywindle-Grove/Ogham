# Creation rework, slice D: the review and the GM's approval card (cw-11; plan m5b-creation-rework)

Any campaign with guided creation and kits (the fixture "Seed · fae-woodland · defaults" works). A new player: Create your character, Guided, through to the review; on Gear take a kit.

1. **iPad, landscape.** On the review, each step is a card: its title, and a small **Change** button (no "›") at its top right. Scores are on one line.
2. The **Gear** card reads as lines: the kit's name with what it gave under it (a thing your class can't wear says "▲ Ring coat: a Reed Witch can't wear it; carried" in bold); a pick that gave things (a keepsake or background) under its list's name; **Given** with "N items" and a **+** square (tap: the given things unfold, the square shows **−**); then "N items · <load>"; then **Starting coin** with e.g. "100 gp rolled · 22 gp spent · 78 gp left". A background that adds coin shows "+10 gp from <its name>".
3. A spell-casting class: the spells card lists each spell with "From your class" (or "From your kindred") under it.
4. **What the rules work out** is one folded row with "N figures · tap one to see why"; tap it, then tap a figure: its breakdown opens.
5. Tap **Change** on Gear, then **Back** without changing anything: you are on the review. Do the same for Languages, a trait list, Who they are and Scores: each Back lands on the review.
6. The **Kindred · Class** card has two lines, "Kindred: …" and "Class: …", each with its own small **Change**. Tap **Change** on Class, then **Back**: the review. Tap **Change** on Kindred, then **Back**: the review. Nothing changed. (A kindred-class, e.g. a setting where the class is the kindred: one line, one Change.)
7. Tap **Change** on Kindred and pick a different kindred: you walk on to Class (it depends on the kindred), then Next through whatever it touches, and back to the review. Mid-way, Back walks back as usual (a change was made).
8. With a step left with **Decide later**: the To finish box's line ends in **Do it now** with no "›".
9. On the review with something unfinished (e.g. take a language off on another device, reopen the draft): the foot reads "▲ Languages: Pick 1 more." in bold; Send to the GM still taps and lands on Languages.
10. Turn on airplane mode and tap **Send to the GM**: the foot reads "▲ You're offline. Nothing is saved until you're back: keep this screen open, then try again." The character stays a draft. Turn it off and tap Send again: it goes.
11. **Phone, upright (375 wide).** Repeat 1–2: the cards fit, Change never pushes the title off, long kit contents wrap.
12. **GM, Players tab.** The new character's approval card has a **Starting coin** section reading the same words as the player's review ("100 gp rolled · … left"; "+10 gp from <pick>" only when a pick added coin).
13. An older character (made before slice C's coin flag) still reads "rolled"; once slice C is in, a coin left at the usual reads "100 gp, the usual (not rolled)".
