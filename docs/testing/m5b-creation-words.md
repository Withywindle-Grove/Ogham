# m5b-creation-words: creation's help texts, not-allowed alignments, class kits only, folded derived fields, level 1 default (cw-3, cw-4, cw-7, cw-8, cw-9)

Open the preview (test project). Use the creation fixture campaign, or any campaign with a pack that has a class limited to some alignments (the fixture's Marsh Chaplain: Lantern or Reed).

1. **iPad, landscape. Help me choose.** Start a character, tap Help me choose on Scores, Class and Who they are. Each shows one plain paragraph, no bold lead-ins, no "What to weigh", ending with "or ask your GM" (Details ends "skip it"). A step where the pack has its own help still shows the pack's text.
2. **iPad. Gear help.** On the Gear step, Help me choose: with a kit on offer it explains kit and shop; with no kit it talks only about buying and never says "kit"; where gear is only given it says so.
3. **iPad. A closed alignment.** Pick Marsh Chaplain, go to Who they are. The Leaning list shows Lantern and Reed with Pick, and Mire as a row at full contrast with "▲ Not open to marsh chaplain" and no Pick button. Tap Mire: a pop-up says "A marsh chaplain can't be Mire." with Got it. Nothing is greyed or hidden.
4. **iPad. Back and change class.** Pick Mire with a class that allows it, go Back, switch to Marsh Chaplain, Next: Who they are says the class can't be Mire and asks for another (as before).
5. **iPad. Derived fields.** On Who they are, "Derived fields · N ›" is folded even in landscape. Open it, leave and come back to Who they are (or reload): it is still open. Fold it, reload: folded. The Review step still shows the derived fields in full.
6. **Phone (375 px). The same.** Checks 3 and 5 on a phone: the closed row's text wraps, nothing is cut off, the ▲ line is readable; the folded group is one line.
7. **Gear step, kits.** With a class that has a kit, only that class's kits are listed (never another class's). App settings and Rules for this campaign (Characters) no longer have "Any class can take any kit".
8. **New campaign.** Create a campaign; under App, "New characters start at" is Level 1; "Player proposes, GM approves" is still one tap away and works.

Gallery pictures expected to change: see the status file, item 6.
