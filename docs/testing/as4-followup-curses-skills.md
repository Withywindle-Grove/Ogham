# Manual checks: curses, a foe's bonus on ammunition, and the weapon penalty in more places (as4-followup-curses-skills)

Ruling `design/rulings/m5b-evening-rulings-2026-11-10.md` (as-5 … as-8, id-1); plan `docs/plans/m5b-attack-sheet-as4-followup.md`. Use the pull request's Vercel preview (the test project), the same kind of campaign as as-4's checks (`docs/testing/m5b-attack-sheet-as4.md`): weapon proficiency on, its specialisation and restricted-weapons options on, a character whose class names a proficiency category.

1. **A cursed arrow from a magic bow (as-5).** In a setting whose rules set has **no** `attack.ammunitionBonus` rule, give the character a bow with `weapon.magicBonus: 2` and a stack of arrows with `weapon.magicBonus: -1`, pick those arrows in the Quiver and tap **Attack** in At hand. As the **GM**: both cards have the bow's row "+2" and a row "<arrows>, magic (hidden from the player) −1", both counted in the line; there is **no** "Not added" note. As the **player**: the bow's row only, and the line one higher than the GM's.
2. **A foe's bonus on arrows from a magic bow (as-6).** Same bow, arrows with `magicBonus: 1` and a `bonusVs` entry (for example `{ "against": "bog lurkers", "bonus": 3 }`). As the **player**: under the to-hit card a quiet note "<arrows>, +3 vs bog lurkers", not counted in the line, and no arrows row. As the **GM**: the same note, beside "<arrows>, magic +1. Not added: the rules don’t say."
3. **Specialised outside the class (as-7).** In creation, open the folded group "Not your class's weapons", tap a weapon twice (Specialised ✓✓), finish and send. As the GM, allow it and approve. Put that weapon in the character's hand and tap **Attack**: the to-hit card has two rows, "Specialised" (the bonus) and "Not your class’s weapon" (the penalty), and its line nets them (for +1 and −2, one lower than without the specialisation's row). The damage card has "Specialised" alone. At hand's figure carries the same totals.
4. **The sheet's Weapon skills line (as-8).** Open that character's **Full character sheet**. The Weapon skills line reads, for example, "Weapon skills: Reed spear, Bog sling, specialised (not your class’s weapon, usable at −2 to hit)". A class weapon in the list has nothing in brackets.
5. **The GM's exception card (as-8).** With a character waiting whose weapon skills include one outside the class, open **Players** as the GM. The card "Needs your special approval" shows the pack's reason and, under it, "If you allow it: usable at −2 to hit." A language or class exception has no such line. If the player specialised in that weapon (step 3), the card's first line reads "Bog sling, to specialise in: <the pack's reason>".
5b. **A cursed bow drawing magic arrows (built, not ruled).** Setting with no ammunition rule; a bow with `magicBonus: -1`, arrows with `magicBonus: 1`, picked in the Quiver. As the **GM**: both cards have the bow's row "(hidden from the player) −1" and the arrows' row "+1", both counted, and no "Not added" note. As the **player**: the arrows' row only.
6. **Unidentified stays hidden (id-1, no change).** A magic bow in a category the rules set marks unidentified: the player's Attack sheet reads as if it were plain; the GM sees its row marked "(hidden from the player)". This stays so until M6.
7. At 375 px wide, check the GM's card (step 5) and the sheet line (step 4): the text wraps, nothing overflows. Check light and dark mode.
