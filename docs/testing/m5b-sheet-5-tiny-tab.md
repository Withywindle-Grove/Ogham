# M5b — the Pack tab's folded Tiny things section (SC-5-2, pl-1 words; feeds gate check 64, with the sheet's Pack load)

On a campaign with the invented pack `scripts/fixtures/m5b-creation.fixture.json` imported and attached (its **Trinkets** category is tiny; its Eel hooks are marked tiny themselves). Carrying set to slots. Open a character's **Pack** tab. iPad first, then a phone at 375 px, light and dark.

1. [ ] **iPad landscape, no tiny things:** a character with ordinary things only: the Pack looks as before, with no Tiny things line.
2. [ ] **iPad, folded:** add a Reed whistle and Eel hooks (Add item). Below Carried, above Add item, a line reads "▸ Tiny things · 2 · don't count". The whistle and the hooks are no longer in Carried, and the load bar's counts don't change when you add them.
3. [ ] **iPad, open:** tap the line: ▸ turns ▾ and Eel hooks and Reed whistle show as ordinary rows. Tap Reed whistle: its actions open (Move to…, Give…) as on any row. Tap the line again: it folds.
4. [ ] **iPad, remembered:** open the section, go to Player and back to Pack (or reload): it is still open. Fold it, reload: still folded.
5. [ ] **iPad, in a bag:** move the Reed whistle into a bag (Move to…): it stays under Tiny things, not under "In the <bag>".
6. [ ] **iPad, coin mode:** set Carrying to coins: if a tiny thing has a weight, the line reads "Tiny things · 2" without "don't count" (pl-2: its weight counts).
7. [ ] **iPad, the Party stash:** open the Party stash below the pack and give it a Reed whistle: the stash shows it in its plain list, with no Tiny things section.
8. [ ] **Phone 375 px, light and dark:** the Tiny things line and its open rows fit with no sideways scroll; a long item name wraps inside its row; the line is a 44 px tap.
