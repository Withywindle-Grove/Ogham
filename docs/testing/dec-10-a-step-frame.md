# Manual checks: dec-10-a-step-frame

1. On the preview, start a new character. Each step's line under the title is one sentence:
   - How to build: "Pick the way your table builds a character."
   - Kindred: "The people your character was born to."
   - Class: "Your trade as an adventurer."
   - First spells: "The spells your character starts with."
   - Languages: "The tongues your character speaks.", then, when the character's score gives extra tongues, "Wits 13 gives you one more." (or "… 3 more."), in your setting's word for the score; nothing more when it gives none
   - Weapon skills: "The weapons your character has trained with."
   - Gear: "What you carry into your first adventure."
   - Details: "None of these change the rules."
   - Who they are: "A name the table will remember."
   (A pack that gives its own line keeps it.)
2. On Scores, an untouched score shows "Average" under its box, not a sentence.
3. On Adjust scores, the lead names the prime without "A higher prime earns experience faster."
4. On Class, each row:
   - the ⓘ, the name and Pick on the first line;
   - the blurb and the fit line under them, across the row's full width, the fit line reading like "Might 15, ⬡d8, +5% experience, to level 12 as Stone kin";
   - the source line still there.
5. Tap the blurb or fit line of a row: its entry opens, as tapping the name does. Tap Pick: it picks and opens nothing.
6. The folds ("1 more needs the GM's approval", "Not open to …") end in a chevron pointing down; open, it points up. No + or − square in creation.
7. Next with nothing picked: a toast says what's missing, as before.
8. On Weapon skills, every weapon row starts with an ⓘ.
9. Outside creation, on the play screen's At hand sheet, a row looks as before, and the Pack tab's folds keep their + / − square.
10. On a trait list you can roll more than once (a background, say), pick the same long-named entry twice: its button reads "Picked ✓✓" on a line of its own at the right, under the name, and the name keeps its width.
11. On the full sheet, open a step from To finish (Languages, say): it is in the same serif type as creation, its folds with the chevron.
12. Phone and tablet, light and dark: steps 4, 6 and 10 again.
