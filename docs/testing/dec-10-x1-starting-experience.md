# Manual checks: dec-10 X1, experience at a higher starting level (cs-13)

On the pull request's Vercel preview (test project), in a campaign whose setting starts characters at a fixed level of 2, with a pack whose class names an XP table.

1. As a player, Create your character → Guided; pick a kindred and the class; on Who they are type a name and tap Next.
2. Carry on to the review and send it; as the GM, approve it; open the character's sheet.
3. You should see the experience at the class's level-2 figure from its own pack's table (for example "Level 2, 2,400 XP"), not 0.
4. Repeat in a campaign that starts at level 1: the sheet says 0 XP, as before.
5. A character made at level 2 before this change still says 0 XP (nothing is changed after the fact).
