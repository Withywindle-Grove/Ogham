# Creation rework, slice A1: the shared shell parts (cw-11; plan m5b-creation-rework)

Any campaign with guided creation (the fixture "Seed · fae-woodland · defaults" works). A new player: Create your character, then Guided.

1. **iPad, landscape.** Open the Scores step. At the top: a **Leave** button on the left, "Step 1" on the right, the title "Scores" under them. No ‹ anywhere. **Help me choose** is a small button with a round ⓘ; tap it: the help opens under it. The foot shows the status line and **Next** only (no Back on the first step).
2. Tap **Next**, then on Kindred look at the foot: **Back** and **Next** side by side. Tap **Back**: you are on Scores again with your numbers kept.
3. Go on to Class. If a "Not open to …" group shows, its row ends in a square with **+**; tap the row: the classes unfold and the square shows **−**. No "›" after the group's name.
4. Tap **Leave**: you leave creation and land where you started it (the campaign). Nothing was saved before Who they are (as the top row's back did before).
5. **Phone, upright (375 wide).** Repeat 1–2. Scroll to the very bottom of each step: the status line, Back and Next sit wholly on the screen, Next not cut by the bottom edge or the home bar.
6. **Phone on its side.** Same as 5 on Class: Next wholly on the screen at the end.
7. Make a character to the review, then tap **Change ›** on Scores (or on the kindred / class). On that first step the foot shows **Back**; tap it: you are back on the review, nothing changed.
8. **New campaign** (GM): Create campaign. Its steps still have **‹** at the top left (unchanged on purpose: the "‹ ›" removal outside creation waits for its own mockup); Help me choose there is the same small button with ⓘ.
9. Languages, Weapon skills or a trait list step with **Other…**: the row ends in a **+** square; tap it: the typing box opens and the square shows **−**.
