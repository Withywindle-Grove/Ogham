# Manual checks: fix-adjust-scores-skipped

On the pull request's Vercel preview (test project), in a campaign whose rules have the score trade (lower a score to raise the prime).

1. As a player, Create your character → Guided. On Scores type 10 for every score, so no trade is possible.
2. Pick a kindred, then a fighting class: Next goes straight to "Who they are" (no Adjust scores), as before.
3. Tap Back on "Who they are": you land on Class (or on the Patron step, for a class that has one), **not** on Adjust scores.
4. Start again with a high score to lower (say 14 in a score that isn't the class's prime): after the class you reach Adjust scores, and Back from "Who they are" lands on Adjust scores, as before.
