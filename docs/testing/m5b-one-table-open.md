# m5b-one-table-open: a step that is one list starts open (2026-11-09)

1. On a phone or iPad, open the pull request's preview, a campaign whose setting has a backgrounds list (or any one-list step, such as a sign), and start Create your character, Guided.
2. Go through to the backgrounds step. Its rows show at once, under the "N to choose from" row, with "Nothing picked yet" there. No tap is needed to see them.
3. Tap the "N to choose from" row: the list folds shut, and the square shows +. Tap it again: it opens.
4. Pick a background, go Back, then Next to the step again: the list is open with the pick showing.
5. Go back to Who they are: a short table there (a liege, for example) still starts folded.
6. On Gear, a pick-up-to-N table still starts folded.
