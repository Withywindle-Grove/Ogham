# m5b-creation-final-a: creation's final drafts, slice A (the shell and the pieces)

Open the pull request's preview (test project), once it exists. Use the creation fixture campaign, or a campaign with fae-woodland attached. iPad upright (820) first, then the phone (375). Each check says what to tap and what you should see. The mockups to compare with are `docs/design/mockups/creation/final/01`, `02` and `08` on `design-gate-m5b-walk`.

1. **Phone. The app bar.** Create, Guided.
   - At the top: ← at the left, the step's title in the middle, ⋯ at the right. "Step 1 of …" sits under the bar.
   - The foot of step 1 reads **Leave** and **Next**. Tap Leave: you're back where you started.
   - The app's own row (the campaign, Messages, the log) isn't there on a phone while you create: only creation's bar (final draft 9). On the iPad it stays.
   - Tap ⋯ before picking anything: a sheet comes up with the app's menu (Your characters, Setting rules, then This device: theme and text size, then Rules library and Sign out), a line, and **Leave** last. Change the text size: the sheet stays open and the step behind it changes.
   - Pick something, tap ⋯ again: the last row now reads **Save and leave** ("Your picks are kept on this device"), and there's no plain Leave.
   - Tap **Setting rules** in that sheet: the setting rules open, and a toast says the picks are kept. Go back and tap Create: "Continue where you left off" brings them back.
   - Go to step 2. Tap ← : you're on the step before.
   - Nowhere in creation is there a "‹" or "›".
2. **Phone. The accent follows the ask.** On How to build (or Kindred), the first row's **Pick** is the dark accent button and Next is plain. Pick one: Next becomes the accent. On Scores, Next is the accent.
3. **Phone. Help me choose.** It's a full-width button. Tap it: a box opens across the width, with ✕ at its top right and no "Close" button. Tap ✕: the button comes back.
4. **Phone. Scores.** The title is just "Scores". Under the line of what the step is, the die (a cube) and in bold "Roll 3d6 at the table for each score, then enter what you rolled." (your setting's dice).
5. **Phone. Sheets.** On Class, tap a class's name: its entry slides up from the bottom over the darkened page. ✕ closes it, and so does a tap on the darkened part. Do the same with a table on Who they are and a kit's rolls on Gear.
6. **iPad (820). Sheets beside their button.** On Who they are, tap Open on a name table: the table opens next to the button you tapped (under it, or above it near the bottom), not in the middle of the screen.
7. **iPad (820). Two columns only where needed.**
   - Class: tap a class's name. Its entry shows in a column to the right of the list, not as a sheet. The entry's **Pick <class>** is the accent; the rows' Picks are plain.
   - Scores: one wide column, three tiles across, no empty column at the side.
   - The review: the cards sit two across.
8. **Any. The warning sign.** A class you can't take on Class shows a small triangle with "!" and "Not open to …" in bold. Offline, the foot's line shows the same sign. No plain "▲" anywhere in creation.
9. **Any. No middle dot.** Walk a character from start to the review and send it. Read every line on the way, the toasts and the review: no " · " anywhere (counts read "6 items: rope, lantern", money "100 gp rolled, 22 gp spent, 78 gp left"). Sign in as the GM: the approval card has none either.
10. **Phone (375). Messy data.** A 40-character name on a kit or a class: the bar's title cuts with "…" in the middle column, the ← and ⋯ stay 44 px. Starting coin of 0 and 999: the box holds them.
