# Manual checks: fix-end-expedition-undo-lines

Logic only, plus one line that no longer shows (T11, ap-11). No gallery pictures are expected to change.

1. As the GM, on Scene, tap **+ Reminder** and make a check every 2 turns. Tap **Start expedition**, then **Next turn** twice. The reminder's check comes due on the card ("Due now").
2. Open ⋯ › **End expedition**, then close the summary. The reminder is still on the card, and the card's heading still reads "Due now".
3. Tap the strip's **Undo**. You should see "Put the clock back", and the reminder should still be on the card. Before the fix it was taken off.
4. In a campaign with no reminders of your own, start an expedition and tap **Next turn** once. The card says "Next turn, nothing due".
5. Open ⋯ › **End expedition** and close the summary. The card should say nothing: no "Next turn, nothing due". Tap the strip's **Next turn** (in town); still nothing.
