# Manual checks: dec-10-c-kindred-class-patron

1. On Class, tap a class's ⓘ. After Disadvantages and before the source line, its level table: a dark bar "<Class> by level", then a row per level with its title (where the class has titles), experience and hit dice. Rows are banded.
2. A class whose titles stop (at level 9, say): the Title cells past the last are empty, not repeated.
3. A class with no titles: no Title column at all.
4. On Kindred, tap an open class's chip: the class you read shows the same table.
5. A very long title wraps in its cell, and the row grows taller. On a phone the table fits the sheet's width without scrolling sideways.
6. Phone and tablet, light and dark: steps 1 and 5 again.
