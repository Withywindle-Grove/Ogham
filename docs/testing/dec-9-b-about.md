# Manual checks: dec-9-b-about ("About this app", plan dec-9 piece B)

Pictures to compare against (approved 2026-11-12, branch `design-december-mockups`, `docs/design/mockups/december/png/`): `08-01-credits-tablet-dark` and `-light`, `08-02-credits-long-dark`, `08-03-credits-phone-dark` and `-light`, `08-04-credits-menu-item-dark`.

1. **On a tablet (or a browser window wider than 700 px), signed in, on Your campaigns:** tap ⋯ at the top right. You should see **About this app** just above **Sign out**, with a small "i" symbol beside it, and Rules library above it.
2. **Tap About this app.** The menu closes and a panel slides over the right of the screen, the page dimmed behind it: the title **About this app** with ✕, one quiet line "Tome, version …, built …" (on the preview: the commit's seven letters and today's date), then **Credits** in small capitals, the line "Icons are shown in the app’s colours.", and a row for each icon: Chest, Crystal ball, Hourglass, Splash, Sword, Trumpet, Two coins, Wizard’s hat, each with its small icon, "Lorc" or "Delapouite", "game-icons.net" and an underlined **CC BY 3.0**.
3. **Scroll the rows.** Under a dotted rule come the fonts: Bitter, Alegreya Sans SC, EB Garamond, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Caveat, each with its authors and an underlined **SIL Open Font License 1.1**. The title and ✕ stay in place while the rows move.
4. **Tap a CC BY 3.0 link.** The licence's page opens in a new tab; the app stays where it was.
5. **Tap ✕.** The panel closes. Open it again and press Escape (keyboard), then again and tap the dimmed page at the left: each closes it.
6. **Inside a campaign on a tablet:** open the campaign's ⋯ (or the rail's menu). About this app is there too, just above Sign out.
7. **On a phone (375 px wide), inside a campaign:** tap ≡, then the person at the foot ("App settings, sign out"). About this app sits just above Sign out; tap it. The sheet fills the whole screen; a long row wraps to two lines and the licence's link stays whole on its line.
8. **Light and dark:** switch the theme in ⋯ → This device and open the sheet in each. The icons draw in the text colour, the links in the accent colour, everything readable.
9. **Text size Larger:** set ⋯ → Text size → Larger and open the sheet. Nothing runs off the side; the rows just wrap more.
