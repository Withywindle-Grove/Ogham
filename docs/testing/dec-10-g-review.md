# Manual checks: dec-10-g-review

1. Make a character to the review. No "Look it over…" line at the top. "Derived values" reads "8 figures" (your count), with a chevron.
2. Gear's card: the trinket on a row of its own, "Trinket"; a pack's pick table on its own row named as the pack names it ("Odds and ends"); the coin reads "120 rolled, 14 silver spent: 106 silver left" (your numbers and coin).
3. Spend more than your coin, then come back to the review: the coin line ends "…: 2 silver over." and there is no separate bold "Over by" line.
4. The foot: "Goes to the GM for approval", then "Start over" and "Save and leave" (plain), then "Send to the GM" (dark red) at the right. On a phone, Send drops to its own row, still at the right.
5. Tap "Start over": "Start over?", with "Keep going" and "Start over" both plain, Start over at the right. Tap Keep going.
6. Tap Change on Languages, change nothing: the foot has one button, "Back to review", plain, and says "Nothing changed". The top arrow also goes back.
7. On Class, tap Next with nothing picked: a toast "Pick a class first." On a pick past the count (Weapon skills): "2 of 2 picked: take one off to pick another."
8. A Marsh Chaplain (or any class that follows a patron): tap Next on Patron with nothing picked: "A Marsh Chaplain follows a patron.", with "Back to Class" and "Choose a patron".
9. On Gear type 190 for a 3d6 × 10 coin: under the stepper "190 is above what 3d6 × 10 can roll. Roll again, or send it and the GM decides."; the foot doesn't repeat it.
10. In a campaign where characters join at once: the review's button reads "Add to the party".
11. As the GM, send a character back with a note: as the player, the note reads "Your GM asks for a change", and the button "Send to the GM".
12. As the GM: the approval card's "Approve" and "Send back" are both plain; its coin line uses the same words as the review.
13. A character with a step left for later: the sheet's To finish card says "You can play now." and lists the step.
14. Phone and tablet, light and dark: steps 1, 4 and 11 again.
