# Manual checks: m5b-ac-1-own-characters

After the migration is on TEST (Francis's turn, through the lead), on this pull request's Vercel preview. You need a GM and two players, each with a character in the same travel group, and an expedition with the day started on ground where a one-character activity and a group activity are offered.

1. As player A, on the Player screen's travel card, tap a one-character activity (in the test setting, one whose pop-up asks "Who acts?"). You see your own character and your hirelings only: player B's character isn't listed.
2. Tap a group activity (its pop-up asks "Who's acting?"). Everyone in your travel group is listed, player B's character too.
3. As the GM, open the same one-character activity. Everyone in the group is listed.
4. As player A, do the one-character activity with your own character: it applies, the toast says so, and Undo reverses it.
