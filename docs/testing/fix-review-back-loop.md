# Manual checks: fix-review-back-loop (cs-39a)

On the pull request's Vercel preview (test project).

1. As a player, Create your character → Guided, and walk to the review (put off what you can).
2. On the review tap ← Back: the last step opens (for example Gear), with the step counter.
3. Tap Back again: the step before it opens (for example Who they are), **not** the review. Keep tapping Back: each step walked opens in reverse, to the first.
4. From any of those, Next walks forward again and ends on the review.
5. On the review tap a card's Change (for example Change Gear), then Back: you are on the review again, as before.
