# Manual checks: fix-gear-record-first-2

Both need a way to lose the connection for one save: switch the device to airplane mode just before the tap, then back.

1. **A pick put back while offline.**
   - In a campaign whose setting gives a pick of a stacking thing (two of something that stacks, such as eels), make a character and on Gear pick that row twice: one line, 4.
   - Go offline and tap − on the row. The problem line says the save didn't go, the line still reads 4, and the row still says Picked ×2.
   - Back online, tap − again: the line reads 2, and the row says Picked ✓.
2. **A patron taken off while offline, then Gear.**
   - Make a chaplain with the Still Heron, walk to Gear: the Reed-grey stole is in the pack. On the Pack, raise the stole's count to 3.
   - On the review, Change patron, go offline, and take the patron off (Decide later). The step says the garb couldn't be swapped, and the stole still reads 3.
   - Back online, tap Change Gear: the stole reads 2, and stays at 2 however long the step stays open.
