# Creation rework, slice C: Gear and the kit's rolls (cw-11; plan m5b-creation-rework)

A campaign on the fae-woodland setting (a class with a starting kit that has rolls, e.g. the knight or the bard) and one on the classic setting (a money roll and the shop). A new player: Create your character, then Guided, through to Gear.

1. **iPad, landscape (fae-woodland).** On Gear: **Starting coin** comes first: − [number] + with the coin's name beside it, and the line "Starts at the usual N. Roll at the table and change it to what you rolled." Then **To choose**, then **Yours already** (folded, a + square). No "›" anywhere on the step.
2. Look at the class's kit row: its line is the kit's blurb, then "· 3 rolls" (the bard's also "· 1 to choose").
3. Tap **Pick** on the kit. A pop-up opens with the kit's name and ✕; under it "Step 1 of 3 · Armour · roll d6 at the table, tap what you rolled". Tap **Next** without tapping a row: the line at the foot says to roll and tap first, and the pop-up stays on step 1.
4. Tap a row: the foot shows what it gives. **Next**: "Step 2 of 3 · Weapons · 1 of 2 …". Tap a row, **Next**, "2 of 2". The last step's accent button reads **Done**. Tap **Back** once: the earlier tap is still ticked.
5. Tap **Done**. Back on Gear the kit reads **Picked ✓**, its line now lists what it gave, and under it **Change the rolls** and **Take it off**. The load line and the pack follow.
6. Tap **Change the rolls**: the pop-up opens at step 1 with your taps ticked. Change the armour, go through to **Done**: the kit's line shows the new armour. Tap **Change the rolls** again, then ✕: nothing changes.
7. Tap **Take it off**: the kit is back to **Pick**, and its things are gone from the pack (the given gear stays).
8. Tap **Pick** again, tap one row, then ✕: a toast says "<kit> not taken." and nothing is in the pack.
9. **Classic setting.** On Gear the coin starts at the usual (3d6 x 10 gp: 100). Tap **+** once: 110. Buy past what you have: the foot reads "▲ N gp over: take something off, or raise your coin". **Browse the shop** has no "›".
10. Go through Gear without touching the coin, on to the review: the coin is 100 (the review and the GM's card show "the usual (not rolled)" once slice D, #533, is in). With the coin stepped or typed, it is a roll: the GM's roll log shows it.
11. **Phone, upright (375 wide).** Repeat 3–5: the pop-up fills the screen, the rows and the foot (status, Back, Next) fit without sideways scrolling; Next is wholly on the screen at the end.
12. A kit thing the class can't use (e.g. armour a magic-user can't wear) reads "▲ <item>: a <class> can't wear it; carried" on the picked kit.
13. **Trinket (stone kin, or any kindred with a trinket table).** Before a pick, one row "Trinket" with **Roll d…**. Pick one: the row is now only the trinket's words with **Change** (no Roll row left above it). Tap **Change**: the table opens with your trinket marked; pick another: the row shows the new one, and the pack holds one trinket.
