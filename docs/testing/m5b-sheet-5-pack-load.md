# M5b — the sheet's Pack load (sheet plan build order 5: sr-9, with #433's bars and cb-1; feeds gate check 64)

On a campaign with the invented pack `scripts/fixtures/m5b-creation.fixture.json` imported and attached (a new import picks up its Trinkets: a Reed whistle, and Eel hooks). Its Carrying set to slots and, in Rules for this campaign, **Two-band slots › On** (Equipped up to 2 slots 120 ft, up to 4 60 ft; Stowed up to 4 120 ft, up to 6 90 ft, up to 8 30 ft). Open the character's **Full character sheet** (or the GM's **View sheet**). iPad first, then a phone at 375 px, light and dark.

1. [ ] **iPad landscape, two bands:** on the Pack tab give the character one thing in hand and five in the bags. The sheet shows a **Pack load** section right after Movement: "Speed 90" on top, then "Equipped 1 of 4 slots · speed 120" with its cells and "Stowed 5 of 8 slots · speed 90 · sets your speed" with its cells, the second line in bold. The Pack tab's load shows the same two bars with the same names.
2. [ ] **iPad, the top line:** tap "Speed 90" on the sheet: both bands' lists open, with "The slower of the two sets your speed."; tap again to close.
3. [ ] **iPad, tiny things:** add a Reed whistle and Eel hooks (Add item). Under the bars, one line per group: "Worn and in hand · …", "In the <bag> · …" (names when 3 or fewer, else "N items"), "Carried · …", and last "Tiny things · 2 · don't count". Tap it: ▸ turns ▾ and "Eel hooks" and "Reed whistle" are listed under it; tap again to close. The two tiny things aren't in the other groups, and the band counts don't change when you add them.
4. [ ] **iPad, empty:** a character with nothing carried: both bars at 0, no band named "sets your speed", and "Nothing carried."
5. [ ] **iPad, coin mode:** set Carrying to coins: the section is one bar with its tier marks, the tier and its speed on top ("Light · speed 120"), and "N of 1,200 coins" under it (a thousands comma); no band lines. The Pack tab's load reads the same.
6. [ ] **iPad, armour mode:** set Carrying to armour, give the character a gem, and as GM tap **Switch off** on their Pack tab: the sheet's section reads two left-aligned lines, "Not carrying treasure · the GM's call", then "Treasure N of M coins' weight · the rules suggest Carrying treasure", with no switch button; its Movement speed is the without-treasure speed, the same as the Pack tab and travel. Tap **Switch on** on the Pack tab: "Carrying treasure · the GM's call" and the second line loses its suggestion. The Pack tab reads the same two lines, with its Switch button on the first.
7. [ ] **iPad landscape, the Player screen:** the sheet beside the play column shows the same Pack load section.
8. [ ] **Phone 375 px:** the section fits with no sideways scroll; band lines wrap ("sets your speed" on its own line is fine); every group line is a 44 px tap; a long item name wraps inside the opened list.
