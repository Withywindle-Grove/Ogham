# M5b — the sheet's Traits and Advancement (sheet plan build order 2: sr-3 as amended by tc-1 … tc-4, sr-5; PR #446; feeds gate check 64)

On the seeded campaigns (`npm run seed:test`), private packs only; or a campaign on the invented `scripts/fixtures/m5b-creation.fixture.json` (a bog folk Lantern Warden: traits Second wind, Small; Steady flame with 1 use a day, Oath of light a drawback). Open each character with **View sheet**.

1. [ ] **iPad landscape, fae-woodland, any kindred and class:** the **Traits** section shows chips, several to a row, under small headings "<Kindred> · <name>" and "<Class> · <name>" (and "From options" when an option adds one). A trait with uses shows a dot per use after its name; a drawback reads "· drawback"; a trait from a later level reads "· lv N" in a quieter colour. Tap any chip: its entry opens (what it does, From, When, Kind); **Got it** closes it.
2. [ ] **iPad landscape, the Player screen:** the sheet beside the play column shows the same Traits chips (one component), not one trait per line. (The play column's own **Abilities** list, with tappable use dots, is the uses tracker and is unchanged.)
3. [ ] **iPad landscape, spent uses:** on the Player screen with the sheet beside, tap Steady flame's filled dot in the play column's Abilities: on the sheet's chip the dot turns to a ring ("Steady flame ○"); tap the ring to give the use back and the chip fills again. In the gallery's Molecules, "Ward the light ●○○" (1 of 3 left). With VoiceOver on, that chip reads "Ward the light, 3 uses each rest, 1 of 3 left: open its entry".
4. [ ] **iPad landscape, Advancement:** an **Advancement** section reads "Level 1 · 0 XP", a bar (empty at 0), and "<N> to level 2" under it; **XP bonus** "+N%" with "prime <SCORE> <n>" as a quiet caption on its own line under it (e.g. "+5%", then "prime STR 13"), no "·" starting a line; **Title** the level title. classic-advanced's lone "XP modifier" box is gone, and the Title is no longer under Identity.
5. [ ] **A character with many traits (a kindred with 13 or more):** the group shows 12 chips and "+ N more"; tap it: all of them.
6. [ ] **Phone 375 px:** the chips wrap about two to a row, 8 px apart, each 44 px tall; a trait with a very long name wraps inside its chip, alone on its row; no sideways scroll. The gallery's Molecules has the specimen (13 traits, "Knows the hidden paths between the drowned willows of the far fen at dusk"). Advancement: "prime MUS 16" sits whole under the bonus, in the caption colour.
