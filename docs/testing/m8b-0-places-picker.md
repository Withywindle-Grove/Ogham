# m8b-0-places-picker — the place picker (M8b slice 0, pull request 2; ruled 2026-11-06 ⚑ 1, 3, 7, 8, 10; mockup `design/mockups/places/01-place-picker.html`)

Needs pull request 1's and this pull request's migrations on the project the preview uses, and a campaign with a region or adventure pack attached (the seeded fae-woodland campaign after a re-seed, or any campaign with the fixture pack's places). Invented names below; use the real pack's on the preview.

1. **iPad, Start expedition.** Scene, no expedition under way → tap **Where? (optional)** (the pin button). **See:** a pop-up "Where are we?" with a search field, Recently visited (if any), From your packs with one folded group per pack ("Region pack · N places"), and "Other… a place that isn't listed". Tap the pack's **+** → its region; **+** on the region → its hexes by map number ("Heron Isle (Hex) · 0412"); **+** on a hex → its sites. Tap a site's name. **See:** the pop-up closes and Where? shows the place's words. Tap **Start expedition**. **See:** one toast "Started an expedition: … · <place>" with Undo; the place card shows the name with its type, the pack and terrain line, "0 of N pieces shown", **Players see: <name>** with Rename for players, **Part of …** and **Here · N places**; the log has ONE line for the start.
2. **iPad, Undo the start.** Tap Undo on that toast. **See:** the expedition, the place and its pieces all go (the card is back to "Where are we?"); the picker's Recently visited no longer lists it.
3. **iPad, search.** Start again → Where? → type a name two places share. **See:** "N places", each row "Name (Type), in <parent> · <pack>"; type a map number ("0412") → that hex; type nonsense → "No place called … in your packs." with "Other… use … as a new place".
4. **iPad, Change place and the related places.** On the card tap **Part of …**. **See:** the party moves there at once, toast with Undo, no travel points spent; the card now lists the old place under **Here · N places** (tap **+** to unfold, tap a name to go there). Tap **Change place** (pin). **See:** the picker titled "Where now?" with **Near here** first (the current place's own places, then its neighbours) and the current place marked "✓ here now" in the tree.
5. **iPad, Rename for players.** Tap **Rename for players**, type a name, Save. Show the description piece (eye). **Phone, as a player:** the Scene shows that name and the piece, never the pack's name or anything of the GM's.
6. **iPad, Move….** In a wilderness day: Move… → **Place (optional)** (pin) → the picker as a step inside the sheet (Back to the move) → pick a hex. **See:** the terrain chip switches to the hex's terrain; Move → one toast with Undo; the party is at that hex; Undo takes the move and the place back together.
7. **iPad, the town.** Pick a place the pack marks a settlement (a town or village). **See:** the town screen's sections on the card (no Safe or hostile needed); its overview says the size ("Town · 400 people"); show a lodging and the description piece → **a player's phone** shows the town with that lodging.
8. **iPad, the region.** At a hex whose encounter check names a regional table, run the encounter check to a hit. **See:** Which table? offers the region's own table first.
9. **Phone (375 px).** Where? → the picker is the whole screen with ✕ at the top right; rows are 44 px and long names wrap beside the fold button; Other… opens the typed-name step with Set place and Back to the list; Escape or ✕ closes.
10. **Empty and broken states.** A campaign with no pack listing places: the picker says "None of this campaign's packs lists places…" and offers Other…. Offline with the list never loaded: the offline note shows; Recently visited and Other… still work.
