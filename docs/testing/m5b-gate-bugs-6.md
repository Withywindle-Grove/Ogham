# m5b-gate-bugs-6 — creation gate fixes: the given kit twice, a full stop inside the gear list, the method step's foot

From Francis's M5b gate walk (2026-11-06). Feeds the creation checks of the gate run (Gear, the review, the method step). Use a fresh character each time: a draft from before this fix may already hold two sets.

1. **iPad, a fae-woodland campaign with "Buy starting equipment" on** (the setting gives everyone a general kit). Create a character, guided. On Gear, the Given group lists the general kit once. Tap **Back** (to Who they are), then **Next** back to Gear. Go on to the review: the Equipment line names each given thing **once** (Rations ×2, not ×2 twice), and the count is the general kit plus your trinket and anything you bought.
2. **iPad, same campaign:** on the review, open **Change ›** on Gear, then **Done**. Back on the review the given things are still listed once.
3. **Phone (375 px), same campaign, a kindred whose trinket is a sentence** (e.g. one ending in "."). On the review's Equipment line the trinket reads without its full stop before the comma ("…sucked on, Backpack"), never "…on., Backpack".
4. **iPad in landscape, Safari, a private tab:** in a campaign where players pick their creation method, Create your character → Guided. On "How do you want to build?" pick a method and scroll to the end: the line "… picked · about N steps" and **Next** sit fully above the home bar, with space under them. Repeat on an iPhone in landscape.
5. **iPad, a classic-advanced campaign at its defaults** (weapon proficiencies off): guided creation to **Who they are**. There is **no "Weapon proficiencies"** field. Turn the option on in Rules, start another character: Who they are asks for it.
