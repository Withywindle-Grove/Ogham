# Manual checks: dec-10-b-starting-scores

1. Start a new character, pick a step or two, and tap Leave. Tap New character again. The sheet "Continue where you left off?" says what you left and when, then the quiet line "Kept on this device only." and nothing more. Its title sits balanced over its two lines on a phone.
2. In a setting with more than one way to build, on How to build: a way that leaves some classes out shows, under its row, the warning sign and "The <class> then needs the GM's special approval" (or "3 classes then need …"). A way that leaves none out shows no warning.
3. Pick that way and go on to Class: the class it left out is still there, pickable, marked as needing the GM's special approval.
4. On Adjust scores, the prime's tile has a boxed "Prime" tag in small capitals.
5. Open Help me choose on Adjust scores: the experience table has a dark bar "Experience by prime score", your prime's name over the first column, and your own band in bold, with no "(you)" written.
6. On the full sheet, open your experience's explanation: its table looks as before.
7. Phone and tablet, light and dark: steps 1, 2 and 5 again.
