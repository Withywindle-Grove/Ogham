# Manual checks: dec-10-fa-long-tables

1. Make a stone kin and go to Gear. The Trinket row reads "Stone kin, d100. Not rolled yet" with Open, and there is no "Roll … for your trinket" line over it.
2. Tap Open. The sheet is titled "Trinket", says "Roll d100, then mark your row.", and the table has a dark bar ("… trinkets") and banded rows numbered "1 to 2", "3 to 4"…
3. Tap "Take it" with nothing marked: "Tap a row first."
4. Tap a row: it takes the wash, a dark edge, bold and ✓, and "Take it" turns dark red. Nothing is in the pack yet. Tap the row again: it's unmarked.
5. Mark a row and tap "Take it": the sheet closes, and the Trinket row reads "Rolled 11: …" with Change.
6. Tap Change, mark another row, then the X: the trinket hasn't changed. Change again, mark another, Take it: it's replaced; Undo on the toast puts the old one back.
7. On Details, open a short table (Face, say): a tap still picks it and closes the sheet.
8. A kit with rolls (a dredger's kit, say): Pick. The sheet reads "Roll 1 of 3", then "Roll d6, then tap what you rolled.", and a table with the roll's name on its bar ("Armour") and "Result" over the column. A roll made twice: "Roll d4 twice, then tap each result."; tap a row twice: ✓✓ and a − square beside it.
9. With a pack whose given gear has a pick table ("Odds and ends: pick up to 2"): the row reads "0 of 2 picked. 6 to choose from". Open it, tap two rows: they're marked, nothing is in the pack. A third says "2 of 2 marked: take one off to mark another." Tap "Take these": both are in the pack and the line reads "2 of 2 picked". Mark another and fold it again: nothing is taken.
10. With a keyboard: Tab reaches every row and "Take it"; Enter marks.
11. Phone and tablet, light and dark: steps 2, 8 and 9 again.
