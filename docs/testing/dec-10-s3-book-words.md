# Manual checks: dec-10-s3-book-words

1. On the preview, start a new character. On Kindred, tap a kindred's ⓘ. The entry opens with:
   - its name in heavy brick-red capitals;
   - its lines, each a coloured label with its value beside it;
   - then "Advantages" and "Disadvantages" as headings over lists, each item a bold name ending in a full stop;
   - then the open classes as chips;
   - "From your setting's rules: …" last;
   - one "Pick" button at the right.
2. Tap one of the class chips. The class's entry opens with "Back to <kindred>" as an underlined link at its top and no Pick. Tap it to go back. From the review, an entry you open shows "Got it" at its foot, as before.
3. On Class, tap a class's ⓘ. Requirement, Prime requisite, Hit die, Languages and the level cap come first, then the lists.
4. As a cleric, on Patron, tap a patron's ⓘ. Its title sits under the name in small capitals. Holy garb and Marks show a line per tier, indented under their label.
5. On First spells, tap a spell's ⓘ. Level, Range, Duration and Saves are labelled lines, the spell's text is a paragraph under them, and the page comes last.
6. On Gear, tap a kit's ⓘ. About, Worn and held, Carried and Cost are labelled lines. "Starting coin" above the coin box is in the small-capitals face.
7. Pick something from an entry: its button reads "Picked ✓".
8. Open your finished character's sheet and tap a spell or a patron. That entry looks as before.
9. Phone and tablet, light and dark: steps 1, 3 and 4 again. In dark the name is copper and the sub-headings a light tan.
10. Gallery (`/gallery.html`), section `creation-final`: the four new specimens (a class's entry, a patron's entry, a class read from a chip with "Back to Stone kin" at its top, the book table, its rows banded).
