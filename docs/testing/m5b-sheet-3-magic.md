# M5b — the sheet's Magic section (sheet plan build order 3: sr-4; feeds gate check 64)

On the seeded campaigns (`npm run seed:test`, private packs), or on the layout harness (`npm run dev`, then `/layout.html?as=player&magic=1` or `&magic=empty`; invented data: Wren, a Lantern Warden who keeps a spell book). Open each character with **View sheet** (or **Character options › Full character sheet** on a phone).

1. [ ] **iPad landscape, a book caster with spells** (a book caster from fae-woodland or classic-advanced, or `magic=1`): after Traits, a **Magic** section: "Spells a day" with "Level 1 ●●●  Level 2 ●●" with `magic=1` (dots, never a list of numbers such as "3, 2, 0"); no separate "Spell slots" box anywhere on the sheet. Under it, buttons "Spell book · N", "Prepared · N" (and others), Spell book pressed. Each row: the spell's name (a quiet "given" box beside a spell the class gives), then "Level 1 · 3 turns · 40 ft". Tap a row: the spell's entry opens (Level, Range, Duration, Rules); **Got it** closes it.
2. [ ] **iPad landscape, two kinds of magic** (a fae-woodland character whose kindred and class each give a kind, or `magic=1`): a tab per kind, named as the pack names its list (e.g. "Glamours · 2"). Tap one: its spells show, the button in the selected colour.
3. [ ] **iPad landscape, Prepared:** after preparing spells on the Player screen (Prepare spells), the sheet's "Prepared · N" counts them; a spell prepared twice is one row "<name> ×2".
4. [ ] **A caster with no spells picked** (Decide later on First spells, or `magic=empty`): the Magic section still shows, "Spell book · 0" and "No spells in your book yet."
5. [ ] **A class with no magic** (a fighter, a thief): no Magic section at all. A divine class below the level it casts from: none either.
6. [ ] **Phone 375 px:** the tabs wrap to a second row rather than scrolling sideways; a very long spell name ("The long lantern of the drowned abbey…" with `magic=1`) wraps inside its row; every tab and row is a comfortable tap. Dark mode: the pips in copper, the pressed tab in the dark selected colour.
7. [ ] **A spell list of more than 8** (a high-level book caster): the tab shows 8 and "N more ›"; tap it: all of them.
8. [ ] **Phone 375 px, gallery** (`/gallery.html`, "Full sheet: Magic"): six spell levels wrap onto a second line, nothing cut off at the right, level 4 (none) not shown; with VoiceOver, "Spells a day", then "Level 1, 4 a day" and so on. Five kinds: the tabs are the smaller buttons, in two rows, each still a comfortable tap; on the iPad the same five are full size. "Fen tricks · 11" shows 8 and "3 more ›". "Prepared · 0" reads "Nothing prepared yet."
