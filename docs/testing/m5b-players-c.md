# M5b: the Pack on creation's standard (plan m5b-players-screens-standard, slice C)

Use a campaign with a pack attached and **Carrying** set to coins. Give one character these things: a Lantern held in hand, a Coil of rope, six Torches (one stack), a wand with charges, and one one-off waiting for the GM (as a player, Add item, then type a thing the list doesn't have). Open that character's **Pack** tab. Check on the iPad first, then on a phone at 375 px, in light and dark.

1. [ ] **The load line comes first:** at the top you see the tier (such as "Light, speed 40 ft"), its bar, then the figures. There's no middle dot anywhere on the screen. Tap the tier: the tiers open, one per line, with commas.
2. [ ] **The groups:** each heading has its count beside it ("Worn and in hand", "1 item"). Every row shows its name, one quiet line under it (in hand, its charges, its weight, with commas between) and a chevron at the right.
3. [ ] **An item's sheet:** tap Coil of rope. A sheet comes up from the bottom on a phone, and opens beside the row on an iPad. You see the description, then rows: Give to… and Put in… with a chevron, then **Drop** apart at the bottom, under "Leaves it where you stand. Undo from the toast". There's an empty star beside ✕.
4. [ ] **Pin:** tap the star. The sheet closes, and a toast says "Coil of rope pinned to the top", with Undo. The rope's line now starts with a small filled star. Open the Player screen: the rope is in At hand. Back on Pack, open the rope: the star is filled and shaded. Tap it: "Coil of rope unpinned", with Undo.
5. [ ] **Undo the pin:** pin the rope again, then tap Undo in the toast: the star leaves its line.
6. [ ] **Drop with no confirm:** open the rope and tap Drop. It's gone at once, with no question. The toast says "Dropped Coil of rope". Tap Undo: it's back.
7. [ ] **A stack:** open the Torches. "How many: 6" has − and +, and + makes it 7. Drop asks how many first.
8. [ ] **Charges:** open the wand: "Use a charge" is a row there, and it spends one. When none are left, tapping it says "<wand> has no charges left." It's never greyed out.
9. [ ] **The waiting item:** below the groups is a card with the warning sign: "1 item waiting for the GM", the item's name under it, and a chevron. Tap it: a sheet "Waiting for the GM" lists the item. Tap the item for Cancel it.
10. [ ] **The Party stash:** below that is a row "Party stash", with "N items: …" under it naming the first three A–Z, and a + at its right. Tap it: it opens (−). Leave it open, go to Player and back: still open. Close it, reload: still closed.
11. [ ] **The foot:** at the bottom you see the count ("9 items") and **Add item**, the only accent button on the screen.
12. [ ] **At hand on the Player screen:** the wand's row has **Use a charge** at its right, and tapping it spends one without opening anything. Tap the wand's name: its sheet opens. A weapon in hand: open its sheet, then the **Attack** row opens where the figure comes from.
15. [ ] **The Pack's At hand group:** pin the Coil of rope and a torch-type light (anything the Light section can light). Back on the Pack, "At hand" is the first group with "2 items", and the rope is no longer in its own group anywhere on the screen. The rope has no action, so it stays in its own group with its star; the light goes to At hand. The light's row says **Light**: tap it, and the Light section on the Player screen shows it lit; the row now says **Put out**.
16. [ ] **Every At hand row has its button** (Francis, 2026-11-07): pin arrows, a draught and a spear. The arrows say **Fire**: tap it, the count drops by one, and the toast says "Fired …, 17 left" with Undo, which puts it back. The draught says **Drink**: the last one leaves the pack, and Undo brings it back. The spear says **Attack** and opens its sheet with its figures. Nothing in At hand ends in a chevron.
13. [ ] **Armour mode:** set Carrying to armour. Gear is a fold row ("Gear", "N items: …. Not counted for speed"). With something At hand it starts folded; with nothing At hand it starts open.
14. [ ] **Phone 375 px, light and dark:** nothing scrolls sideways, a long item name wraps inside its row, every row and the star are a full finger's tap, and the sheet's Drop card is set apart.
17. [ ] **The full sheet's Pack load:** open a character's full sheet. Each group is a row with the + square at the right, reading like "Worn and in hand: Reed spear, Storm lantern" or "In the Backpack: 8 items"; tiny things read "Tiny things: 5, not counted". Tap a row: it opens to its things and the square shows −. No ▸, no "·".
18. [ ] **Item sheet details:** tap the Lantern (or anything held). "Slot" sits at the left and "hand" at the right edge, on one line.
