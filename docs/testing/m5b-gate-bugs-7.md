# m5b-gate-bugs-7 — Start expedition shows the whole Scene at once

From Francis's M5b gate walk (2026-11-06): after Start expedition only the time strip changed; the place card and the day's travel showed only after leaving the tab and coming back. Feeds gate check 17.

1. **iPad, the GM, a campaign whose setting has a day-by-day mode, no expedition under way.** On Scene, pick the day-by-day mode in the Mode row (it shows only when the setting has two or more modes), type a place in **Where? (optional)**, tap **Start expedition**. At once, without leaving the tab: the strip reads "<mode> · day 1", the place card shows the place you typed, and the travel card shows with **Move…**.
2. **iPad, same campaign:** tap **Undo** on the toast. At once, the strip is back to the Mode row and Start expedition, and the place and travel cards are gone.
3. **iPad, gate check 17's "known place":** End expedition, then Start again with the same place picked from the **recent places** chips under Where?. The pieces you showed players at that place come back shown (the toast says how many).
4. **Phone (375 px), a player in the same campaign:** while the GM does 1, the player's Scene follows within a few seconds (this still comes through the live connection; it is not changed here).
5. **iPad, the GM, a day-by-day expedition with weather due:** enter the weather roll. The card shows "Today's weather · <result>" with its effects in words, and stays until tapped. Tap it: it folds into the small cloud by the date. Tap the cloud: the card opens again. If nothing seems to happen, scroll down the time strip and say where the card appeared.
