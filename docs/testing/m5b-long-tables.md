# Manual checks: m5b-long-tables (long describing tables in a sheet)

Open the pull request's Vercel preview on the iPad, then on a phone. You need a campaign whose packs give a kindred a describing table of 50 rows or more (a d100 table on Gear or Details), a short table with a die (a d30 trinket table), and a name table of 40 rows or more.

1. **Two columns.** Make a character up to Gear (or Details) and open the d100 table. On the iPad the sheet is the screen's width and the rows run in two columns, the left column first (1, 2, 3 … down, then the right). There are no "1–25" fold rows to open.
2. **The range bar.** Tap **51–75** on the bar. The sheet scrolls so row 51 is just under the bar, and 51–75 shows pressed. Scroll on by hand: **What you rolled** and the bar both stay at the top of the sheet. Tap **1–25**: back to the top rows. On a table whose rows each cover several numbers, the bar's labels are the numbers each band covers (1–50, 51–100, say), each band 25 to 40 rows.
3. **Typing a roll.** In **What you rolled**, type 37: nothing changes yet. Tap **Show**. Only row 37 shows (never row 3), with **Show every row** under it, and no line in the strip. Tap its Pick: it's picked as before. Tap **Show every row**: every row is back and the box is empty.
4. **A ranged row.** On a table whose rows cover several numbers (31–40, say), type 37: the 31–40 row shows.
5. **A number no row has.** Type 140 and Show: under the strip a line says "No row has 140.", the 140 stays in the box and every row is still there. Type 3.5 (on a keyboard) and Show: "Whole numbers only."
6. **Opening on a pick.** Pick row 62, tap Done, then Change: the sheet opens with row 62 in view under the bar.
7. **Phone.** The same table on a phone: one column. The strip at the top is two lines: "What you rolled", the box and Show on one, the bar on the other. If the bar doesn't fit, swipe it sideways (it doesn't wrap). Tapping the box shows the number pad.
8. **A short table with a die.** Open the d30 trinket table (15 rows): the box and Show are there, no bar, one column. Type a number and Show: its row.
9. **A table of 24 to 49 rows.** Two columns on the iPad, the box (if it has a die), no bar.
10. **A long name table.** On Who they are, open a name table of 50 rows or more: the box and the bar are there, no folds. Type 37 and Show: the first-name cell on that row shows; tap the second part's tab: its cell on the same row shows.
11. **Large text.** At the Large (and Larger) text size on the phone, "What you rolled" may sit above the box, but the box and Show stay side by side on one line; nothing is cut off.
