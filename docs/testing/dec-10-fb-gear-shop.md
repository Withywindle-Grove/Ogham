# Manual checks: dec-10-fb-gear-shop

1. Make a character to Gear. "Starting coin" is a heading of its own; under it "Roll 3d6 × 10, then enter it.", the stepper with the coin's name beside it, and "Average" under it.
2. Take a kit with a price: under the stepper, "Average, 22 silver spent: 78 silver left" (your coin and numbers). Type a roll: "120 rolled, …: … left".
3. A kit with a price shows it at the right of its blurb's first line, bold; a free kit shows none.
4. "The shop" is one row with Open, no second line. The foot reads "No kit yet, nothing bought" before anything is taken.
5. Open the shop. "‹ Gear" at the top left, "Shop" as the heading, then "100 silver to spend", and nothing about the load.
6. The search box shows "Search items" inside it. The categories show their counts ("Armour: 2") with a chevron; tap one to open it.
7. A row: the name underlined (tap it: its entry opens), what it is and its weight under it, the price at the right, and a Buy button with two coins. A thing your class can't use shows "▲ A Reed Witch can't use it." and why; Buy still buys it.
8. Buy a thing that doesn't stack: it's bought at once, with an Undo toast, and the top's number goes down.
9. Buy a thing that stacks (rations): a screen opens with the count, "Where it goes", the cost, and a "Buy 1" button; tap + and it reads "Buy 2".
10. "Done shopping" is at the right; back on Gear, what you bought is listed with its "Put back for …".
11. Phone and tablet, light and dark: steps 1, 5 and 7 again.
