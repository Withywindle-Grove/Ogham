# Manual checks: m5b-creation-retest-3 (creation retest 3, PR 1: A–E, 5b, 5c, 5d)

Open the pull request's Vercel preview (it uses the test project), sign in as a player in a campaign with the classic-advanced pack (and the homebrew tables pack, for a kindred with two name tables), and start a Guided character. An iPad and a phone (or the browser at 375 px wide) for the size checks.

1. **Roll for a name, one table.** On Who they are, under the Name field: one button "Roll for a name" with a die; no "Name tables" fold row. Tap it: the table's sheet opens straight away. Its first line is the pack's prompt if the table has one, else "Roll d20, then tap the rows."
2. **A tap only marks.** Tap a first name: it gets ✓, the tab moves to the next part, the Name field doesn't change, the foot says "Picked: …, … still to roll." Tap a second part, then **Use this name**: the sheet closes, the field holds the name, and under it "From <table>, rolled 3 and 14. Edit the field to change it."
3. **Edit and re-roll.** Type one letter in the field: the "From …" line goes. Tap Roll for a name again, pick other rows, Use this name: the new name and a new line.
3b. **A part left unpicked.** On a two-part table with printed halves ("Adder-" / "-ley"), tap only the first half and Use this name: the field reads "Adder" with no hyphen, and the line says "…, second half still to roll."
4. **Nothing tapped.** Open the sheet and tap Use this name without tapping a row: the foot says "Tap a row first: …" and the field is unchanged.
5. **Several tables.** With a kindred that has two name tables (the homebrew pack's): Roll for a name opens "Roll for a name", "Two tables give names: open one.", listing both with Open; open one, tap ‹ (Back) at the top left: the list again.
6. **Tablet.** On the iPad the button sits beside the Name field; on the phone under it.
7. **Long tables fold by 25.** Open a d100 table (a trinket in Gear, or a d100 name table): four fold rows "1–25", "26–50", "51–75", "76–100", all closed. Open one, tap a row; close and reopen the sheet: that row's group is open, the others closed. A d20 table has no folds.
8. **Describing tables start collapsed.** Who they are with a short table field (a knight's liege): its rows sit under "N to choose from / Nothing picked yet"; the row opens them. Gear's pick-up-to-N table and a backgrounds step: the same. Pick one, go Next and Back: the fold starts open with "Picked: …".
9. **Pick a row twice (5d).** On Gear's pick-up-to-N table, Pick a row, then tap its button again: it reads "Picked ×2", a − square sits beside it, the heading says "2 of N picked". Each tap shows its own toast with Undo. Tap −: back to "Picked ✓". In the Pack, a stacking item (rations, arrows) is one line with the total; a non-stacking one is two lines.
10. **Non-class weapons (D).** With restricted weapons on, Weapon skills: "Not your class's weapons: N" is closed, with the pack's rule as its line. Open it: each weapon has Pick, the sign and "Needs the GM's special approval". Pick one: it counts; on the review, the exceptions card names it. As the GM, the approval card shows it with the pack's rule and Allow it.
11. **The review (E).** Kindred, class and alignment read as plain text ("Kindred: Stone kin"), no rounded chips. Open Derived values: square tiles, three across on the phone (four past 1000 px), nothing between them; the saves in their own group under "Saves", five across on the iPad. A word value ("Medium") is smaller than a number. Tap a tile: its breakdown sheet.
12. **Trade chips.** Adjust scores, make a trade: the chip is square-cornered with ✕.
12b. **Two primes say which counts.** With a two-prime class on Adjust scores, the lead says which prime sets the experience (the pack's own words, or "With two primes the lower one sets your experience.").
12c. **Long tile labels wrap.** The Player screen's save tiles and the full sheet's Score tiles: a long name takes two lines, never "…"; the tiles in a row stay the same size.
12d. **Next is the one accent (5e).** On Method, Kindred, Class and Gear, no row's Pick is filled in the accent colour, before or after a pick; Next is. On an iPad, with an entry open beside the list, its Pick is plain too.
12e. **An item's full entry (5f).** On Weapon skills, tap a weapon's name: its sheet shows the pack's description, then Qualities, Damage and use, Cost and Weight as label and value rows, then the page, and Pick at the foot. In Gear's shop, tap an item's name: the same sheet, the shop's price as its cost; Pick adds it as Add does.
12f. **Word figures and the coin's words.** On the review's Derived values, a word value ("Medium") is smaller than a number and in the heading font; a long one stops at three lines with "…" and its tile's sheet has the whole words. Leave the starting coin untouched: the review's Gear card says "…, the average (not rolled)", never "usual".
13. **Prime table (5c).** On Class, pick a class, tap Help me choose: a small table "Prime score / Experience", one row washed with ", you". On Adjust scores, Help me choose: the same table, the row following your prime as you trade. A setting without the experience table shows none.
14. **Gear's words (C).** On Gear, the lead and Help me choose quote no dice; only the Roll row shows the coin dice. (The pack's words; the app only checks.)
