# Manual checks: dec13-c2-head (plan dec-13, row C2: the head and the aligned top; frames P1 and P2 of december/14)

On the pull request's preview (`/layout.html?...`), or `npm run dev` and the same addresses on the URL it prints. Each page opens on the campaigns list: tap "The Storm Lantern Road".

1. Open `/layout.html?as=player&p1=1` on a tablet held sideways. The bar reads "Brannoch Fenward the Bog folk, Warden". Under it, a large "B" circle and four rings beside it, no words. The first score box at the right starts level with the hit points box.
2. Tap the snowflake ring. The "Chilled" sheet opens.
3. Same page on a phone. The bar says "Brannoch Fenward"; the head says "the Bog folk, Warden" beside the portrait, the four rings under it.
4. Open `?as=player&p1=messy` on a phone. You see three rings and "+6". Tap "+6": a "Statuses" sheet lists nine. Tap "Watchful": its own sheet opens. On a tablet: four rings and "+5".
5. Open `?as=player&p1=two`. Each ring has its name and time in words beside it.
6. Open `?as=player&p1=noline` on a tablet held sideways (a setting with no roll for scores). "Scores" stands alone, with no die line, at the bottom of its space, level with the foot of the portrait; the first score box still starts level with the hit points box.
7. Open `?as=player&p1=classless` on a tablet held sideways (no class). The head shows the GM's card line (its fields, ending "Level 3") above the rings, and the head is taller than the portrait to hold it; nothing spills out. The aligned top does not hold here (see the plan's C2 Departures).
8. Repeat step 1 in dark mode.
