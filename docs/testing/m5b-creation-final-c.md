# m5b-creation-final-c: creation's final drafts, slice C (Kindred, Class, Who they are)

Open the pull request's preview (test project). Use the creation fixture campaign, or a campaign with classic-advanced or fae-woodland attached. Check on the phone (375) first, then the iPad upright (820). To compare, the mockups are `docs/design/mockups/creation/final/02` and `03` on `design-gate-m5b-walk`.

1. **Phone. A closed class.** Create, then Guided. Pick a kindred that some class isn't open to (the fixture: Stone kin).
   - On Class, the closed class (Reed Witch) is in the list after the open ones. It has the warning sign and "Not open to stone kin", and no Pick. There's no folded "Not open to …" group.
   - Tap its name. A sheet opens with the sign and why under the name, then a sentence saying whose rule it is and that your GM can allow it. At the foot is **Ask the GM to allow it**, with no Pick.
   - Tap Ask the GM to allow it. You go on to the next step, and a toast says "Reed Witch picked: needs the GM’s special approval", with Undo.
   - Finish and send the character. As the GM, the approval card lists "Needs the GM's special approval: Reed Witch" with the reason.
2. **Phone. A closed outlook.** Pick a class with an outlook limit (the fixture: Marsh Chaplain). On Who they are, a closed outlook (Mire) shows the sign and "Not open to marsh chaplain".
   - Tap it: a sheet explains and offers Ask the GM to allow it.
   - Tap that: Mire is picked, marked "Needs the GM's special approval", and Next goes on.
3. **Phone. Name tables.** As Stone kin, look at Who they are.
   - The Name box is empty with the grey hint "Type a name, or pick one from a table".
   - Inside the fold: a die and, in bold, "Roll d6 for a first name and a surname, then tap the rows." (your pack's die and parts).
   - Under it, one folded row "Name tables: 1" (or your count) with +. It's folded every time you reach the step. Tap it: the die and the bold line show, then one row per table with **Open**. Another pack's table says "Optional, from <pack>" first.
   - Tap Open. A sheet comes up with the table's name, the die and "Roll d6 for each part, then tap the row.", one tab per part (the first is pressed), and the rows. There's no number box.
   - Tap a row: the name gets that part, and the next part's tab opens. Tap a row there: the name is whole. Tap the same row again: that part comes off. ✕ closes the sheet.
   - Each row shows its number in a narrow column on the left and the name beside it, with no colon. A d100 table's rows sit under "1–9", "10s" … headings.
   - The Kindred step's line reads "What your character is born as. It sets what they can be and do before any training." and Class's "Your trade as an adventurer.", unless your pack has its own line (classic-advanced does).
4. **Phone. Hit points.** The heading "Hit points" appears once (no second small label over the box). Under it is the die of your class and, in bold, "Roll d8 for hit points and enter it." (plus "Grit 13 adds 1." when your score gives a bonus).
   - The box already holds the average, in a lighter weight, with "Average value, replace with your roll" (and "With +1 for …: N hit points" with a bonus).
   - Type your roll: the number goes full weight, and the line reads "3 rolled, +1 for Grit 13: 4 hit points".
   - With a minus bonus and a 1 rolled, it says "1 hit point, never below 1".
   - Leave the average untouched and send the character: the review's hit points read "HP 4, the average, not rolled". The GM's card says the same.
5. **Phone. The liege** (a knight-like class whose pack has a liege table). The houses are pick rows. Pick one: a toast says the liege and the outlook set, with Undo, and Affiliation fills with the house. Change Affiliation by hand: picking another house leaves your words alone.
6. **Phone. Derived values.** Folded, reading "6 figures the rules work out" (your count). Open it: each figure is on its own row, and Armour shows its parts beside it. Tap a figure: its breakdown opens.
7. **iPad (820). Who they are's second column.**
   - With nothing open, Derived values sit in the right column.
   - Tap Open on a name table: the table shows in the right column, not as a sheet.
   - Close it: Derived values come back. Tap a house's name (a knight): its entry shows there.
8. **Phone (375). Messy data.** A 40-character table name wraps on its row and in the sheet's title. Hit points of 0 and 999 stay in the box. A class with 0 open classes shows the closed ones and the line "No class is open to this pick".
9. **No middle dot.** None of the above screens shows " · ", "‹" or "›".
