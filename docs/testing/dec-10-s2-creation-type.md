# Manual checks: dec-10-s2-creation-type

1. On the preview, open a campaign as a player and tap **New character**. Every creation screen should be in the book's type: a serif face (Bitter), with the step's title in bold. Next and Back are in the same face.
2. On the Scores step, look at any score with a 0 in it (10, for instance). The zero should be plain, with no slash.
3. On the Class step, tap a class's ⓘ. The entry that opens, a sheet on a phone or beside the list on a tablet, should be in the same serif face as the step.
4. Leave creation and open your character's sheet, the Rules tab or the GM's screens. These should look as before: no serif body text.
5. As GM, start a new campaign. Campaign setup shares creation's step frame, but its steps should look as before.
6. Phone and tablet, light and dark: steps 1 to 3 again, in both themes.
7. Gallery (`/gallery.html`): the creation sections (`creation-shell`, `creation-review`, `creation-who`, `creation-gear`, `creation-final`, `creation-final-d`, `creation-long-tables`, `creation-trait-list`, the patron's creation specimens, and the same-name Class step) should be in the book's type. Every other section should be unchanged.
8. On a phone, finish a character and open the review. The figures (Armour, Attack, Speed…) sit two across, every word whole: "unarmoured" on one line, "Nimbleness +0" on one line. The saves stay three across.
9. On Scores, look at a score of 11 or 10 in its box. The 1 has its little foot, like the 1 in "+1 to hit" under it.
