# Manual checks: dec-10-e-magic-languages-weapons

1. Make a character whose kindred rolls for magic (a stone kin, say) and go to Magic. Under "From your kindred": "Roll d6, then tap the row.", then a table with a dark bar naming it, a head row with the die and the column's word, and banded rows, each with its number at the left ("1", "2 to 3").
2. Tap a row: it takes the wash, a dark edge, bold and its ✓. Tap another: the ✓ moves.
3. A caster whose class rolls (a reed witch, say): the table's name is on its bar and not said again above it. A row whose spell you already have reads "Already yours: roll again"; tapping it says so.
4. Tap a rolled row's ⓘ (at the right of the row): the spell's entry opens. Close it; nothing was picked.
5. A character starting at level 2: the last group reads "Pick 1 more, for level 2".
6. "Given" beside a given spell or language is in small capitals.
7. Languages, with a pack whose languages are rolled: "Roll d6, or tap any row.", then the table (column "Language"). A language your kind can't learn stays in its row with "▲ Not open to …"; tapping it explains. One needing the GM's leave keeps its row with the sign and can be picked. The heading reads "Pick 1 more" with "0 of 1 picked" beside it; the rest are under "Others you may choose".
8. With a score that gives two languages, the rows let you tick two.
9. Weapon skills: "Pick 2" sits directly over "Weapons", with no empty band.
10. With a keyboard: Tab reaches every row of a table and its ⓘ; Enter picks.
11. Tap Roll for a name on Who they are: the name table's rows are banded again.
12. Phone and tablet, light and dark: steps 1, 7 and 9 again.
