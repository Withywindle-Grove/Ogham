# What players may read of a place's rules and its check (2026-11-13)

Piece 3 adds two read functions for players. A change to who may read what needs Francis's yes with the change said in plain words; he gave it after reading these words.

| Id | Ruling | Whose |
|---|---|---|
| rp-1 | **Players may read the open rules of the place they have been shown**, and of the places it sits in: never a rule the pack marks GM-only, never one the GM has closed. | Francis: "yes" |
| rp-2 | **For the last place shown to them, players may read when its encounter check is rolled and its chance** ("on every move, 1 in 6"): never its table, never its note. | Francis: "yes" |
| rp-3 | A rule the pack marks `gmOnly` never reaches a player, whatever the GM's switch says: the database refuses it, and on the GM's screen its eye is the crossed eye and cannot be opened. (His yes was "never a rule the pack marks GM-only"; whether a GM may one day open such a rule is a new question, for after 5 December.) | The lead's, from his words |
| rp-4 | A rule that reaches players from a place they were **not** shown (a region the shown place sits in) appears under the shown place's own name: no name, id or other fact of the unshown place is sent. If the region itself has been shown, its rule carries the region's name. | The lead's, under hard rule 5; his to loosen |
| rp-5 | A link mark in a rule's text never reaches a player as a raw id: an unlabelled mark is put into words as the app shows it, and a mark pointing at something the player has not been shown reads as plain words. | The lead's, under hard rule 5 (found by the local review) |
| rp-6 | A place's own encounter check is named by the pack when the pack gives `encounterCheck.name`, else "Encounter check"; a pack's name is printed as the pack writes it. | Francis: "approved", on the builder's pick |

The database enforces rp-1 to rp-5, not the screen; each has a test as a player proving what is not sent.
