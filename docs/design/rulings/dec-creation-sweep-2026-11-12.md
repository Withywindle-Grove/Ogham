# The style sweep of character creation: rulings (2026-11-12, night)

Francis asked for creation to be swept to the day's standards and made "dec 5 ready" first, with patrons live at once. The Design Studio redrew every creation screen in eight batches from the built screens (`docs/design/mockups/creation/final/16-…` to `23-…`, branch `design-december-mockups`). Batches A to E he approved one by one ("Approve your changes", "Approve with fixes"…). For F, G and H and the final scan he pre-approved the lead's changes: "I pre approve your changes for the remainder of the batches, and when complete I want you to do a scan of each redraw also with changes preapproved." Rows marked (lead) were ruled under that pre-approval; he may overturn any of them. Examples are the mockups' invented ones.

## What the scan found
One early picture had not been brought to the rulings, and the example character's figures disagreed between pictures; both were redrawn. The gallery's Gear specimen and the approved mockup's shop did not match the built screens; Gear and the shop were redrawn from the Builder's pictures of the real screens. The creation shell in the pictures is the built one (the back arrow, the title centred, "⋯", the count under the bar: ruled 2026-11-07); one picture that showed another bar had been rendered outside creation.

## Honest notes
- The lead opened every picture's first render and re-opened about a quarter of the final ones; the Studio opened every final picture in both themes. Contrast was worked from token values, not measured. Larger text was not drawn.
- Batches E, G (in part) and H were drawn from the approved mockups where no picture of the built screen existed; their captions say so.
- cs-13 and cs-15 change behaviour; the hit points prompt at level 2 has a words fault (it says "add 1" where the app adds one a die). These are on the "creation is ready" list with the sweep.
- Not built and not in the sweep: taking back a character already sent; setting a patron on an approved character.
