## His answer

"P7 all a"

For p7-1 the sheet asked for his yes in plain words, since it changes who may write what. The sentence put to him, and given with the answer above:

> A player marks a call sent to them as seen. That is one mark the player writes for themselves, readable only by that player and you; nothing is dropped, renamed or deleted.

| # | Question put | Ruling (option (a) of the sheet) | His words |
