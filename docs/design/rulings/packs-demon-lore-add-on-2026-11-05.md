# The next add-on to classic-advanced: its open cases (design ruling, 2026-11-05)

The pack session's inventory of an add-on with demons, cults and new classes (its note is beside the region inventory in `design/notes/`), ruled one at a time with Francis. Ids `ao-1` … `ao-7`, and `sn-1a`. Invented examples only.

- **ao-1 (C) Study, sigils and rituals.** A caster studies the subject (a cost, weeks, a chance to fail), learns a sigil per creature by research, and performs rituals that aren't memorised (time, materials, a failure chance that grows with the creature's toughness over the caster's level). **Now:** text panels, a trait saying the character has studied, and a typed list of sigils on the sheet; the GM runs it from the text. **Later:** a ritual kind is designed once, with downtime's second stage (study and research are downtime).
- **ao-2 (A now) Familiars.** A bound familiar gives its owner extra hit points and a save bonus while it serves, wants paying each week, and costs a level if it dies. **Now:** a hireling-like companion whose effects are text, applied by the GM. **Later:** a companion whose bonuses count on its owner's sheet is designed in the hirelings session (M9), with mounts and the like; a5-5's conjured companion is the nearest shape.
- **ao-3 Commanding, beside turning.** No shape today: the book's third outcome ("command") and its table are walked in the paper test of magic in play, with the two settings' turning (a5-2).
- **ao-4 (B, in M7) A cult's weapon limits replace the class's.** A trait-list entry (a cult, a deity) may carry its own `weaponsAllowed`, which replaces the class's while that entry is chosen, as a class's forms do (eq-8); the equip check names the source. One rule for every class that picks a cult. Built in M7 with the deity work (G-62); text on the entry until then.
- **ao-5 Traits shared by a rank of monsters.** No ruling needed: 07's `group` and `groupAbilities` on stat blocks, as the existing packs write them.
- **ao-6 (A) Treasure that turns demonic.** A chance, on every magic-item roll of the base tables, that the item comes from the add-on's tables instead: a GM reference panel states the rule and the GM rolls it by hand; the tables are in the pack. No shape that patches a chance onto every table. If ever wanted, it goes with M7's treasure hand-out (rr-1).
- **ao-7 Bargaining.** A table and a GM reference panel with its modifiers, in the pack's own words; the app keeps no memory of earlier dealings with one creature (the GM's note; an NPC's attitude over time is M8b's).
- **sn-1a Identical entries in packs that don't require each other** (a refinement of sn-1's point 2). When an add-on needs an entry that an unrelated pack also has, identical (invented: both sell **Tallow candles (10)** at the same price with the same rule), it carries its own, with **`sameAs: "<pack id>:<id>"`**. With both packs loaded the app lists one (the one pointed at); with only the add-on loaded, its own shows. `pack:check` checks the two really are identical when both are given.
