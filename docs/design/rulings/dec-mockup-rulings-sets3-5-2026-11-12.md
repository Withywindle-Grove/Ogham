## Honest notes
- Several pieces grew past what the December plan assumed, the place's sheet most of all; none is sized yet.
- The fae-woodland-region pack's twelve settlements are flattened and cannot feed the place's sheet without about a day of pack work; the port's pack already can. Not needed for the first night.
- The dagger for "pin" sits on the same row as a hook's mark, a note pinned by a dagger; the Studio judged it not confusing once drawn.
- With the "You" tag gone, only a name tells a player's own character from the rest where portraits are alike.
- The toggle's "on" state in light mode, with colour taken away, rests on the knob and its tick alone: a pass, not a comfortable one. The switch without its tick is the weakest of its forms in grey, and reads.
- Contrast was worked from token values throughout, not measured. Two colours need names in 06: the sub-heading's and the box's ground.
- Still for Francis: the Row and Swim icons and their arrangement; the built descriptor at 16 px against the mockup's 14 to 15; whether his table sees the book's own step names or the verbs used on the pictures; the app's own five words; his concepts for six of the port's places.
