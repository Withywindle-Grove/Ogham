# Creation's sweep against the built screens, and the night's last rulings (2026-11-12)

After the eight batches were drawn, the Builder drew the real creation screens whole for the first time, and the Design Studio compared them with its frames: 23 differences on Class, Scores, Gear and the shop, then 41 more on the entries, Who they are, Magic, Languages, Weapon skills, Details and the review. Several pictures Francis had approved left out things the app shows, or showed things it does not. The lead ruled the differences under his pre-approval and told him each one that changed something he had approved. Examples are the mockups' invented ones.

## The principle
| Id | Ruling |
|---|---|
| cs-30 (lead) | The sweep dresses the built screen: it removes nothing the build shows and adds nothing the build lacks. Where a picture and the built screen differ in what is shown (a line, a part, a state, an order), the build wins and the difference comes to the lead. The pictures govern type, the book format, icons, glyphs, placement, and the words ruled in cs-1 to cs-29. |

## What changed in pictures he had approved
| Id | Ruling |
|---|---|
| cs-31 (lead) | Who they are keeps the build's one sentence ("A name the table will remember."); its parts and order are the build's: Name, Starting level, Hit points, the alignment pick under the pack's own label, the portrait, motto and backstory. |
| cs-32 (lead) | An entry shows the build's parts in the book format, never invented ones. A kindred: blurb, Requirement, Languages, Advantages, Disadvantages, Open classes as chips with their caps (a tap opens that class to read, with a way back). A patron: its parts in the build's order, garb and marks as plain tiers (no "To come" at creation; that mark belongs to the sheet). A spell: Level, Range, Duration, Saves, the text. A weapon: Qualities, Damage and use, Cost, Weight. A kit not yet taken: what it holds, its cost. On a phone's sheet "Pick" carries the accent; in a tablet's pane it is plain (the screen's accent is Next). |
| cs-33 (lead) | The Class step as built: a summary line of the kindred and scores; a row with the pack's blurb and a fit line carrying all the build's does, in the short wording ("Might 15, d8, +5% experience, to level 12 as Stone kin", "casts" for a caster); no row's Pick has the accent; the foot warns "Pick a class to continue" and Next has the accent; two folds, the classes needing the GM's approval and "Not open to <kindred>" (a row inside does not repeat the heading). ## Facts the comparison turned up
- A campaign sets where new characters start (Rules, Characters, "New characters start at": level 1, the party's lowest, a fixed level, or the player proposes). With a fixed level the step shows no control.
- Next carries the accent on every step, with nothing picked too; no row's Pick does (already ruled and tested).
- The gallery's creation specimens were parts, some out of date; whole-screen pictures of the built steps now sit beside the mockups.
