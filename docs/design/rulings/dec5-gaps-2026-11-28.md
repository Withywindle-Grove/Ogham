## G-27: a place with no encounters

Today `encounterCheck: null` (or none) means "inherit the parent's"; there is no way to say "none here". The nearest place in the chain with a check decides (`placeCheckOf`), and a check that says `replacesScheduled` stands in for the setting's scheduled check, with the GM's switch (rg-5) to put the setting's back.

| ⚑ | Question | Options | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
