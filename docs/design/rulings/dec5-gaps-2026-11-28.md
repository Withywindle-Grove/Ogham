## G-27: a place with no encounters

Today `encounterCheck: null` (or none) means "inherit the parent's"; there is no way to say "none here". The nearest place in the chain with a check decides (`placeCheckOf`), and a check that says `replacesScheduled` stands in for the setting's scheduled check, with the GM's switch (rg-5) to put the setting's back.

- **dg-1 (⚑ 1 A): the pack's line.** `"encounterCheck": { "none": true, "note"?: "…" }`: an object, so the pack's own sentence and `name` still fit. It sits in the chain where a check sits, so "the nearest place decides" and "covers the places inside" need no new rule. Invented example: `"encounterCheck": { "none": true, "note": "The harbour watch keeps the quay quiet." }`. `pack:check` warns on `none` with a `chance`, `tableId`, `tableRef`, `every` or `byTrack` beside it, and on `none` that isn't `true`.
- **dg-2 (⚑ 2 A): what "none" does.** It is a stand-in check that never comes due: while the party is there or inside it, the setting's scheduled check, a place's check further out and the GM's own encounter-check reminders are all silent; a place inside with its own check still decides for itself; **Check now** still rolls the setting's check (the GM asked); the GM's switch (rg-5) puts the setting's check back there, logged, with Undo.
- **dg-3 (⚑ 3 A): what shows.** The GM's "Checks here" card says "No encounters here." with its switch on, and the pack's sentence under it, as any stand-in check's card. The facts row's Encounter check tile, for the GM and the players: "None here". The words on both are as dg-17 redrew them (the tile's small line "Its own rule."; the card's second line; the card switched off). Players read the check in force on this tile already (page 44, PS2); "None here" is the same fact.
- **dg-4 (⚑ 4 A): Move on's "Then" line** into such a place has no check line: nothing comes due, so nothing is said.
- **dg-17 (page 48's findings 1, 2, 4 and 6): page 48 approved.** Finding 1: the tile's small line is **"Its own rule."** (a place's name no longer reaches the tile, so it is never cut at 375 px). Finding 2: for "none" the card's second line is **the pack's sentence and "The Quay's own rule." only** ("It stands in for … while the party is here." is dropped). Finding 4: switched off, the card reads **"Encounters as usual here."**, with the same second line as before ("River Rules' wandering encounters apply here again, until you switch this back."). Finding 6, **a builder test, not a drawing**: a "none" place inside a place whose check says `replacesScheduled`: the nearer decides (the "none" place is silent; a place inside it with its own check decides for itself). Findings 3 and 5: no action.

## G-233: an item's count names its unit

Today a counted item says "charges" everywhere: "3 charges left", "Use a charge", "Charges: ?", the Reveal pop-up's "Its charges", the toasts and the log. The format has `unitName` and `pluralName` for a stack's pieces (torch, torches), nothing for a count.

- **dg-5 (⚑ 5 A): where the word lives.** On the item's `charges`: `"charges": { "initial": 3, "rechargeable": false, "unit": "portion", "units": "portions" }` (invented); `units` defaults to `unit` + "s"; with no `unit`, the app says "charge". One field, one place: no default per category, no field on Add item.
- **dg-6 (⚑ 6 A): which words change.** Every place the count is said: the row's line ("2 of 3 portions"), the item's sheet ("Portions: 2 of 3 left"), "Use a portion", "Portions: ?", "Portions not set", "Rum bottle has no portions left.", the Reveal row "Its portions" and its toast ("Told Wren: the rum bottle has 2 portions left."), the log ("Wren used a portion of the rum bottle"), the GM's roll prompt ("Portions (3)"). The stacked button stays "Use"; only its spoken name changes. **Recharge** stays "Recharge".
- **dg-7 (⚑ 7 A): hidden data.** The word travels with the item's details on its line (the line's copy of the item, which the database already fills); where those details don't reach the holder (an unidentified item's face, or `gmOnly: ["charges"]`), the holder reads "charges" until the fact is told: "doses" could tell a player an unidentified thing is a potion (hard rule 5). Technical, for the spec: confirm the line's copy carries `charges.unit` for an identified item (the line keeps `charges` today: `item_data ? 'charges'`, `supabase/migrations/20261121120000_q2_stacks_join.sql`), so no migration.
- **dg-8 (⚑ 8 A): items already handed out** keep "charges" until handed out again (the GM gives the thing back to the stash and out, or adds it again). No update to stored rows.
- **dg-18 (page 49's findings 2 and 3): page 49 approved.** Finding 2: the app writes **"an" before a vowel** ("Use an ounce"); **07 says a unit is a countable word** ("flask", not "oil"); **`pack:check` warns on a unit that ends in "s"** (a plural, or an uncountable word, given as the unit). Finding 3: when the full count is 1, the line reads **"1 dose left"** (not "1 of 1 dose"). Findings 1 and 4: no action (Recharge stays, dg-6; "Portions: ?" as built). Finding 5 (info) is moot: frame 47-3 was approved on 28 Nov with C2 and D1. For the spec, technical: "an" before a vowel is the first letter's rule, so the spec names its test cases; a checker cannot know every uncountable word, so the warning is the ending in "s" plus 07's sentence.

## G-228: what a person carries (a first slice)

Francis: "important that they move with and are attached to the NPC"; "the packs can tell us what items are held by which npc but the rules and functionality is app-based". Today a pack's people are GM-only library entries with no screen of their own; a person shows to the GM as a **person piece** on the place page (from a room's `playerView.people` with its `npcId`), named by Q7 under "Only you see this.". - **dg-10 (⚑ 10 A): the slice.** The person's piece carries what they hold: under "Only you see this." their row (name and stat line, Q7) and one line, "Carries: Warden's lantern, iron key." (on a phone above the stat line, dg-19); the piece's words and arrow open **the person's sheet**, the find sheet in the person's dress (page 50): over the title the place, the title their name, the piece's words, "Carries" and their things a row each (the book's note under each), "Edit the piece's words" last. **No "Send all"** on a person's sheet (dg-19, finding 1): a row a thing, each sent from its own one-thing sheet (TR1's: To a character, To the party stash). - **dg-11 (⚑ 11 A): one way.** The GM sends a held thing (to a character, or to the party stash). Taking, trading, stealing and looting at death are the table's story; the app records only that it changed hands.
- **dg-12 (⚑ 12 A): keyed to the person.** A thing given is gone from that person wherever they next appear (another place's piece, a later visit): their line reads "Carries: iron key.
