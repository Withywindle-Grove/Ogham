# Magic and advancement: the seven open cases of pack review area 5 (design ruling, 2026-11-05)

The gap rows G-137 … G-143 from the pack session's review of magic and advancement (`design/notes/area-5-magic-review-2026-11-04.md`), ruled one at a time with Francis (each with A, B and a synthesis) once magic in play was moved ahead of fights. Ids `a5-1` … `a5-7`. Invented names throughout.

- **a5-1 (A) A spell reversible on one list and not another (G-137).** `reversible` and the reversed form are read **by list**, as range and duration already are; the checker accepts the keyed form. One spell today; no pack change.
- **a5-2 (deferred) Turning as data (G-138).** No shape is ruled. One setting turns by a table (the caster's level against the creature's toughness, then a roll for how many), the other by one roll read in bands with modifiers and limits. A block per style would be a shape per rulebook. **One turning attempt in each setting is walked in the paper test of magic in play**, and the shape follows from what the screen needs. Until then: the rule as text on the ability, and the table where a pack has one.
- **a5-3 (synthesis) A setting's general magic rules (G-139).** Two of the facts the screens need already have fields: how magic comes back (`spellcasting.recover`) and when a reversed form is chosen (`spellcasting.reverseAt`). **One new field per caster class: `spellcasting.duplicates`** (`true` | `false`, default `true`): whether the same spell may be prepared more than once. Everything else in a rules set's magic section (casting conditions, effects stacking, a lost spell book, research) stays **reference text**, opened from a **Rules ›** link on the Prepare and Spells screens; shown, never enforced.
- **a5-4 (A, with a condition) XP after a level drain depends on what drained (G-140).** A drain (a stat block ability's or an item's) may name its own rule, `levelLoss.xp` with the same values as the rules set's (`midpoint` | `minimum`); the rules set's `levelLoss` is the default. **Lose a level shows which rule it is applying and the GM can switch it**: never silent. Read in M7 with level loss; packs may carry it now.
- **a5-5 (A, widened by Francis) Creatures a spell conjures (G-141).** Each conjured creature is a **stat block the spell points at** (`refs.statBlocks`); the spell's text keeps its printed stat line, so the player can always read it. **In play:** casting makes the creature a **temporary companion of the caster**, with the spell's duration as its timer; it is listed with the party like a hireling and readable by the caster's player (it is theirs); the number conjured is entered when it is rolled. **In a fight** it is a combatant on the party's side, acting with the party (ns-1), whether cast during the fight or already there when one starts. It leaves when the timer ends, it is dismissed, or it drops; all logged, with Undo. **What scales, and the shape** (the pack session's survey of the thirteen conjuring spells, the same day): in one setting no stat line changes with the caster's level; what scales sits outside the creature (how many, for how long). So the spell carries **`conjures: [{ statBlockId, count?, note? }]`**: `count` is a number, dice (rolled at the table and entered) or a formula in the caster's level, as durations already are; the creature's time is the spell's own duration (a timer, or none for concentration and "until dispelled", X-3); `note` carries what limits the caster's control ("turns on its maker if the caster is harmed", "cannot fight", "obeys, then flees"), shown on the companion's card and never enforced. Where a creature's own figures do change with the caster's level (one steed in the other setting, in five bands), a stat block **variant may say which caster levels it is for** (`variants[].casterLevel: { min, max? }`) and the app picks it at casting. A spell that reshapes any monster by the caster's level stays text, the GM's to run. A conjuring effect with no creature of its own (a count or bonus equal to the caster's level, nothing to list) stays in the spell's worked-out values.
- **a5-6 (A) A class whose spell list is chosen (G-142).** `spellcasting.listId` may be a list of choices. The player picks one on the Class step at creation; the pick stays with the character, Change › at the review can redo it, and **the GM's approval card shows it as a choice the GM confirms** (the book gives the choice to the GM). No campaign option.
- **a5-7 (the general shape) A reversed spell with its own figures (G-143).** A spell may carry **`reversed: { name, range?, duration? }`**: the reversed form's own figures, written as the spell's own are, overriding them in the reversed form's pop-up and worked-out values. `reversedName` is still read as `reversed.name`. One spell today.

**How it stays hidden-safe (the builder's read, technical):** the companion is a party-side actor owned by the caster's player, not a monster; the figures it needs are copied from the stat block when it is made, so no monster entry becomes readable by a player.

**For the pack session:** a5-1 nothing; a5-2 nothing until the paper test; a5-3 add `spellcasting.duplicates` only where a book forbids preparing a spell twice; a5-4 the drain's `levelLoss.xp` on the four stat blocks and the items; a5-5 a stat block per conjured creature (reusing one that already matches), `conjures` on the spell with its count and control note, the one banded steed's variants; a5-6 the zine class's two lists; a5-7 the one spell's `reversed` block.

**Not checked by the designer:** the app code for any of these; the sizes are the builder's and are in the plan.
