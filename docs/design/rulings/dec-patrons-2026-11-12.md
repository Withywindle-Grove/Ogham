# Patrons: when one is chosen, by whom, and where (2026-11-12)

Ruled by Francis on the pictures of the built patron section and on the Design Studio's mockup of the Patron step (`docs/design/mockups/creation/final/14-patron-step.html`, branch `design-december-mockups`), which he approved. The option he called the "enhanced cleric rules" is the existing option **"Expanded cleric creation"** of the patrons session of 2026-11-07 (pt-1): one switch a campaign turns on, off by default.

| Id | Ruling | His words |
|---|---|---|
| pt-9 | A patron is chosen only when "Expanded cleric creation" is on for the campaign. With it off no patron is asked for or shown anywhere. Every pack's patrons hang off that one option: a second switch in another pack is a fault. | "patron should be selected only as part of the 'enhanced cleric rules'… optional rules" |
| pt-10 | Choosing a patron is a creation step of its own, straight after Class, shown only for a class that takes a patron. Next with none picked explains and does not skip; a blocked Next is never a disabled button. | "select patron should be its own step, after selecting 'cleric' as a class"; "approve" (the mockup) |
| pt-11 | On the review the patron is a line in the first card, under kindred and class, with its own Change; with no patron the line is absent. | "B" |
| pt-12 | The classes that choose a patron are those the packs name as its followers: for the December campaign the rules' cleric, the zine's acolyte, and now the rules' paladin and drow, who also cast from the cleric's list. The region's cleric and friar keep their book as written (his ruling of 2026-11-07). | "b" |
| pt-13 | If the option is switched off later, a character's patron is hidden on the sheet, the review and everywhere, and its value is kept: switching the option back on brings it back. Items of holy garb already held stay. | "a" |
| pt-14 | If the option is switched on after a character was approved, nothing asks that character for a patron. A way to set one afterwards waits until after the December campaign's first night. | "a" |

**Costs and caveats.** The rework is about 2 to 2½ builder turns; the migration already on the databases is not affected. The app already gates each patron by its own option, so pt-9 is a pack matter and one test. The paladin and the drow may hold a patron before they can cast: the patron's garb and marks follow the tiers the pack carries. What the ⓘ on a patron's row opens is drawn in the mockup from the sheet's fields and has not yet been compared with the built entry.
