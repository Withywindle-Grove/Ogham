# The full character sheet review (design ruling, 2026-11-04)

Francis's aim (2026-11-03): every field on each setting's printed sheet is on the full sheet or one tap away, fields tied to an option appear only when it's on, and this becomes the model for future settings. Review: `design/notes/sheet-review-2026-11-03.md` (passes 1–3 on the printed sheets, the packs' layouts, the app and the seeded characters). Mockups: `design/mockups/sheet/01-sheet-review.html`, `02-sheet-extras.html`. Ruled one at a time with Francis, each with A — Builder, B — Critic and a synthesis. Ids `sr-1` … `sr-10`.

- **sr-1 (A) Combat and movement.** Four tiles, each captioned: **HP** ("4 of 4 · 1d8 · CON +0", hit dice in the caption), **AC** ("12 · leather · DEX +0"), **Attack** ("+1 · melee +1 · missile +1", never a bare number; THAC0 where the option says), **Level** ("1 · 0 / 2,000 XP"). **One Movement section** in the pack's words (speed, exploring, in a fight, overland) from the travel speed (bq-5); the other movement figures fold under Speed, one tap away; no raw "Movement: <mode>" boxes. Every tile opens its breakdown.
- **sr-2 (A) Skills in one place.** Class skills leave the Combat grid. Two groups: **everyone's** (classic-advanced's adventuring skills among them) and **the class's own**, named after the class; a kindred's change as a tag; each figure the pack's way ("5", "1-in-6", "87%"); a group folds after 4. *(Note, 2026-11-04: the "5" in this example reads "5+", as 06 and 07 write equal-or-over skills; the builder built "5+".)* The Player screen uses the same names in the pack's capitals.
- **sr-3 (A) Traits** (cs-7's layout): rows grouped by source (kindred or race, class), each opening its entry (ct3-11), use pips where it has uses, drawbacks tagged in words, later-level traits shown quietly ("from level 3"); folds after 6.
- **sr-4 (A) Magic.** A **Magic section** on the full sheet, only for characters with any magic: spells a day as pips per level (not "1, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0"), a tab per kind the character has (Spell book · Prepared · Glamours · Runes · Knacks), each row opening its entry. Whether casters also get their own tab is decided with M7's Cast.
- **sr-5 (A) Advancement.** XP with a bar to the next level and the words beside it, the prime-requisite bonus with its reason (rc-1), the level title; replaces the lone "XP modifier".
- **sr-6 (A) A field shows only when it applies** (its option on, its class or race, the kindred not excluded: of-9, cs-3, ct3-4b); "—" only for an applicable empty field (Affiliation, earned in play); every value opens its entry.
- **sr-7 (A) The order.** Tablet: two columns, play figures left (Combat, Saves, Movement, Skills), who they are right (Abilities, Traits, Magic, Identity, Advancement); Backstory and Notes below. Phone: the same order stacked, each section folding to a one-line summary that opens in place.
- **sr-8 (A) Tapping Attack:** the breakdown (class figure at this level; melee and missile with their modifiers) and, **with descending AC, the to-hit row** (the d20 roll by AC 9 … 0, "from the rules: descending AC"); with ascending AC, "+N to the d20 against AC".
- **sr-9 (A) The Pack tab's load:** one bar per band (fae-woodland's equipped and stowed, ga-1; weight: one bar), each with its count and speed, the slower named "sets your speed"; tiny items as their own group; no coins line until purses (M7b).
- **sr-10 (A) The model for future settings.** The sheet review is a written method in `docs/plans/pack-review-method.md` (list the printed sheet's fields; map each to the app; check seeded characters on screen with options on and off; check, recheck, a fresh session's pass). A setting pack carries **`sheetFields`**: the printed sheet's fields by name, each naming its home (a layout field, a figure, the Pack tab); `pack:check` lists any printed field with no home. *(Technical confirmation, 2026-11-04 evening, after the packs' `sheetFields`: homes `name`, `class`, `portrait` added; a field may carry `option`; an overlay may carry its own `sheetFields`; the checker reads the stack.)*

**Already ruled and still to build, seen on screen:** Liege for knights only (cs-3), moon signs not for excluded kindreds (ct3-4b), weapon proficiencies hidden with the option off and the duplicate field gone (of-9), the friar's and the fur's AC bonus (cs-7), the XP modifier (rc-1, #347), Exploring blank (#337).
