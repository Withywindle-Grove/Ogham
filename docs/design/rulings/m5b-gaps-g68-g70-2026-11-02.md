# Gaps G-68 to G-70 (design ruling, 2026-11-02)

From byzantine-dungeon stage 2 (the volume-4 appendices; private detail in `packs/byzantine-dungeon/GAP-REPORT.md`). Francis ruled each from A/B/C options, one at a time, 2026-11-02 evening.

| Gap | Ruled | Francis |
|---|---|---|
| G-68 a rival party as one encounter | **A** — encounter table rows may carry `statBlockIds: [ids]` beside `statBlockId`; tapping the rolled row in Add foes adds every member, at the band (variant) the GM picks once. An `encounterGroups` kind waits until a second adventure needs groups outside a table. M6 follow-up. | A |
| G-69 an item whose study grants a reward | **A** — deferred whole to the **downtime design session** with training (G-32) and projects (G-54): study becomes a downtime activity with a duration and a reward, and the reward shapes are ruled there. The pack keeps its notes and the XP field. | A |
| G-70 hiding one custom field of an item | **A** — the importer catches up with 07: `gmOnly` may name a custom field by its label (`"fields.Location"` or the label itself), hiding that field and leaving the rest player-readable; `pack:check` warns on a label that doesn't exist. No format change; Fable-sized; any time before M8b. Until then the pack session's stopgap is the location in `notes`. | A |
