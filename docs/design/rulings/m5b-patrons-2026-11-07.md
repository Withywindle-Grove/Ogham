# Patrons and expanded cleric creation (ruled 2026-11-07)

Francis, 2026-11-07, from the daemonology add-on's gods (demon lords) and the zine's gods, which share one shape: a cleric may follow a patron, and the patron changes what the cleric wears, wields, prays and casts.

## Ruled

1. **A patron is one pack kind, `patrons`**, the same in the daemonology add-on, the zine and any pack that has it: a god or a demon lord with: who may follow (classes, alignments); **holy garb** (items given); **strictures** (text); **weapon restrictions** (a list that replaces the class's); **equipment restrictions** (armour and other gear the patron forbids or requires); **prayer** (text); **alternative spells** (a spell list the patron swaps in or extends, the G-80 `extends` shape); **mark of servitude** (a trait). All in the pack's own words (07's IP rule).
2. **A patron applies, not only shows:** its weapon and equipment restrictions replace the class's at creation and in play (the equip warning, the attack penalty), and its spells are the cleric's list.
3. **An optional rule, "Expanded cleric creation"**, off by default, in every pack that carries patrons (daemonology, the zine, …). On, creation gains a **Patron** step for the classes the pack flags (clerics, and whatever else the book names), after Class and alignment: pick rows, the patron's entry on a tap (garb, strictures, weapons, prayer, spells, mark as its sections), a patron the alignment or class doesn't fit as a closed row with the sign and **Ask the GM to allow it**. The sheet carries the patron in the identity block; the GM's card calls out its exceptions.
