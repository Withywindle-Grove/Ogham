# Fight dry run rulings (Francis approved, 2026-10-29)

Changes to M6 from Francis's paper dry run of a fight. Saved from the design chat, with the example foe's name replaced by an invented one. The specs say what is true now; open points this raised were listed in `docs/10-open-questions.md` (M6 fight dry run).

**Open points answered (2026-10-29):** all eight ⚑ this raised (death panel taps, when Down clears, hireling HP, the confirmed hit for AC, Talk and Avoid detail, dice-duration spells, the turn buttons' words, journey budgets) were answered the same day and removed from 10; see the follow-up lines under 2026-10-29 in `docs/09-decisions-log.md` (the death panel, Down, hireling HP, AC, chips, dice durations, End our turn / End turn, journey 26 and `abilityLoss` lines). The buttons this page calls "Our turn's done" and "Done" are now **End our turn** and **End turn**.

| # | Ruling | Date |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Encounter outcomes:** Fight · Talk · Avoid, all always available whatever the reaction roll says (a hostile result is a starting attitude, not a limit). The reaction ⓘ explains what each result means at the table, from the pack, not only how to roll. This replaces any narrower set of endings in M6-7. "We run" stays separate (escaping a fight already started). | 2026-10-29 |
| 2 | **Players don't record damage to foes; the GM does.** "Players enter damage to foes" (M6-1) is removed from M6 (it's in the parking lot). A player may tap a foe (optional) to see their roll and hit target (once AC is known; before that, "Roll and tell the GM"). That tap shows on the GM's screen ("Bram → Bog lurker 1") and pre-selects the foe on the GM's damage pad. Skipping it is fine: the GM picks the target by hand. | 2026-10-29 |
| 3 | **GM mirror:** tap a foe, then a character, to see the foe's attack and what it needs ("d20 +1 · hits Bram on 14+", from the character's AC). | 2026-10-29 |
| 4 | **Turns end by tap and the round advances by itself.** Side initiative: any player taps "Our turn's done"; the GM ends the foes' turn. After both, in initiative order, the next round starts automatically (logged, round timers tick). Individual initiative: each player taps Done for their character; the GM does so for each foe or group. The GM can always end any turn or the round ("Next round" stays as an override). Nothing nags. This replaces "Acting now" taps as the routine step. | 2026-10-29 |
| 5 | **Initiative follows the setting's rule:** rolled every round or once per fight. The round-start "which side won" tap appears only when the rule re-rolls; ties follow the pack. The same side can act twice in a row. **Delay:** a button on a character's turn (where the rules or GM allow). It sets a visible "Delayed" flag; the character can act later, including during the foes' turn. The flag clears when they act, or at their next normal turn. | 2026-10-29 |
| 6 | **Party damage is the players' job:** each player records damage to their own character; a hireling's HP is managed by their employer (any player may in principle). The GM can override or take over. | 2026-10-29 |
| 7 | **Casting (refines M7-3):** a spell opens a sheet only when the player has a real choice to make (reversed form, targets). Otherwise it casts in one tap with Undo, and any light or timer starts by itself. The GM never confirms a cast. | 2026-10-29 |
| 8 | **Morale prompts come only from the setting's rules** (pack triggers). App reminders such as "leader down" or "employer drops" appear only when the pack's rules include them; the app never invents a trigger. | 2026-10-29 |
| 9 | **Down and death (refines M6-5): no pop-up.** At the setting's threshold the character is marked Down quietly, for everyone, and the player announces it at the table. Tapping a Down character opens the death panel, built from `rulesSets[].death`. Plain rule (for example, 0 or less is dead): the rule's text, Dies, and Not yet (fiat, in ⋯). Dying rules: the rule's text, rounds so far and when death comes, Helped / Healed / Stabilised and Dies; per-round effects happen by themselves at each new round, as automatic HP loss or a prompt for the death roll (Dies / Survives). "Helped" results follow the rule: a wake-up timer from a roll the GM enters, or HP restored plus a permanent ability loss the player rolls and picks. No "keep dying" button. Logged and undoable. Both private test packs were checked: the panel must cover their four cases (two settings, death's door off and on). | 2026-10-29 |
| 10 | **Temporary (bonus) HP:** a character carries bonus HP {amount, source, optional timer}. Damage takes it first (the pad shows the split). The HP bar shows it as a separate band. It ends on its timer or at 0. Main path: the granting spell, ability or item (M7; pack field `grantsTempHp` { dice \| formula, duration }). Quiet manual entry: "Add bonus HP…" in the character's ⋯ (M6). New register row G-23 (status ruled). | 2026-10-29 |
| 11 | **A Down or dead character's pack and purse open to the other players,** like the Party stash: Take from <name>, per item or amount, every take logged. A named exception to "other players' purses are hidden", written into CLAUDE.md (hard rule 5, "Hidden means hidden"), 03 and the visibility rules, and enforced in row-level security by character state (Down or dead). It closes again if the character recovers. A hireling's pack is always open to their employer. | 2026-10-29 |
| 12 | **Every foe card has a quick "+ Status"** (from the setting's conditions or typed, optional round timer) **and "Note"** (GM-only by default, can be shared), each one or two taps from the card. Subdual damage stays R2 (G-14); until then, use a status or note. | 2026-10-29 |
| 13 | **We run is on the players' screen too:** any player can call it for the party; the GM resolves it with the setting's pursuit rule (Got away / Caught). We run can be undone (Undo toast and log): Undo returns the party to the fight exactly where it left off (round, whose turn, statuses, timers, HP, foes and their states). | 2026-10-29 |
| 14 | **Planned and random encounters matter equally.** Prepared encounters (M8b rooms, or a saved group) carry Hidden and Arrives-in settings, so mid-fight setup is only for escalation. Added to the M8b design notes. | 2026-10-29 |
| — | **Loot picker:** offers "Add "<typed>" as a one-off" when a search finds nothing (the same path as Add item's one-offs; the GM needs no approval). | 2026-10-29 |

## 09 lines

The thirteen lines added to `docs/09-decisions-log.md` under 2026-10-29: encounter outcomes; the GM records damage to foes; GM mirror; turns end by tap; initiative timing and Delay; players record their own characters' damage; casting asks only for a real choice; morale only from the setting's rules; Down and the death panel; temporary HP; Down or dead packs and purses open; + Status and Note on foe cards; We run on the players' screen with Undo.

## Notes from writing it into the specs

- The source asked for "CLAUDE.md hard rule 6"; the hidden-purses rule is hard rule 5 ("Hidden means hidden"), so the exception went there.
- The visibility matrix (`docs/generated/visibility-matrix.md`) is generated from the migrations' access rules, so it shows the exception once the M6 migration lands; until then the hand-written rule is in 03.
- So that morale reminders like "leader down" can come from a pack, 07's morale triggers gain the events `leaderDown` and `employerDown`.
