# The light's ring is the tap (2026-11-22)

Francis, shown a phone picture of a lit torch, asked for its two toggles to line up. Page 34 drew that (Keep lit's toggle under the lit toggle, Refill to its left), and he turned it down: "I don't like it". He then gave his own idea, the ring as the tap, and asked: "Mock up your recommendation". The driver kept hard rule 7 (Undo over confirm) in it. Page 35 was drawn to that and **he approved it from pictures the same day**, "Approved", after the driver gave him the four answers below. The answers are as the driver relayed them; only the words in quotes are his own.

Mockup: `docs/design/mockups/december/35-the-light-ring-is-the-tap.html`, frames 35A to 35F, light and dark (`december/png/35-*.png`).

## The rulings

| Id | What | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| lt-1 | The light's lit or out | **No toggle on the light's row** (supersedes tg-1 for this alone). The light's Status ring is the tap. |
| lt-2 | The ring as a tap (the affordance) | **The ring over its verb, one target with no edge** (72 by 76 px; the verb in the stacked button's word style): "Put out" when lit, "Light" with one light to light, "Light…" with several. A ring with no word under it is not a tap (burnt out). The light's name still opens its item sheet. |
| lt-3 | Lit, tapped | **Put out at once, no pop-up, with Undo** (hard rule 7). The light stays in its place, not lit, keeping its fuel as today. |
| lt-4 | Not lit | **The Light section and its "Light" heading and button go.** A light that isn't burning sits in At hand, in the lit light's place: the ring empty (the track alone, the flame in the muted colour) with "Light" under it. With one light, a tap lights it at once with Undo; with several, "Light…" opens today's pick, unchanged. |
| lt-5 | Burnt out | The ring struck through and still (no verb); today's words ("Burnt out. 3 refills left"); **Refill** and **Light another** as stacked buttons at the right, one width (page 14, frame P5). Burnt out with nothing to light: the words alone. |
| lt-6 | Keep lit | **Its own row under the light:** its name and ⓘ at the left, its toggle (the bare switch, unchanged) at the row's right edge, and for a holder **Refill** (the stacked button with the jug, ic-3) 8 px to the toggle's left. Shown only while the light burns, as today. |
| lt-7 | The head's status rings (answer 1) | Their tap still opens a status's details; the light's ring acts. **Yes: the verb under the light's ring tells them apart.** |
| lt-8 | The toasts' words (answer 3) | **The app's own word order:** "Put out Storm lantern", "Lit Torch", each with Undo. The brief's "Storm lantern put out." is not used. |
| lt-9 | The character overview (answer 4) | **Its Keep lit stays as it is.** |
