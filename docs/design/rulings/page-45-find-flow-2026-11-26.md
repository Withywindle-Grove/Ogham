## Honest notes

- **What "Approve" covers.** His words approve the page as redrawn after the first sitting, with FD11 and the fixes he picked. The pictures of those fixes (FD11 as redrawn again, FD3c, FD11b, FD9b D's shorter toast) were made after it. He has not seen them, and `impeccable critique` has not run on them.
- **p45-21 changes what K20 built.** `newFinds` (`src/modules/characters/findsSeen.ts`) reads a row's age against the mark, and with no mark every row is new, so a character's whole pack would read "New" on the first night; a find that joins an older stack is missed. "New" now reads the log line of each of the four roads. The seen mark, its table and its rules do not change. Which lines a character's gift, "Give coin…" and the GM's Add item write, and whether each names who did it on the player's device, was not read.
- **Not asked under p45-21.** A thing another person's tap makes by another road: an activity's yield the GM resolves, a hoard shared out (K17). The build counts the four roads he named and no other until he is asked.
- **p45-23 as read.** "Before a call card, one of the two at a time" is read as: with a find card and a call both due, the find card shows and the call waits for "Got it", one tap. A call for a roll can so wait behind a find. If he meant the call to take the place first, K18d changes one comparison. The slot stacks its other prompts, newest on top, with one call card last (p7-9, `src/app/PromptSlot.tsx`). Only the find card with nothing else due is drawn (FD1c C).
- **p45-25, the height.** In FD11 the card is 206 px before and after and "Got it" does not move, measured. That holds as drawn because the quiet line is the name's size and every line there is one row. Where a line carries its own giver and time, or the quiet words wrap to more rows than the name did, the build must hold the line's height itself; that case is in words only.
- **"Got it" still moves in one approved case:** a second find arriving on a card of one takes it from beside the line to the card's foot (FD10b C, p45-14). The re-check did not raise it and nothing here changes it.
- **p45-26 with p45-28.** After Got it there is no card, so a pop-up that closes because its thing was taken back has no line to say why. Only the Log says it.
- **The quiet line's "the".** "Your GM took back the …" doubles on a name that starts with "The" and reads oddly on coin. FD11 E draws the lean (no second "the"); it is ⚑ F8, and kin to p45-18.
- **p45-5 saves no height at five.** Measured at 375 px: four things listed are 325 px, and five (three lines, "and N more", "Got it") are 324, because "and N more" is now a 44 px tap. It saves from six up. A one-thing card went from about 230 px to 113.
- **p45-17's second sentence** was drawn after the first sitting ("Check your connection and try again.") and is gone: p45-24, "Short". One thing on the card still reads "these": the mark covers every new thing.
- **The critique ran on the page as first drawn,** from its source and four of its 21 frames as pictures. The re-check ran on FD11, FD3b, FD1 and FD1c as redrawn, in light only; it did not recheck the toast's words or the scroll-hold note, and it has not run on the second sitting's frames.
- **The redrawn pictures were taken in WebKit from the file,** at 256 colours, both times; the second sitting retook only the six frames it changed or added (45-3, 45-7, 45-19, 45-22 to 45-24). Larger text, motion (the scroll holding, p45-19), a screen reader and the tablet held upright were not checked.
- **Page 44's send,** which these cards answer, is still a proposal (#102).
