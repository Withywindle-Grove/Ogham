## Honest notes

- **F1 changes what K20 built.** `newFinds` (`src/modules/characters/findsSeen.ts`) reads a row's age against the mark, and with no mark every row is new, so a character's whole pack would read "New" on the first night; a find that joins an older stack is missed. Under the lean, "New" reads the send's or the gift's own log line. The seen mark, its table and its rules do not change.
- **p45-3 against the slot as built.** The slot stacks its prompts, newest on top, with one call card last (p7-9, `src/app/PromptSlot.tsx`). "Takes its turn" is read as one of the slot's cards; which goes first is F7, and FD1c C is drawn with nothing else due.
- **p45-5 saves no height at five.** Measured at 375 px: four things listed are 325 px, and five (three lines, "and N more", "Got it") are 324, because "and N more" is now a 44 px tap. It saves from six up. A one-thing card went from about 230 px to 113.
- **p45-17's second sentence.** The picked finding's fix reads "Couldn't mark these as seen. Check your connection and try again."; the driver's relay quotes the first sentence. FD9b D draws both sentences, the error pattern 06 gives. One thing on the card still reads "these": the mark covers every new thing.
- **The critique ran on the page as first drawn,** from its source and four of its 21 frames as pictures. It has not run on the redrawn frames or on FD11.
- **The redrawn pictures were taken in WebKit from the file,** at 256 colours. Larger text, motion (the scroll holding, p45-19), a screen reader and the tablet held upright were not checked.
- **Page 44's send,** which these cards answer, is still a proposal (#102).
