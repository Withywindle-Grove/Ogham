# M6 rulings (encounters and fights)

Plan: `m6-design-plan.md` (v2, approved 2026-10-26). Influences: kept private (packs/_design).

| # | Ruling | Date |
|---|---|---|
| M6-1 | **Who enters what.** Campaign setting "Players enter damage to foes" (Combat group, On by default). Players always enter their own damage taken; with the setting on they also apply their damage to a foe (tap the foe → amount → "Deal N"), which counts as the confirmed hit for revealing AC (CS-8). The GM can enter anything for anyone. Every entry logged with who made it; the GM can undo. The GM sees "<Foe> down · from <Name>" when a foe drops. | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-2 | **The round.** Header "Round N" with quiet elapsed minutes; the pack's combat sequence as a one-row reference strip whose current step follows Acting now (never tapped through); **Acting now**: the GM taps the side that won, then the other side ("Both" when the pack's ties are simultaneous; individual mode keeps 05's pad); **Next round** is the one primary (phone: sticky bar; tablet: top right of the control column): advances the clock, ticks timers, raises end-of-round reminders, resets Acting now; undo from the log, no confirm. Players see the round and the acting side. **No telegraph state:** nothing about what a foe will do is entered, stored or sent; the GM says it at the table. Budget: 3 taps, 0 screen changes, 0 typing. | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-2a | **Foe nudges (GM only).** Each foe group shows one or two muted lines drawn from pack or adventure text (behaviour, wants, tactics, how it fights), chosen by fight state when the pack tags lines (start, losing, below half…), else its first lines; "More" opens the rest (behaviour notes, abilities, tactics table). Moments the GM might miss show as a ▲ line (pack triggers, morale). Limited abilities show as dots (tap when used). "Roll tactics" appears only when the pack has a tactics table. No typing; never shown to players. | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-3 | **Damage and healing.** Foes in a group are numbered (numbers never reshuffle; players see the same numbers). **HP bar (GM, and later characters):** a segmented bar, one segment per HP up to 12, one segment per 5 HP above 12 (a part-lost segment fills partly), with "N of M"; no plain bar. Players see "took N damage", "unhurt", statuses and Down (Combat setting "Players see damage on foes": numbers by default, or words: hurt / badly hurt, or nothing). Big groups collapse unhurt foes to "N more unhurt". **The pad** (players and GM alike): title "Damage or heal", the targets named (removable ✕), Damage / Heal, a **stepper with −5 −1 [N] +1 +5** starting at that person's last amount (tap the number to type a big one), then "Deal N to <target>" / "Deal N to each" / "Take N damage" / "Heal N". **No fixed quick amounts** (they go stale as characters rise); replaces UI conventions §6.1's HP chips. **Area damage:** only the GM's pad has "Add a target". Statuses and stances show as status symbols. Lights in a fight: "Drop light" (lands burning, can be picked up) and "Put out". Theatre of the mind: nothing assumes a grid (grid combat: a later release). | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-4 | **Adding foes.** One "Add foes" sheet (GM). A search field at the top always filters all monsters. Two open/close buttons with icon, name and ▾/▴: **Bestiary** (all monsters grouped by the pack's monster types, collapsible, counts and source tags, like Add item) and **Encounters** (encounter and wandering tables, the current table first, others collapsed; tapping a row picks that monster with its "how many" dice filled). Tap to open, tap again to close; opening one closes the other. What's open on arrival depends on the way in: from an encounter check's "Hit", Encounters opens on the current table; from "+ Add a foe", both closed and search ready; otherwise both closed. **ⓘ** on every row opens the full entry (side panel on tablet, sheet on phone) with Add inside. After picking: **how many** (the pack's dice shown, stepper from the average; the app never rolls), **Hit points**: Average (default, Combat setting "Monster HP") / Roll each (one quick screen of boxes, the next opens on its own) / Roll once; 05's variable-HD prompt stays; **Modifications** from the setting (only when the pack has them; apply its stat changes); **More options** (collapsed, switches): One is the leader (foe 1; feeds morale), Hidden from players (never sent to players' devices; Reveal in one tap), Arrives in N rounds (added hidden; a reminder with Reveal on that round; players never see a countdown). Primary "Add 4 Mire goblins": into the current fight's foe list (or starts an encounter), sheet closes, the group highlights briefly, toast "Added 4 Mire goblins · Undo". Adding to a group already fighting continues its numbers. Allies: "Join the party's side"; hirelings join automatically. Budget: from a table row with average HP, 2 taps plus stepper taps. | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-5 | **Down, dying, dead, fled, surrendered.** *Characters and hirelings:* at the pack's death point the one M6 dialog opens ("Wren is at −2 HP", the pack's rule and source): **Wren dies** (M4b death flow; mid-fight the new-character step waits until the fight ends) or **Not yet (GM's call)** (fiat, logged). With dying rules on: a **Dying** status and ▲ line, then each Next round a one-tap reminder (**Applied** / **Was helped** for a set loss; **Dies** / **Holds on** for a chance); the final point opens the death dialog; the pack's cost of being helped shows as a reminder. Negative HP only when the pack allows it. Non-lethal damage (when the pack's option is on): a Lethal / Non-lethal switch on the pad, a second marker on the bar, "Subdued" at the threshold. *Foes:* Down on their own at 0 HP (logged with who); the pad's **Mark as**: Down · Dead · Fled · Surrendered (Down stays alive for capture until Dead); players see what their characters would; fled foes remembered (End fight lists them; a "Fled earlier" group at the top of the Bestiary; "went where" note optional); all four count toward morale. *Packs:* `rulesSets[].death` `{at, belowZero, note, dying?: {startAt, perRound: {hp} | {chance: {die, on}}, deadAt?, helped, note}, byOption}`; without it: "0 HP: dies?". Added to setting-1 0.5.4 and setting-2 0.2.7. | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-6 | **Morale.** Every trigger in the pack is modelled: `rulesSets[].morale` gains `roll`, `breaksWhen`, `triggers` [{id, event: firstDeath / halfDown / firstWound / hpAtOrBelow (fraction), scope: group / alone, name}], `oneRollPerSide`, `maxChecksPerEncounter`, `passesToStop`, `neverChecksAt`, `alwaysBreaksAt`, `noModifiersAt`, `modifierRange`, `result`, `explain`; retainers' `loyaltyCheck` gains `breaksWhen` and its list of occasions. App-own triggers (tagged with the app's name): leader down (M6-4's leader), a hireling's employer drops; Combat setting "Extra morale reminders" (on by default). A trigger shows as a ▲ line on the foe group with **Check** (once per group; limits respected; Skip clears it). The check: chance first ("Roll 2d8 · they break on 11 or more"; invented numbers, 2026-10-29), then **the target's parts**: score, each modifier with who added it (GM's + Modifier; pack modifiers only where the pack states them), the target; "Check 1 of 2 this fight"; **They hold** / **They break** / Skip. Break: the pack's result text, **All flee** / **All surrender** (M6-5 states in one tap) or Decide one by one. Passes-to-stop gives "Fights to the end" (no more reminders); never-checks and always-breaks scores apply. **ⓘ About morale**: the pack's short explanation, When to check (every trigger with its source tag), Limits, source line; packs without the fields show their prose rule and no automatic triggers. Hirelings use the same flow with loyalty. Players see nothing of the check. Budget: 2 taps (+1 on a break). Packs: setting-1 0.5.5, setting-2 0.2.8. | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-7 | **Encounter flow.** The GM's Encounter panel runs the pack's encounter order as a short procedure (S17 procedure parts; fallback: Surprise, Distance, Reaction), top to bottom, each showing the odds and its modifiers with sources; every step skippable, Fight always available. Nudges (M6-2a) under the title; optional **+ Objective** line (can be shown to players). **Surprise:** an on/off button per side ("Party surprised", "Goblins surprised"), off by default, glowing in the accent colour when on (the lit-light glow stays reserved for lights); the pack's notes beneath (ambush, light prevents surprise). A surprised side loses round 1 ("Goblins surprised: they don't act this round"). **Distance:** a reminder line only ("roll 3d4 × 5 ft", invented, the both-surprised variant, for the current mode); nothing to enter or share. **Reaction:** the pack's table as row buttons with the speaker's modifier shown, and **ⓘ** for the pack's explanation. **Endings:** **Fight**, or one-tap past-tense outcomes **Parleyed · Bribed · Evaded · Sneaked past · They left · Retreated**, each logged, applying the pack's time passing, back to Explore, with Undo; **Evaded** first shows the pack's evasion rule with **Got away** / **Caught** (Caught starts the fight). The encounter stays open while the table talks. Players see nothing until the GM shows something or the fight starts (05). Budget: about 4 taps for the full flow; straight to Fight 1. | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-8 | **Layouts** (canvas "M6 fight layouts"). *GM tablet:* header (Explore / Encounter switch, "Round N · minutes", place and time, Fiat, ⋯); three columns: **Party** (characters and hirelings: HP bar, AC, statuses, ▲ dying; recent log at the bottom) · **Foes** (numbered groups, GM bars, morale score, nudges, ▲ triggers with Check, ability dots, a dashed card for hidden foes with Reveal) · **Controls** (Next round at the top, round strip, Acting now, + Add foes, Crit · Fumble, Roll tactics, Log and End fight at the bottom). **Tapping any combatant** opens its panel over the controls column (party and foes never move): foes as ruled (stats, Damage or heal, behaviour, dots, triggers, Mark as, source); characters and hirelings (class, race, player; HP bar, AC, attack, Damage or heal; statuses with time left; light; saves; spells and abilities; Full character sheet). Back: ✕, tap outside, Escape; tapping another combatant swaps the panel. *Player phone:* Scene in a fight is one column: round and acting side, Foes first (numbered, "took N damage", statuses, Down; tap to deal damage and see what the character knows), the party's HP grid (when allowed), a log line, bottom tabs. *Player tablet:* Scene in a fight has three columns: Foes · Party · **You** (the Player screen's play column), so nobody switches tabs mid-fight; outside fights the Scene drops You. uiux checklist run: text 14 px minimum and 44 px targets fixed in the mockups; empty, loading, offline and long-name states follow the conventions (to show in the gallery). The table test is the real check. | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-9 | **Prompts.** *Principle:* a player taps only to record something that changes the game (damage dealt or taken, a spell spent, an item used); everything else on their screen is reference they can ignore. **Attacks (CS-8), in the campaign's AC style:** with the foe's AC known, the panel shows the roll first (ascending: "Roll d20 + N · hit on a total of AC or more"; descending: "Roll d20 · hit on N or more"), then the sum (attack bonus or THAC0, the foe's AC where it applies, each modifier with its source; players add situational ones with + Modifier); before the AC is known, "Roll d20 + N · tell the GM your total" (the GM always sees what's needed). Buttons: **Deal damage** and **Miss** (closes the panel, nothing logged; tapping outside does the same). **Saves (CS-3):** the GM may **Call a save** (who, save type): each target's screen shows their target built from their save and each modifier with its source, **Saved** / **Failed**, answers to the GM and the log; optional (the GM can just say it). **Natural-roll rules** from the pack shown as text on save and skill prompts ("a 1 always fails") where the pack states them (pack field `rulesSets[].naturals` per roll type: `{die, high: {on, text}, low: {on, text}}`; added to setting-1 0.5.6 and setting-2 0.2.9). **Crits:** no natural 20 / 1 buttons and no house-rule automation; players say "crit" and the table resolves it; the damage pad has no maximum. Crit and fumble tables one tap from the GM's controls only when a pack has them. **Spell disruption:** no casting declaration in the app; each spell's rules pop-up has **Spent** (crossed out like a cast spell) and shows the pack's disruption rule for reference. **Chance…** (GM): a chance in 6, a d20 target or a percentage; "Roll d6 · success on 1–2"; Success / Failure logged; note optional. **Intent-first rounds:** not included for now (Francis will decide after seeing combat without it). Dry run: a scripted 5-round fight counted on paper, target at most one tap per player per round on average; then the automated click-through at the M6 gate. | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-10 | **GM add-ins (tables): first version, to be dry-run.** Current table per place (05), first in Explore, Encounters and Fight, switchable any time. Rolling: roll at the table, tap the row or **type the roll** to light the matching row (d20, d100); each row says what tapping does: **Add** (a monster with its how-many dice, M6-4), **Table ›** (opens the sub-table in place with a trail back), **Event** (to the log). Two-dice tables as a grid (first die across, second down); weighted 2d6 as normal 2–12 ranges. **Quick table builder:** name, die, one row per line with ranges split evenly (editable), a row may link a monster or table, tag Encounters / Tactics / Crit / Fumble / Other; saved to the campaign's homebrew, logged. **Copy** (a new table) or **Edit** (your version replaces the pack's in this campaign, M3 stacking; the original untouched). Tags drive Roll tactics, Crit · Fumble and the Encounters list. Francis approved the look; the process is critiqued by doing: a dry run of creating add-ins joins the M6-12 dry runs before it's final. | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-11 | **Ending a fight.** **End fight** (the party stayed): no confirm, undoable (toast and log); a summary: time (rounds already on the clock; topped up to the pack's fight length, e.g. "rounded up to 1 turn"), foes by outcome (dead, fled → Bestiary "Fled earlier", surrendered, **captured → a Captives list on the GM console** until released), **XP waiting** from the foes the pack counts (defeated, fled, surrendered; held for M7 awards, adjustable there), **loot** (stat block treasure lines as reminders; + Add loot opens 05's loot picker; **Send loot to the party** or **Done without loot**; optional log line). Players get 05's **Loot to share** strip (Claim / Put in stash / Leave behind; unclaimed to the stash at session end). Then back to Explore. **We run** (its own button beside End fight): an **Escape** step with the pack's pursuit rule; **Drop food or treasure** (pick items; removed from packs, logged; the pack's chance to stop pursuers shown); **Got away** ends the fight with no loot step and no XP unless the pack says otherwise; **Caught** returns to the fight. **XP for getting past foes:** M6-7's non-fight endings show XP waiting when the pack allows; Rules for this campaign "XP for foes outwitted" (pack default); the GM can switch XP off per encounter. Treasure-type rolling lands with M7. setting-1's pursuit and evasion rules aren't in the private pack yet: to add from Francis's book (page references, our own words). | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-13 | **Monster behaviours format.** *Stat block fields (all optional):* `profile` {wants, appearance, voice, intelligence}; `nudges` [{text, when: start / winning / losing / belowHalf / alone / leaderDown / any}] (read by M6-2a); `abilities` [{id, name, text, uses?: {count, per: day / fight / round}, trigger?}]; `triggers` [{event: firstSight / death / belowFraction (fraction) / eachRound / round (n) / firstWound, text}] (▲ reminders, never automatic outcomes); `actionMenu` {pick, options: [abilityIds]} (reference under More); `tables` {tactics?, signs?, lair?, other: [ids]}. *New kind `modifications`:* {id, name, statChanges: {hd?, ac?, morale?, hp?, …}, adds: {abilities?, nudges?}, notes} (chosen in Add foes). *New kind `overlays`:* {id, name, keepNumbers: true (default) / false, links: [{packId?, statBlockId, behaviourId}], behaviours: [{id, name, profile, nudges, abilities, triggers, actionMenu, tables, numbers?}]}; explicit links first; otherwise the app suggests a name match and the GM confirms once per campaign (remembered); nothing links silently. Every field optional; a plain stat block still works. Book text only in private packs (own words, 8-word check). Tests: the pack checker validates the new kinds; a private overlay-1 (stage 1) on the setting-1 pack plus tagged setting-2 stat blocks; Francis's runnable checks in `docs/gates/m6/m6-gate-tests.md`. | 2026-10-26 |
| M6-13 (change) | The action menu is reference text under "More", not something the GM sets. Packs may tag behaviour lines with when they apply. | 2026-10-26 |
