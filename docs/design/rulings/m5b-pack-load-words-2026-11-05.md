# The sheet's Pack load: its words, and tiny items' weight (design ruling, 2026-11-05)

Two questions from the sheet plan's build order 5 (#479; SC-5-1 and the builder's wording departure). Ruled by Francis (yes to both). Ids `pl-1`, `pl-2`.

- **pl-1 One set of words for the load.** Counts say **items** on the sheet as on the Pack tab ("9 items", never "things"); the two slot bands are **Equipped** and **Stowed** (06's words list), on the sheet and the Pack tab alike, unless the pack names its own bands; **Tiny things** stays as that group's name. The sheet's groups fold in place with ▸ / ▾ (› is kept for going somewhere).
  - *Why:* one word, one meaning across two screens that show the same load.
- **pl-2 A tiny item's weight is as built.** With weight (coin) encumbrance a tiny (`unencumbering`) item counts its own weight; with slot encumbrance it takes no slot; the Tiny things group says "don't count" only where that is true. No change to the Pack tab's numbers.
  - *Why:* that is what the settings' books say in each mode (the pack session's reading, 2026-11-05); "tiny counts nothing everywhere" would go against the weight rule for nearly every tiny item in one setting. Left out on purpose: the books' optional "all miscellaneous gear counts as one flat amount" (a separate option if ever wanted), and the slot rule's "unless carried in large numbers", which stays the GM's call.

**Also corrected here (designer):** the sheet mockup (`design/mockups/sheet/02-sheet-extras.html`) and 06's Pack summary example drew slot counts and speeds that matched a setting's real figures; both now use invented ones. The mockup's PNG still shows the old figures until it is next redrawn.

**Still open, for Francis:** when the Pack tab's folded Tiny section (06, Pack summary) is built (SC-5-2).
