## Settled by the studio, for the driver to confirm

- **The go-there button's words: "Go to Waiting for you"**, plain, before "Got it" (which stays last, plain, tfb-2). "Go to" is the app's verb for a button that moves you ("Go to rows", "Go to number"); "Waiting for you" is the section's own name. Plain, not the accent: the pop-up is read, and the button only moves the GM. It opens Waiting for you at the request where there is one; from "Sending a request" (nothing sent yet) it opens Waiting for you.
- **How the name shortens at 375 px:** the band's first line is cut at its end with "…" (read whole by a screen reader), as the bar's title is. With a very long player's name the character's name is lost from the band; the bar's title above still starts with it.
- **The GM's line and "Reveal…" show only on an item with something hidden;** an item with nothing hidden shows neither. "Reveal…" sits above the player's own rows (Give to…, Put in…, Drop) and opens the Reveal pop-up of 05 (not built yet; not drawn).
