# Fewer links: buttons for actions, and the Watches step (design ruling, 2026-11-03)

Francis's feedback on the wilderness screens (#364's gallery): "I want fewer links"; "View and Dismiss should be buttons"; "I don't love the watch setup". Mockup `design/mockups/m5-gate/b19-fewer-links.html` (pictures `m5-gate/png/b19-*`). Ids `fl-1` … `fl-4`.

- **fl-1 (A) Actions are buttons; links only go somewhere — everywhere.** The one thing to do now: the accent button (one per screen). Any other action: a secondary button, with a **small** size for low-priority actions in a card's foot (44 px still). A choice among a few: chips or segments in the Selected wash. Opening a thing: the whole card or row is the tap, with ›. **Underlined text only for going somewhere** (the back link, a link inside a sentence) and help lines (Help me choose). The quiet underlined button style is retired for actions; a destructive action is a button with the danger word.
- **fl-2 (A) The Encounter waiting card:** **Start** is the accent when no other accent shows on the console (else secondary), **View** and **Dismiss** small buttons, Which watch? as chips ("1st · Wren, Osric"). Dismiss keeps its Undo toast.
- **fl-3 (A + B) The Watches step, by watch:** one box per watch (its number, and the time of night where the pack gives it) with the names on it as chips (✕ takes one off) and **+ Add** listing who's free (hidden at 3, the limit); "Not on watch: …" and the casters line under the boxes. **Split evenly** fills the watches in marching order (casters kept off the last watch where it can), then the group adjusts; a change is logged as today. Replaces SC-4-2's chips per character.
- **fl-4 #364's Open card is fine** under fl-1 (the whole card is the tap, with ›); #364 goes ahead.
