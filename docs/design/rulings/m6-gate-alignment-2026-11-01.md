# M6 after the M5 gate: what the fight screens inherit (ruled 2026-11-01)

**Francis ruled 2026-11-01: yes to all five; Distance stays a number field (no chips, no skip).**

The M6 rulings (`m6-rulings.md`, absorbed) were made before the M5 gate. The gate's rulings reach into the fight screens in five places. The mockup `../mockups/m6/m6-fight-screens.html` draws the fight screens with them applied (light only, full size). None of these changes a fight rule; they are the gate's consequences, listed for Francis to confirm in one go.

| # | Inherited from | What changes in M6 | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **B2** encounter tables | The fight's Controls column shows the **Encounter tables ›** row instead of "current table · Roll"; tables open full screen; **no typed roll anywhere**, so M6-10's "type the roll to light the row" goes (Go to number stays for long tables). Add foes' Encounters section is B2's list and its tables open B2's screen, the tapped row coming back with the monster and its dice. | 05 Combat tracker (Add foes), 06 Fight layouts, 06 GM tables (M6-10) |
| 2 | **B18** players' table view | The players' fight Scene is the table view with the time band reading "Fight · Round N · whose turn", the three columns **Foes · Party · You** in the marching-order band's place, the place folded to its name, and prompts (Down with Helped, a message, We run called) in B18's slot. Phone: one column, same order, turn controls in the sticky bar (as ruled). | 06 Fight layouts (Player tablet, Player phone) |
| 3 | **B3, B4** top row | The GM's fight header carries ✉ and the open-book Reference beside Log; Fiat stays the hat. | 06 Fight layouts (GM tablet header) |
| 4 | **B6** statuses | A foe card's **+ Status** opens the same widened pop-up (durations on one row, effect under each name, families from the pack); typed statuses are custom statuses with What it does. | 05 Foe cards: + Status and Note |
| 5 | **C3** weapons at hand | The You column shows each weapon's bonus and damage, and the attack line "d20 +1 · hits AC 6 on 13+" against the tapped foe (M6-9's prompt with the numbers already there). | 06 Fight layouts (You column) |

Kept as ruled, untouched: the round and turn controls, side and individual initiative, Delay, the HP bar, the damage pad, Down and the death panel (no pop-up), morale from the pack only, nudges, the GM mirror, the combatant panel, several fights, End fight and the loot strip.

**Distance** in the encounter panel keeps a number field: it is a number the GM reads off the dice, not a table row (B2 removed typed *rolls*, not typed *numbers*). Say so if you'd rather it were a table.
