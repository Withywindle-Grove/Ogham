# Rulings on the open design prompts, 2026-10-31 (Francis ruled)

The designer session of 2026-10-31 went through the three prompts the build agents left (groups and settlement; M5b; the agents' 26 choices), one ruling at a time. Neutral names only; the prompts themselves are private.

## Ruled one at a time
| # | Question | Ruling | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q6 | One shared "player asks, GM answers" pattern? | **Yes: one `Request` record and one card.** Who, what (text and the cost as text), a kind, a status (waiting · answered · withdrawn), the answer. The GM answers from the existing **Waiting list** (extend it, never a second copy). Answer kinds are a closed set: Yes / No; Hit–Miss then a choice; Offer → the player Accepts or Declines (selling is the only two-turn one). While waiting the player sees "Sent to the GM · Cancel" on Scene; the result pops up on Scene and goes to the log; Undo on the GM's answer, no confirms. **Escape rolls are the GM-started variant:** Leave the dungeon makes one request per character (or one for the party), players fill in the roll, the GM previews and Sends | One component for secret activities, Describe…, buying, selling, lodging and escape rolls; M7's Ask the GM and M8's inbox reuse it |
| Q3 | Something else… vs Things you can do | Already ruled (05 §Something else…, 06 Components): one pop-up in every mode; the town uses the same one. **Added:** in the dungeon, Describe what you want to do still works, as a Request the GM answers with a cost in turns | Turn modes have no activity buttons (DG12), but players still need a way to ask |
| A | 31 choices the agents made where the rulings were silent, all built and harmless (30i 1, 2, 4–8; 31k 1–4, 6, 8, 10–23, 25, 26; 30j 5) | **Confirmed as built** | A "yes" costs nothing; the list is in 09 |
| C | The four settlement questions (Back in safety by whom; Out and about by whom; the settlement pack fields; Start downtime vs End expedition) | **Deferred** to the downtime design session already planned before M7; the settlement view's build leaves M5b with them | M5b's risk was rated high; the questions are downtime questions |
| B1 | Join up: a visible quiet button (built) or in the switcher's ⋯ (docs)? | **The button.** Once places per group exist it shows only when two groups share a place | Rejoining is the one action the switcher exists for; ⋯ hides it |
| B2 | Wilderness budget, forced march, Lost and camp: per group or per party? | **Per group.** One clock for all stays (DG14) | Each group already has its own place, order and lights |
| B3 | Getting lost default | **Secret** | Keeps D7's behaviour; Open is the opt-in |
| B4 | "Weather rolled by" setting's home | **Adventuring** (the code moves; the docs already said so) | With its siblings Getting lost and Show hex numbers |
| B5 | Getting lost Open: who taps Found the way / Still lost? | **The GM only.** Players may enter the recovery roll | One rule for every roll: players enter, the GM applies (as DG18) |
| B6 | The darkness dim and the contrast rule | **Dim the background only;** text and controls keep full contrast; 4 seconds and the "No light" reminder stay | A lighter overlay either fails contrast or stops reading as dark |
