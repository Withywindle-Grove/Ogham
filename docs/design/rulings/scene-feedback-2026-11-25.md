## How to read the table

- **Track:** *ruled* = his own words settle it. *drawn* = a proposal with no open question, redrawn, and approved with its frame on 26 November. *open* = waited for his pick (none is left). *bug* = a fault; a builder fixes it. *pack* = work for the pack tools. *later* = after 5 December.
- **Size:** S = one small row. M = one row with its tests. L = two or three rows.
- **sf-N** is the proposal's number. **⚑ N** is a question he ruled on 26 November (the table above).
- **A ruling in words is not an approval of a screen.** The frames of page 44 were approved on 26 November ("Approve with picks"); the parts redrawn after it, to his picks, are listed under the approval.
