## A. Known items
1. Items listed in a starting kit count as known (kits are player-readable), so they can show at creation.
2. Yes, leave revealed_facts until unidentified items are built.
3. Items become known when the GM sends them as loot (M6), and through shops and "Show" with Handouts (M8b).

5. Correct. An item is known when a party member holds it, it's in the Party stash, it's in a kit, or the GM sends or shows it. Items on monsters or at places stay unknown until looted or shown.
