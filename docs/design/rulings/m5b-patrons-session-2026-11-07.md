# Patrons: the paper dry run's rulings (2026-11-07, evening)

The walk is in `docs/design/notes/patrons-dry-run-notes.md`. Francis approved all eight at once. Build and test from the zine first; the daemonology add-on converts all at once when its own session has settled the rest.

**The option**
- **pt-1.** One switch, "Expanded cleric creation", off by default, shared by every attached pack that carries patrons (one ruleKey); patrons come only from attached packs. Its row explains the rule and how it is applied ("Clerics, and other classes the setting names, choose a patron; the patron sets what they wear, wield and cast").
- **pt-2.** Every class the pack flags carries the hint on its row and in its entry ("With Expanded cleric creation: chooses a patron").

**The step**
- **pt-3.** Patron is the first section of Who they are, before alignment, built like the liege (cw-5): pick rows (name, blurb, the alignments it allows), the entry on a tap (depiction, followers, holy symbol, garb by tier, strictures, prayer, weapons, spells, mark, source), Pick at its foot. Pick sets the alignment when the patron allows one (toast with Undo) or leaves only the allowed rows open. A mismatched intent is no exception: the patron rules.
- **pt-4.** A patron belongs to one pack; the list is that pack's, grouped by pack only when two packs carry patrons. The entry shows the whole ladder of tiers.

**What it applies**
- **pt-5.** Spells: the patron's swaps are applied inline on the class list, each swapped spell marked "from <patron>"; a note-only patron (no swap) shows its note as a line under the list.
- **pt-6.** Gear: the tier-0 garb is given free at creation, marked by source; weapons and gear the patron forbids carry the sign and stay buyable (an exception on the GM's card); a kit's rolled weapon the patron forbids is kept and flagged.
- **pt-7.** The patron fills the Affiliation field (prefilled, editable), as the liege does; the structured patron link stays underneath for the rules and the sheet's identity block.
- **pt-8.** At each level-up, the tier that comes online (garb, marks, abilities, effects) is listed in the preview and applied; garb is free at every tier. In play, equipping what the patron forbids raises the equip warning with the patron's reason, logged; the attack prompt adds the penalty with its source.

**Not walked, already covered:** Ask the GM path; the GM's card (exceptions first); visibility (a lord's stat block, plane and cult stay GM-only; the patron's follower page is player-readable).

**Pack tasks:** the zine's gods as `patrons` entries with tiers; the option row's text; the class hints. The add-on later, all at once.
