# M7 rulings (design session, started 2026-10-28)

The plan is in `m7-design-plan.md`. M7 is split: **M7** covers XP, level-up, spells, creation revisit and dots; **M7b** covers money. Each ruling below goes into 05 and 06 with the M7 docs prompt.

| # | Ruling | Date |
|---|---|---|
| M7-1 | **Award XP.** XP collects in one **XP waiting** pot on the GM console, fed by fights (M6-11), foes the party got past, Treasure XP from Split Loot (M7b) and bonus XP. Each line can be edited or removed. **No forced moment:** End expedition and End session each show one quiet line ("445 XP waiting · Award now"); the GM can award at any time. **Award XP sheet:** the waiting lines; "+ Bonus XP…" with a reason; **Who shares** as chips (defaults to characters on the expedition; retainers get the pack's share, here ½; dead characters are left out until tapped back in); then the **shares**. **Pack bonuses** (prime requisite and any other bonus a pack defines) are added per character, each named with its source; tapping a share shows the sum ("127 + 10% prime = 140"), using the shared "where this number comes from" pattern. The total under the button explains itself ("445 waiting + 19 from bonuses"). **Rounding:** shares round to the nearest whole number and half shares round up, unless the pack says otherwise. **One-level cap:** Cap / Keep appears only on a character who would cross it (05). With nothing waiting: "Nothing waiting" plus "+ Bonus XP…". The single accent button "Award N XP" logs one undoable step. Players get a quiet "+N XP" toast and "▲ Ready to level" where it applies. | 2026-10-28 |
| M7-1 (build note) | The mockup's ✎ glyph was unreadable in light mode. In the app, icons come from the symbol family (`src/ui/icons.tsx`) in the ink token, never emoji or font glyphs, and each icon-only button has an accessible name. | 2026-10-28 |
| M7-2 | **Level up.** The approved R1 preview stays (HP roll tiles, What changes, Use / Keep for edited values, "I'll enter it myself"). **Additions:** new abilities show "Rules ›" (one tap to the rule); **Learn a spell · Choose ›** when the pack gives new spells at that level (the same picker as creation, M7-4); **HP without a roll** when the pack gives a fixed gain (tiles hidden, the reason stated). **When a character can level:** pack setting with a campaign override, **Downtime only** (default) or **Any time**. "▲ Ready to level" always shows. In Downtime-only campaigns, tapping it during an adventure offers **Ask the GM**: the request lands in the GM's "Waiting for you" list (inbox from M8); **Allow now** opens the preview for the player, and **Not yet** says so quietly. Both are logged. **HP rolls:** tiles up to d10; d12 and bigger use the shared number stepper (typing allowed). **Several levels pending** run as one preview after another. **No nagging:** the badge is the only reminder. Confirm before the roll stays tappable and says "Tap your roll first" (no disabled button). **G-04 closed:** the CS-1 `traitLists` format goes into 07, and the pack checker learns the kind. | 2026-10-28 |
| Mockup note | Accent-button text and the chosen Use/Keep button were hard to read in the light mockups. The chat's own button styling overrode the mockup colours: the app's tokens give about 10:1 (#FBF8F1 on #6E2A1F). Future mockups draw buttons as plain blocks so the real colours show, and the gallery's contrast test (axe) keeps checking the app. | 2026-10-28 |
| M7-3 | **Spells.** The Oct 26 player-screen ruling supersedes the SP slot header: levels read "Level 1 · 2 a day", with no count left; a cast spell is crossed out (line and muted text) and moves to the bottom of its level. **Rules one tap away everywhere:** tapping a spell's name (anywhere on its row except the Cast button) opens its description and rules: what it does, how it works at the table, worked-out duration and range for this caster, reversed form, source. This applies in the Spells section, Prepare, the spell picker, Scrolls and the level-up preview. **Cast:** spells with no duration and no choices cast at once, crossed out, with an "<spell> cast · Undo" toast; spells that need something (reversed form, targets, a timer, a light or condition) open one sheet, then cast. Every cast is logged. **Prepare** ("Prepare ›" in the section header): **Same as yesterday** is one tap and the default; in the slot grid, tapping a number adds one and holding clears it; changes save as made. Book casters see their book; divine casters see their whole class list (×N allowed). **Getting spells back:** the pack sets `spellcasting.recover` (full rest / rest plus study time / prayer); the GM's full rest and End expedition ask once, "Casters get their spells back · <names>" (on by default, logged); each caster then sees a quiet "Prepare spells ›". **Lost when hit:** where the pack says so, the GM's hit prompt marks the spell being cast as **Lost** (crossed out, "lost"), undoable. **Scrolls:** **Read** uses the scroll up (logged, Undo); a scroll the class can't use stays tappable and explains why (the scroll-type warning; never blocks); arcane scrolls offer **Copy into book…**, showing the pack's copy rule as text, with the player tapping **Copied** or **Failed** after rolling at the table. The scroll is used up either way, unless the pack says otherwise. **Spell book** is a list on the character in R1. ◈ A spell book as a losable item is left open. | 2026-10-28 |
| M7-4 | **Character creation revisit: guided.** Creation is the most guided place in the app. **Every step** opens with one short line on what it is and why it matters (the pack's `creationGuide`, else the app's text). **Every choice** (spell, ability, class, race, kit, background) shows a one-line summary on its row (the pack's `blurb`, the author's own words) and opens its full rules on tapping the name. **Help me choose** (a quiet link) expands the pack's guidance for the step. **Steps** (each only when the pack defines it): method, scores, race, class, who they are, **first spells** (book casters; choose N, given spells, or roll and tap), **gear** (kit, or **Shop** = Add item in creation mode, "Gold to spend: N gp" at the top, each buy deducted at once with Undo, then Done shopping), **languages**, **weapon skills**, **background and other trait lists**. The last three offer **Decide later**, which sends them to a **To finish** card on the sheet ("You can play now. Finish these any time.") that disappears when done. The GM can approve with To-finish items open. A pack can make any step required (`creation.require`). The step counter counts only this character's steps. **Time target:** the M7 gate is "a guided fighter and magic-user in about 5–6 minutes each"; the M4b gate stays as written. | 2026-10-28 |
| M7-4 (blurbs) | One-line summaries say what an entry does in play, about 8 to 12 words, 14 px under the name (rows 56 px). Without a `blurb`, the row shows duration and range instead. **Pack task:** the converter writes blurbs for every spell and ability in setting-1 and setting-2 (own words, 8-word copy check) as part of the M7 pack work. | 2026-10-28 |
| M7-5 | **Ability-use dots** (the section and dots exist from the Oct 26 player-screen ruling). Getting uses back: each ability's `uses.per` is day, rest, fight or week. Rest and day uses return with the GM's single rest prompt ("Casters get their spells back, and abilities refresh · <names>"); fight uses at End fight; week uses when downtime passes a week. The GM can refresh any ability from the character's ⋯ (logged). The name opens the rules. **Factor hooks** switch on; their first use is the per-character XP bonuses in M7-1. | 2026-10-28 |
| M7b-1 | **Money group (M7b).** The R1 boards (Split Loot, Treasury, Charter) stand, with these changes. **(1) Before M8:** Split Loot and Treasury are run by the GM; any player can open Split Loot and send it to the GM as a draft. After the founding version, charter changes are proposals that land in the GM's "Waiting for you" list; the GM applies them (logged "proposed by <player>, applied by the GM"). M8 switches on the Treasurer title and unanimous votes with no rework, because commands already record who proposed what. **(2) Treasure XP**, only when the setting gives XP for treasure (pack default; campaign option "Treasure gives XP" to override), becomes a "Treasure · N" line in the XP waiting pot (M7-1); otherwise no Treasure XP step appears. **(3)** "What did you bring back?" fills itself from coins and gems in the Party stash and loot claimed this expedition; every line is editable; "+ Add a valuable" stays. **(4)** Split Loot keeps one confirm (it changes every purse), then it's one undoable log step: whoever ran it or the GM can undo, if nothing changed since. **(5) Purse sheet:** tapping the coin line on the player screen opens your purse: each denomination, carried versus each stored place with Move (free in downtime; on an adventure it needs the GM's OK), your Due list and recent entries. It reuses the Treasury rows. Other players never see it. Unchanged: fund percentage and exceptions, remainder to the fund, equal shares with companies off, recurring Due, GM-approved places, stored amounts private. | 2026-10-28 |
| M7-9 | **Export and recap.** Confirmed as ruled in M5: Export adventure (Markdown, GM and player versions, grouped by day or turn, routine lines counted) from the End expedition summary and the log ⋯; the Archivist's recap draft (GM when there's no Archivist), edited and published as an Archivist note; the outside "Nemeton recap" skill. **New:** **Download a copy** from a character's ⋯ at any time. **Restore from a file** (⋯ → Your characters) arrives as a waiting character for GM approval. Restore lists anything that doesn't match this campaign's setting rules (class, items, spells); unmatched entries are kept as plain text on the sheet, and the GM sees the same list before approving. **File format:** new files use `tabletop-character` version 1 (no product name, like `tabletop-pack`); restore also accepts the old `grimoire-character`. GM-only fields are never in a player's download. | 2026-10-28 |
| M7-4 (re-test additions, 2026-10-28) | From the M4b re-test, for the M7 creation steps: **N9** no Later button in guided creation (Back on the first step leaves; per-step "Decide later" stays); supersedes the 2026-10-22 Later ruling. **N10** every mandatory field (alignment included) is filled before going on; Next stays tappable and says what's missing. **N15** HP is asked whenever the setting's sheet includes it (class die and modifier, or the setting's own HP rule). **N16 / N2 / N11** the scores step enforces the setting's rules (trades, minimums); overriding happens only on the final check step, which asks "Why? Your GM sees this." **N5** each alignment choice shows the pack's one-line explanation (none shown without one). **N6 / N14** secondary skill and literacy are explained from the setting, and hidden when it gives nothing. **N13** languages offer the setting's list plus "Other…". **N23** creation uses long names with the setting's short description. **N4** creation never asks for values the setting works out (movement, weapon proficiency counts, AC). **N8** Back and Next share one row. **N24** with no max HP, show the number plus a "Set max" link, not a bar. **N29** the Prepare sheet layout follows M7-3. | 2026-10-28 |
