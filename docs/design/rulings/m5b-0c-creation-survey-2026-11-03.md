# Creation, the survey's results for both settings (design ruling, 2026-11-03)

From the pack session's surveys of the books' creation chapters against the packs and the app: `design/notes/fae-woodland-creation-survey-2026-11-03.md` (#331) and `design/notes/classic-advanced-creation-survey-2026-11-03.md` (#332); gaps G-81 … G-94. Ruled one at a time with Francis, each with A — Builder, B — Critic and a synthesis. Mockup: `design/mockups/creation/11-details-step.html`. Ids `cs-1` … `cs-7`.

- **cs-1 (A) Per-kindred creation tables and an optional Details step (G-81).** A race may carry `tables: { names?, trinket?, details?: [tableIds], growth? }`. The **name table** opens from the Name field ("Roll a name · <kindred> ›"; its columns, first name and surname or by gender, each tapped). The **trinket** is rolled in Gear with the kit rolls and becomes an item. The **detail tables**, plus age, height and weight (the race's dice shown, the player types), sit on one optional **Details** step before Review, skipped in one tap ("Skip · leave blank"), never blocking Send, all editable later from the sheet. A race's `growth` (rolled at Level 1, and at later levels in M7) shows on the Details step for that race only. The app never rolls: each table opens, the player rolls at the table and taps the row.
- **cs-2 (A) Kindred grants at creation, added to the class's (G-82).** A race may carry `firstSpells` (the class shape: `rollTable` or `choose`) and `startingItems` as item ids. They are **added to** the class's, each labelled with its source ("From your kindred · roll d20 · 1 glamour"; "From your class · …"; the item in Gear's Given group "from your kindred"). The step appears whenever anything is granted (an elf fighter gets it) and is named **Magic** when it holds glamours, runes or knacks. The kindred-classes carry `spellcasting` and `firstSpells` as data, rolled the same way.
- **cs-3 (A) Class limits in one place, hard or soft (G-84, G-89).** The one place is the race's class limits (`classMaxLevels`, with pg-2's `raceMaxLevels` from a class); each entry may be `{ maxLevel, soft: true }`. **Hard**: under "Not open to …", Next explains. **Soft**: pickable, flagged "Needs the GM's special approval" (ca-3) on the review and the GM's card. A class's `alignments` limit what Who they are offers (hard: explained, not offered; soft: special approval). fae-woodland's `classes[].kindreds`, `races[].forbiddenClasses` and `creationRestrictions` fold into the one place. **The knight's liege**: a class-gated field (`fields[].forClasses`) from a table whose rows carry `alignment`; picking a house sets the knight's alignment, with a line saying why.
- **cs-4 (A) A kindred-class bound to its kindred (G-91).** Picking a kindred-class (`classes[].race`, `replacesRaceTraits`) sets the kindred (languages, name, detail and trinket tables follow it) and replaces the kindred's traits with the class's. On the ordinary path the Class step lists it under its own kindred only, with the line "replaces your kindred's traits"; under the kindred-as-class method the Kindred step is skipped. Changing the class away from a kindred-class reopens the Kindred step (as cf-7 clears hit points).
- **cs-5 (A) Race fields switched by an option (G-94).** A race may carry `byOption` (the shape kits and rules sets use): under an option's choice, the fields that change (`npcOnlyClasses`, `classMaxLevels`, `abilities`). Creation and level-up read the race after the campaign's choices. `npcOnlyClasses` moves under the choice that turns it on, so the option's default is honoured.
- **cs-6 (A) Six smaller pieces.** G-83 `startingEquipment.pick { tableId, upTo }` (with ct3-6's pick-N, fewer allowed); G-88 kit rows `ifSize` swaps, races' `armourSubstitutes` read on kit rows, `oneOf` items, races' `weaponsAllowed` read beside the class's; G-90 `languages.list[].roll` and `learnableBy` (creature types; "with the GM's leave" = special approval); G-92 a line's `units` beside `qty`, `startingEquipment` lines as `{ itemId, qty }`; G-93 an overlay's `languages.list` merged into its base's. **M5b** (one creation follow-up). G-87 `spells[].usage { per, times? }` and the runes' level-band form: **M7**, with G-63, for the sheet's pips.
- **cs-7 (A) The bonuses now, the traits box at the sheet review (G-85).** In M5b the race's and class's AC bonuses (with their conditions, counted only when true, shown in the AC breakdown, ct3-9) and save bonuses (including classic-advanced's CON-based one) count; the XP bonus goes into Award XP (M7); retainer loyalty with hirelings (M9). A **traits section exists** on the full sheet (every kindred and class trait, each opening its entry, ct3-11, use pips where it has uses); its layout is ruled at the full sheet review.

**Also settled:** ct3-3 (Affiliation is a sheet field earned in play; its `help` read by the ⓘ); `fields[].help` and `fields[].forClasses` named in 07. G-81 … G-94 ruled; G-87 and the traits box's layout are later.
