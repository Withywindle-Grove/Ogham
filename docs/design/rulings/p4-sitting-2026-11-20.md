# P4, the players' place card, and the open ⚑ (2026-11-20)

A short design sitting (Driver 32's brief). Scene P4 stopped before code: its approved frames (page 18, PL1; page 24, PL1b to PL1d; 09-PR1) need data no players' read sends on main. A builder's cut with a stand-in heading "This place" was refused (no interim placements). Francis ruled P4 one question at a time, then the open ⚑ from Drivers 30 and 31 in four batches, on his phone. Each ⚑ was looked up in its pull request's own text before it was asked.

## Part 1: P4

| ID | Ruling | Francis said |
|---|---|---|
| p4s-1 | **What the players' read sends.** For places the group already knows, and only those, a player's device receives: the Location's number from the pack ("1. The Jetty"); the trail above it, Region > Area, each name only if it has been shown to the group (an unshown name is left out of the trail, not replaced); and for the right card, the Area's name with the other Locations of that Area the group knows, each with its number. A Location never shown to the group sends nothing: no name, no number, not that it exists. **Accepted leak:** numbers can show gaps (knowing 1 and 3 hints a 2), as a revealed printed map does. One add-only migration replacing the players' reads (`scene_pieces`, `known_places_campaign` or a new read beside them, the builder's choice), through a database session and his "push". No mockup change: PL1 and PL1b stand as approved. | "Yes, send them" |
| p4s-2 | **"What everyone knows" is the Area's knowledge, shared section by section** (mc-35; page 01, frame 7b, approved 2026-11-12), not today's What we know. Built for 5 December, in the same database change as p4s-1: the eye on each section of the GM's Knowledge sheet (frame 7b), a record of which sections are shared (and at which turn), and on the players' card one row per shared section, titled by the section's own heading with "Shared, turn N." under it (PL1); a tap opens the section's full words. No section shared: no row. Sharing logs and undoes like a piece's eye. | "Yes, build it for the 5th" |
| p4s-3 | **Today's What we know card leaves the players' Scene once P4 and the players' map (P5) have both landed**, so a revealed map always has a place. Until then it stays where P1 put it. The places of earlier expeditions are not shown meanwhile, until the location browser (M8b); on 5 December there are none. Answers the players' plan's ⚑ 10 for What we know only (Split party, the Reference lines and Recently stay open there). | "Yes, take it off then" |
| p4s-4 | **A tap on a known Location on the right card** ("2. The Wreck ›") opens the standard sheet titled with that Location's name, holding its shown pieces in the card's book format, nothing else. Built from approved parts (the card's pieces, the standard sheet); **no frame is drawn**: Francis approves it from pictures of the real screen, as p2p-flag3 did for the prompts. | "Yes, approve it from the real screen" |

**Hidden data (P14, hard rule 5).** Each of p4s-1, p4s-2 and p4s-4 sends a player only what the group has been shown: an unshown Location, an unshared knowledge section and an unshown piece are in nothing a player receives, enforced in the read itself (row-level security or a security-definer function scoped to the group), with a database test for each. The knowledge sections' text reaches a player's device only once shared.
