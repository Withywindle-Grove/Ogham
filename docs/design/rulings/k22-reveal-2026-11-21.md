# K22: what a player notices, the GM's edit and the Reveal pop-up, Francis's answers (2026-11-21)

Francis answered K22's open questions at a sitting with the driver on 21 November (the eight questions of the ruled-so-far sheet for K22, compiled from `main` at 5843513). The answers below are as the driver relayed them; only the words in quotes are his own. Page 33 is drawn to them and **awaits his approval from pictures**: nothing here approves a screen.

## The rulings

| Id | Question | Ruling | His words |
|---|---|---|---|
| k22-0 | Scope (⚑ 41 of `plans/test-feedback-2026-11-19.md`) | **All of K22 comes before the GM's view (C15) goes to `main`** | "K22 first", then "All of K22" |
| k22-1 | Where the GM edits "What you notice" (`noticed`) and what an item appears as (`appearsAs`) | **From a row on his item sheet, any time after the item is handed out.** Not in Add item's form. One place for every way an item arrives (Add item, the stash, a hoard, a gift) | relayed |
| k22-2 | Handing an item out already identified | **Hand it out, then Reveal… and "Tell them everything".** No new switch | relayed |
| k22-3 | The two fields | **Both start from the setting's words; either may be empty.** An empty "appears as" falls back to the category's singular name; an empty "What you notice" shows no line. **No warning on his words** | relayed |
| k22-4 | What the Reveal pop-up lists | **Only what is hidden on this item, one row per hidden fact** (true name, bonus, powers, that it's cursed, charges; duplicates merged). **"That it's magic" only on a magic item;** told alone, the player sees that it is magic and nothing more. **GM notes are never offered** | relayed |
| k22-5 | A tap on a row | **It tells that fact at once; its row then reads as told; the pop-up stays open.** "Tell them everything" at the foot is the pop-up's one accent | relayed |
| k22-6 | Undo | **Each tell happens at once with an Undo toast; Undo hides it again; both are logged** | relayed |
| k22-7 | The player's side | **"What you notice" stays on the player's sheet until the true name is told** (as HM4); **each told fact shows on the player's sheet; the log line names the fact** (his example: "Sam learns the ring is cursed"). **The player gets no pop-up** (05) | relayed |
| k22-8 | The old buttons | **The one "Reveal…" row and its pop-up replace today's per-field "Reveal …" buttons and "Reveal the count"** on the GM's item sheet **everywhere**, in and out of the GM's view, on every item with something hidden | relayed |

**On ⚑ 40.** Earlier on 2026-11-21, for batch G3 only, "Reveal…" was read as opening the GM's item sheet as built. k22-8 replaces that once K22 lands: the row opens the pop-up, and the per-field buttons go.

**On hard rule 4.** Undo of a tell hides a fact again; it deletes nothing, and both acts are logged. No new kind of deletion.

## Needs a ruling (drawn one way on K22h; both touch hidden data, so they are his to decide, not the designer's)

1. **Who reads the log's line for a told fact.** Drawn: the holder and the party; the true name, every player (sw-8). The other way: the holder alone.
2. **The players' log after Undo.** Drawn: the players' line goes, and the GM's log keeps both acts. The other way: the line stays, with a second line taking it back (a player may already have read it either way).

## Technical (for the builder's plan)

- Undo of a reveal is new: today's `inventory.reveal` copies the value into the line's details and has no Undo; `known_items.revealed_facts` exists and nothing writes it.
- The fact list is read from the setting's hidden fields with duplicates merged; "That it's magic" is a fact of its own.
- A toast with Undo over an open pop-up, on a tablet and above the sheet on a phone, is not built anywhere today; with the sheet at its full height on a phone the toast would lie over the sheet's title.
- The pop-up opens from inside the item's sheet: its own form and a layout check (CLAUDE.md, Things Claude gets wrong).
