# A switch for a pack's player content (design ruling, 2026-11-05)

Raised by the pack session for the next add-on to classic-advanced; Francis's direction, written down with him. Id `pc-1`.

**Francis:** "classes switched on by default if the pack is loaded, but for this and other packs that have player content the GM should have the option to switch off the player content (classes for now, but it could be other things as well)."

**The case.** A campaign loads an add-on for its monsters and items but doesn't want its classes on offer (invented: a horror add-on's **Grave Warden** class). Today every loaded pack's classes and kindreds appear at creation.

- **pc-1 (synthesis).**
  1. **One switch per loaded pack that adds classes or kindreds**, in Rules for this campaign at the top of that pack's group, and in campaign set-up: **"Offer this pack's classes and kindreds to players"**, **On** by default. The words name exactly what it covers; if spells or items a player picks join it later, the words change with them.
  2. **Off:** that pack's classes and kindreds are not offered at creation (nor at a later Change ›). Everything else in the pack stays: stat blocks, items, tables, reference.
  3. **A character already made from that content keeps it**, shown as it is. To let one player have such a class, the GM switches it on, the character is made, and the GM switches it off again: no exception is built.
  4. **A pack that replaces a core entry by id** (eq-4) is left out of the switch: turning it off would need the original back.
  5. Logged, with Undo, like every rule change. A pack with no classes or kindreds shows no row.
  - *Later, if asked for:* a tick per class.

**Build:** after the M5b gate (`plans/after-m5b-gate.md`); to be sized by the builder. The packs need nothing: a class belongs to its pack.
