# The At hand breakdown's parts (design ruling, 2026-11-05)

One scope check from the small follow-ups PR (#462), ruled with Francis. Id `sq-4a`.

**The check.** sq-4 lists the At hand breakdown's parts as base, ability, magic and specialisation. The At hand attack figure doesn't count magic or specialisation today: no pack field holds a weapon's magic bonus, and specialisation is read only for M6's attack prompt (the builder's findings; not checked by the designer).

- **sq-4a (accepted for the gate).** The breakdown shows the parts the figure is made of today: the class at its level, the score's modifier for the kind of attack, and the damage with its rule; "Open <item> ›" at its foot as ruled. A breakdown never lists a part its figure doesn't count.
- **With M6:** a weapon's magic bonus (a pack field, with the pack session, as a gap row in 11) and specialisation join the figure and its breakdown together, when the attack prompt reads them.
