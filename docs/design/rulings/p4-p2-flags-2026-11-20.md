# The open ⚑ of #1097 (P4) and #1098 (P2) (2026-11-20)

A short design sitting from the driver's brief (row p4-p2-flags). Two cloud builders' pull requests on the players' Scene stopped on ⚑ that are Francis's: #1097 (P4, the players' place card, the Area's card, the GM's eye on Knowledge; ⚑ 1 to 6 in its section 5) and #1098 (P2, a player's Where to?; ⚑ 1 and 2). Each was put to him one at a time with a recommendation; he took every recommendation. For ⚑ 6 he saw the built cards (branch `scene-p4-place-card`, `layout.html?placeCard=1` and `=messy`) beside PL1b (page 24, approved 2026-11-18).

| ID | ⚑ (where) | Ruling | Francis said |
|---|---|---|---|
| ppf-1 | #1097 ⚑ 1: when an Area's or Region's name counts as shown | **An Area's and a Region's names count as shown to a group as soon as the group has been shown any Location inside them** (not only once the Area itself is shown). Otherwise in point to point, where the party stands only at Locations, the trail and the Area card's heading stay empty all night. An Area or Region with no shown Location inside it still sends nothing. Accepted: an Area's name that is itself a hint shows with its first Location. | "Shown with a Location" |
| ppf-2 | #1097 ⚑ 2: which Knowledge the GM can share | **The eye works on the Area's Knowledge sheet while the GM reads ahead** (frame 7b's sheet; no new screen). The eye is a deliberate act, so it is the one thing that writes while reading ahead; everything else there still writes nothing (B2's "The party isn't here" stays for the rest). A Location's Knowledge button keeps opening that Location's own sections. The Area's sections are not added to a Location's sheet. | "Eye works reading ahead" |
| ppf-3 | #1097 ⚑ 3: a split party | **A shared section goes to the group it was shared for**, as a shown piece does ("that group's players only"), not to the whole party. Kept as built. | "That group only" |
| ppf-4 | #1097 ⚑ 4: the known Locations' rows | **As drawn on PL1b:** each known Location's row on the Area card has a second line, the first piece the group was shown there (usually the description), cut to one line with an ellipsis. Players already have that piece, so nothing new is sent. In the same migration as p4s-1. | "As drawn: first shown piece" |
| ppf-5 | #1097 ⚑ 5: piece order | **The description first, then the other pieces in the order they were first shown**, not the book's order (the book's positions would hint at pieces still hidden). Kept as built. | "Order shown" |
| ppf-6 | #1097 ⚑ 6: the Location's card | **Approved from the real screen with three fixes; it goes on the players' Scene.** (a) "What everyone knows" reads "Shared, turn N." as drawn (the layout page showed "Shared." alone, the words' fallback when no step is known: the builder checks the page passes a step); (b) the known rows' second line (ppf-4); (c) the small picture button at the Location's top right, as PL1b draws it, where the players' read already has the picture; else it waits for its own row and the PR says so. "Where to?" is #1098's part and joins the card there. | "Approve with those fixes" |
| ppf-7 | #1098 ⚑ 1: a row whose far end isn't explored | **Kept as built for 5 December:** the row takes its way's own name ("Walking"; several joined, "Rowing or swimming"; none, "Another way"), with "Not yet explored." **After the 5th**, the Pack Creator adds an optional route name to the pack format, which the read sends and the row then uses (as the frame's "Along the piles"). | "\"Walking\" now, name later" |
| ppf-8 | #1098 ⚑ 2: F3, the line after a move | **As drawn (01-07e, approved 2026-11-12):** "You rowed here from the jetty. Through the breakwater's gap. 4 turns passed: it is turn 18." After a move, a player's device of the group that moved receives the way taken (its `by` word, for the verb) and, where the GM's Yes let the party through an obstacle, the obstacle's name, and nothing else of it: never its note, extra time, chance or approval. The party took that way and crossed it at the table, so nothing new leaks. A hidden link's way is sent too, for the same reason. `place_moves` itself stays the GM's (pt-f3); this goes through `party_moves` or a read beside it, add-only, through a database session and his "push". | "As drawn: way + obstacle" |

**Hidden data (hard rule 5).** ppf-1 sends an Area's or Region's name only to a group shown a Location inside it; ppf-4 sends only a piece the group was shown; ppf-8 sends the way and the obstacle's name only to the group that moved, only for its own moves, never a barrier's note, time, chance or `through` place. Each needs a database test.

## What each builder changes

- **#1097 (P4):** its migration `20261120200000_p4_players_place_card.sql` is not applied anywhere, so it changes in place: `private.known_entry_name` (or its caller) counts an Area or Region as shown once a Location inside it is shown (ppf-1), with a database test; the known Locations' read adds the first shown piece's words (ppf-4), with a test that an unshown piece never comes; the eye's command accepts a share from the Area's Knowledge sheet while reading ahead (ppf-2), and the reading-ahead guard lets that one tap through. The card goes on the players' Scene with ppf-6's fixes. Its review's findings 1 to 7 are unchanged by these rulings.
- **#1098 (P2):** nothing changes for ppf-7. ppf-8 is new work, a migration (the way's `by` and the obstacle's name for the reader's own group's moves; the obstacle passed is recorded with the move if `place_moves` doesn't hold it) and the line's words, through a database session; the driver decides whether it lands in #1098 or its own row before 5 December. Its review's findings 1 to 5 are unchanged.
