# Style direction brief: the app (currently Nemeton)

Status: DRAFT 2026-10-23. Starting point for the style release (⚑ timing: R1.5 or within R2, decided after Table test 1). Nothing here is built yet. Today's look is set by the tokens in `../../06-screens-and-design.md`; they win until the style release changes them.

## 1. The story
The app is a threshold: opening it steps the group from the kitchen table into the other world (the spirit or fae realm, the realm of imagination). The name is the place: nemeton, a sacred grove where people gathered. The voice is the guide: Ogham, an archivist and teacher who speaks from the other side. The main goal still rules: the app pulls people toward each other and the story, never toward the screen; the otherworldly feel is atmosphere, not spectacle.
Tagline: "Under the old oaks." (2026-10-23; replaces "The gathering place.", too close to a well-known card game's name; avoid every form of "gather").

## 2. Mood
Mystical, medieval, primordial, rooted in real history (Gaulish and Irish words, ogham, stone circles, sacred groves). Carved and earthy marks (cut into stone or wood). Twilight, not daylight: light theme = dawn, dark theme = dusk (the "true" view). Quiet strangeness: faint glow around fire and light, a hint of mist at the edges of empty screens, circles echoing fairy rings and stone circles.

## 3. Colour
Base: calm ink and parchment (dawn), deep night and soft ash (dusk). Sky accent gradient "Ember sky": plum 6E3B5E · rose-red C2566A · coral E9845F · amber F2A65A · gold F7D58B (exact values tuned for contrast in the release). Gold is Ogham's colour (his mark, hint signatures, the oak leaf, new log entries). The warm end of the sky is "now" (flame, active tab, main button, Urgent strip edge). Gradients sparingly (flame, loading screen, maybe one header band); flat colour elsewhere. Contrast rules in both themes; meaning never by colour alone.

## 4. Marks (one carved family)
Lighter carving; thin double ring; tapered cuts sparingly. Main mark: "Nemeton" in ogham around a ring, a simple three-lobed oak leaf in the centre (⚑ ring-only version for small icons). Ogham's mark: the notched stem (ogham N), in gold; signs tutorials, hints, emails. Grove and fire: four carved trees around crossed logs, flame in the sky gradient. Etched stem as divider. Before final: check every ogham stroke against a scholarly chart.

## 5. Type
Wordmark always in small capitals (heading serif, slight letter-spacing; ruled 2026-10-23). Keep readability first: book serif headings, highly legible sans body. ⚑ Optional inscriptional display face for the wordmark and loading screen only.

## 6. Texture and motion
Faint stone or parchment grain, barely visible. Loading screen fades in like a memory; otherwise near-still, no animation during play.

## 7. Signature moments
Loading screen: the dictionary entry (nem·e·ton /ˈnɛm.ə.tɒn/ · noun · Gaulish. A sacred place, often a grove, where people gathered.). Empty screens: a line from Ogham ("Nothing carved here yet") with a mark. Emails signed by Ogham with the notched stem.

## 7b. Voice and sayings
Ogham offers a short saying on the loading screen, empty screens, and at session end; never during play; one line. Session sign-off (fixed): "Until the returning night." (from atenoux, the returning night, Coligny calendar).
Historical (attributed honestly): "Honour the gods, do no evil, and practise courage." (druid triad, Diogenes Laertius) · "It's a long road that has no turning." (Irish) · "People live in each other's shelter." (Irish) · "There's no hearth like your own hearth." (Irish) · "The druids would not write their lore; Ogham keeps it for you." (after Caesar).
Table sayings (original): "Every torch counts." · "The ten-foot pole is never wasted." · "Check the ceiling." · "Running away is a plan." · "The dice remember nothing. Ogham remembers everything." · "A closed door is a question." · "Count your rations before you count your gold." · "The map is not the dungeon."
Rules: one line, plain words, no exclamation marks; historical sayings attributed honestly; table sayings original, nothing quoted from published games; the list lives in brand settings. ⚑ Homebrew packs might add sayings later.

## 8. Avoid
Clip-art Celtic knots and triskeles; neon, glitter, glossy chrome; heavy carving that turns busy small; low-contrast text on gradients; any published game's names, art or trade dress; anything animated during play.

## 9. What the style release delivers
1. A palette (sky gradient, dawn and dusk bases, gold), contrast-checked. 2. The final mark family at icon, header and loading sizes. 3. Updated design tokens across all screens. 4. The loading screen and empty states. 5. Before and after screenshots of Player, Pack, Events and GM screens.
