# M5b design sessions: rulings

The design agent's record of M5b design sessions 2 and 4 (approved). The full M5b rulings file is `../rulings/m5b-wilderness-2026-10-30.md`; where they differ, the rulings file and the specs win. Aliases only.

## Session 2 (2026-10-30): the day's checks and weather — approved
1. Weather card: players roll; everyone sees the result and its effects; folds into the icon beside the date after 8 seconds or on tap; reopens from the icon.
2. Two-outcome checks use Hit / Miss, with the instructions and modifiers shown; no number entry.
3. After Hit the table pops up: candidate tables with the suggestion first; rows show their number ranges; the GM taps the rolled row; linked tables chain; long tables get "go to number".
4. Encounter waiting cards queue one per hit with Start, View and Dismiss.
5. Lost card (GM-only): Found the way / Still lost each morning, the pack's chance shown.
6. Special seasons: a switch in the time strip's ⋯; a quiet GM prompt (Hit / Miss) on possible start days.
7. Travelling in darkness: a switch in the time strip's ⋯; a "Sunset around now" hint from the pack's daylight table.

## Session 4 (2026-10-30): the players' wilderness screen and settings — approved
1. Players' wilderness screen: date, weather icon, travel points left, the ground around them (terrain always; a place's name only once shown), the marching strip with "You", the rest prompt when due, one button group (the pack's activities by their own names, Force march with ⓘ, Something else… last) laid out evenly by count (2 columns phone, 3 tablet, odd last centred), then What we know.
2. Something else… opens a pop-up: searchable grouped actions and rules, plus "Describe what you want to do → Send to the GM".
3. Secret activities appear to players as request buttons showing their cost; the GM gets the secret prompt; cost applies when the GM accepts; players see nothing until narrated. Changes D3.
4. What we know pops out into a panel: shown places with descriptions and revealed maps (M8b adds handouts).
5. During camp the screen's middle swaps to the camp steps.
6. Settings (Rules for this campaign → Adventuring): Weather rolled by; Getting lost Secret / Open; Show hex numbers to players; Show each other's HP; the pack's camping options "from the setting". Per device: darkness effect. Special seasons and darkness stay in the GM's time strip ⋯.
7. UI rule (06): pop-ups and pop-out panels over nested collapsible lists; reference lists (rules) stay collapsible with search.
8. Every activity shows and applies its cost, explains its roll, and pops up its table when needed.
