## Rules in force (check each frame against these before you report)
- The current stylesheet and the book's format on every frame, the band included; compare by eye with the pictures of the built place card.
- An icon on every button with its word under it (ic-1); icons medieval or fantasy in nature, from the set or its source, credited.
- A pair of opposite actions is a toggle. No explainer lines. No Close beside an X. The eye and the crossed eye as ruled. The die glyph beside a named roll.
- No disabled button: a blocked action stays tappable and explains why in a pop-up.
- 44 px targets with 8 px between; one accent a screen, and on a player's Scene the accent is the player's own next step if there is one.
- Nothing on a player's frame that P14 says is never sent: no hidden way, no obstacle's note or chance, no unshown place's name, no table.
- Tokens from 06 only.
