The pop-up is wider (a centred card on iPad, full screen on a phone) so the duration buttons sit on one row (it scrolls sideways on a phone, never wraps). Each row shows the status's brief effect from the pack (`notes`, 07). The search field never takes focus by itself (A4). "Until removed" replaces the old "None" (ruled 2026-11-01, C2). A family row (Poisoned · 4 kinds) opens its kinds; a status with ordered levels adds at its first level.

The kinds shown here are invented examples for the mockup only: families and their kinds come only from the pack's own conditions; the app never supplies a list of poisons or anything else (Francis, 2026-11-01). A family is a condition's optional `family` name in the pack (new, G-46): conditions sharing one are grouped behind a row named after it, and the status shows its own name on the card ("Blade poison"). The duration chosen on the first screen carries over; the kind's own `defaultRounds` wins when it has one.

A custom status is a name, a one-line What it does (shown wherever the status shows, exactly like a pack condition's effect) and a duration; Stacks is a pressed toggle. No effect builder: numbers never change by themselves (05, Rest), so a builder would promise what the app doesn't do. A custom status is kept in the campaign's homebrew and offered next time.
