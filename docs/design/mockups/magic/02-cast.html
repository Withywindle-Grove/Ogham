2 · One tap, and what the spell brings

A spell with nothing to choose casts in one tap, with Undo. What it brings starts by itself: here a light joins the party's lights and its timer joins the hour track. A duration rolled at the table doesn't block the player: the GM's console shows "? turns · Set" until the roll is entered.
