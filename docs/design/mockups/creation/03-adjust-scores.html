The same six tiles as the Scores step, now with the rule's buttons instead of free − / +: −2 on the scores the setting lets you lower (A: three named scores; B: any non-prime), +1 on the class's prime requisites, ↶ undoing a trade. Each tile shows its before → after and the modifier's change ("+2 → +1 saves vs magic"), the prime its XP bonus. A tap the rule forbids is never greyed — it stays tappable and answers in a line ("INT can't go below 9"; "STR can't be lowered as a knight"; "a prime can't go above 13 by trading", B); the pale buttons here mark "this setting never trades this score", with the reason under the number. The bank line says what was lowered, what it bought, and what's still to spend. Next checks: unspent points ("2 to spend: raise a prime, or undo the lowering").

3 · When the step doesn't exist

The step appears only when the rules set carries the trade as data (07, below) and this character can use it: a class with a raisable prime and at least one lowerable score above the floor. Otherwise it is skipped and the counter never shows it. A setting whose rule is prose only (both test packs today) has no step until the pack says it in data — the checker warns.

Proposed 07 shape (⚑): abilityAdjustment: { lowerBy: 2, raiseBy: 1, lowerable: ["STR","INT","WIS"] | "nonPrime", raise: "prime", floor: 9, cap?: 13, classExceptions?: [{ classId, cannotLower: ["STR"] }], note, source }

Checked: every rule the step enforces is visible before the tap (which buttons are live, the floor in the why-line, the bank); forbidden taps explain (N9, no disabled buttons); 44 px −2 / +1 / ↶ targets with 8 px between; 375 px as two columns; one accent (Next); never colour alone (ghost buttons carry the reason in words under the number). Not drawn: dark mode; the Back-and-forth case (Back to Class with trades made: the trades are kept while the new class shares the prime, otherwise reset with a toast and Undo — ⚑).
