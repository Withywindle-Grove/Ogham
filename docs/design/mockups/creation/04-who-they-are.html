The step asks only for what nothing else fills: the name, the hit points rolled at the table (the class's die and the CON modifier shown, the pack's minimum applied, the number typed), the alignment as Option rows with the pack's one-line notes, an optional portrait, and any free sheet field the layout leaves (setting B's affiliation). Derived fields are never asked: armour class, movement, attack value, saves, literacy, title, skills — they sit under Worked out for you, read-only, updating as picks change, so the player sees them without being asked for them. Fields other steps own (race, languages, weapon skills, trait lists) don't appear here. Next checks: a missing name or hit points; hp outside the die's range ("d8 gives 1–8").
