4 · Help me choose — about the choice, not the options

✕Choosing a class

The pack's guidance when it has one (creationGuide.class.help). Otherwise the app's own, generic and rules-free:

What this decides. How you fight, what you can learn, how fast you level, what you may wear and carry.

What to weigh. Your best score: a class whose prime requisite matches it earns bonus XP. Your race's level cap. Whether you want spells (a caster starts fragile and grows). What the party lacks.

If you're unsure. A fighter is the simplest to play well. Pick one now: every later step builds on it.
