C · The card is the pick; "Read more" inside

Reed elfGraceful and long-lived; magic comes easily; frail.Needs INT 9 · you have 14 ✓ · p. 36 · Read the full entry ›

No control at all: the card picks, a word in the card says so. Cleanest, least explicit; the "many" case (pick 2 languages) reads worse.

Recommendation: A. It works the same for one-of-many (kindred, class, alignment) and N-of-many (languages, spells, skills: the button reads Pick / Picked ✓ and the foot counts). Pick → Next: on a one-of-many step, Pick (row or entry) moves straight to the next step with a toast "Bog folk picked · Undo"; on N-of-many the step stays until Next. Applies to every pick row: kindred, class, alignment, spell books, kits, languages, skills, trait lists.
