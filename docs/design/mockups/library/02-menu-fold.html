A · The divider is the handle

A short pill (6 × 44) sits on the divider at mid-height, in the Control token; the whole divider is a 44 px-wide tap zone, top to bottom. Tap: fold or unfold. Drag works too (a drag past half folds or unfolds; nothing in between is kept). Nothing in the column itself; no words. Accessible name "Fold the menu" / "Show the menu" on the handle (screen readers only). Cost: a new part (Divider handle), 2–3 turns. Risk: the pill is quiet; a first-time GM finds it by seeing it, as with a sidebar handle on a desktop app — the 06 help line "Tap the line between the menu and the page to fold it" can show once.

No new element: the selected tab carries a quiet ‹ at its end; tapping the tab you're already on folds the column, tapping any icon in the folded column opens that section and unfolds. Cost: 1–2 turns. Risk: a tap on the current tab usually means "go to the top of this section" in apps; and unfolding by tapping a tab changes section as a side effect. Least discoverable of the three.

Also considered

Edge swipe (swipe the column left to fold, swipe from the left edge to unfold): no visible affordance, fights iPad's own edge gestures and the Scene's horizontal panels. Can be added to A as a second way, never alone. Long-press on the column: hidden; no.

Recommendation: A (the divider as handle) — nothing in the column, nothing on the top edge, and it matches what a hand does with a sidebar. B if you want it findable without a help line. Checked: 44 px tap zones in every option; the folded column keeps 48 px icon rows with accessible names; the fold is remembered per device as today; never colour alone (the handle is a shape). Not drawn: dark mode; the phone (no fold there: bottom tabs).
