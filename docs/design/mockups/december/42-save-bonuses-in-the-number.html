What works. In all three, a bonus that always holds simply is the number: the tile, the sheet’s head and its roll line agree, and the player adds nothing. The sheet’s rows keep the book’s “+2” and show the number after it, so the direction of the roll never has to be worked out. The figure stays the loudest thing on the tile in every option.

  1. B can turn a fail into a called pass. The tile shows 11; against anything but venom the number is 13, and nothing on the tile says so. The one thing a save tile must not do is show a number that is wrong for most rolls. Fix: do not pick B. (harden)
  2. A’s tile has no limit for long or several conditions. Two conditions are two lines; a long one is four (the row 126 px, against 64 today), and the saves below move down. Fix, one of: (a) accept the wrap; (b) the tile shows one condition at most, and with more, or with one over two lines, reads “more on its sheet”, a cut, so it needs a ruling; (c) the pack gives each condition a short tile word. (harden)
  3. A leaves a blank line in the neighbours. On a phone, Mire and Drowning sit in Fever’s row and grow with it, with empty room under their figures (frame 42-1). The armour class tile has the same blank beside the attack tile and it was accepted there (page 39, A2-1). Fix: none drawn; the figure could be centred in the taller tile. (layout)
  4. C’s mark is never explained. “±” appears nowhere else in the app, and a first-time player cannot guess it. When every save has a bonus at times (frame 42-5) all five carry it and it tells nothing apart. Fix: if C, a word (“varies”) rather than a sign, which then costs the same line as A while saying less. (clarify)
  5. A counted number leaves no trace on the tile. Wisps reads 12; a player who knows the book’s 14, or the “+2” on their paper sheet, may add the +2 again. The sheet says it, one tap away. Fix: none drawn; a line in the changelog and the sheet’s rows are the answer proposed. (clarify)
  6. The sheet’s right-hand column means something else than on Armour class. There the right-hand figure is each part (14, +1); here it is the number after each row (14, 12). A player who reads both may stumble. Fix: keep it (the running number is what removes the “+2 makes it lower” puzzle), or show parts as on Armour class (“−2”) and lose that. (clarify)
  7. The class’s row comes back. Ruling sv-2a (2026-11-14) took the class-and-level line off a save’s sheet. The rows need a starting number, so it returns, only when something changes the number (frame 42-4: with nothing counted, no rows, as today). For the ruling’s wording. (clarify)
  8. “The lowest a d20 can show is 1” is the app talking rules. ⚑ A setting may say a rolled 1 always fails, and then “1 or higher succeeds” is not the whole truth. Fix: show the worked number as it is (“−1”) and let the rule text speak, or keep the stop at 1 without the sentence. (clarify)
  9. The line under the heading is a third line of words before any tile. On a phone: “Saves”, the roll line over two lines, then “Spell ward: +1 vs magic, from Lore.” It is still not worked into any number, so against magic the player still adds. It is the lesser evil against five second lines or five marks, and each save’s sheet carries the worked figure. (distill)
  10. “Vs venom only” and the second roll line are new words. Today’s row reads “Vs venom: +2 from Bog folk.” The drawing adds “only” and a second line with the die. Not ruled; the voice guide’s check was by eye. (clarify)
  11. States not drawn. A status that changes saves (a blessing’s +1 for six turns); the GM’s save prompt in a fight, where today the modifier is “offered”; a rules set that does not say which way saves are rolled (nothing can be counted: the designer’s reading is that it stays as today, beside the number); a save set by hand; Larger text; a tablet held upright; loading, offline and error. (harden)
  12. The spoken name is written, not heard. A’s tile is named “Fever 13, 11 against venom: how it’s worked out”; C’s “Fever 13, changes at times”. Neither was tried with a screen reader. (harden)
  13. (On 42-7, 42-8.) K’s sheet buries what is new. With the save’s line, two roll lines and Modifiers above it, the general paragraph makes the sheet the longest on the tab, and it is word for word the same on five sheets. Fix: M. (distill)
  14. M adds a target to a heading row that had none. The ⓘ is 44 px beside a 14 px heading, so the heading row grows; on a phone the row holds “Saves”, the ⓘ and a two-line roll line. Scores and Skills headings have a roll line too and no ⓘ: one heading would differ from its neighbours unless they follow later. (layout)
  15. M with no line from the pack leaves a near-empty sheet. A number, a roll line, a source. Honest, but a first-time player learns nothing there and may not find the ⓘ. Fix: none drawn; the pack checker could note a save with no line. (harden)
  16. The save’s line reads as running text with no label. It sits between the head and the die with nothing saying what it is. It reads as “when this save is asked for” only because of its place and its opening “When”. A pack that writes it another way (“Poison, death rays.”) would read oddly. Fix: none drawn; a labelled line (“When”) is the other shape. (clarify)
  17. The pack’s field was written for another use. A save category’s notes holds the line (the driver’s fact, 2026-11-24). In one pack it is a full sentence with the save’s name in front (“Name: Effects that …”), in another it will be a short “When …” line. The sheet draws what the pack gives, so the two will read differently under the same heading, and a line that repeats the save’s name repeats the title. For the pack’s wording, not the app’s. (clarify)
  18. “Vs magic only: +1 from Lore” is on every save’s sheet. The pack does not say which save it belongs to, so all five sheets carry the row, and each tile’s worked number against magic is only there. Whether it is also said once under the heading is finding 9. (distill)
