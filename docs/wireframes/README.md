# Wireframes

Open `hybrid-wireframe.html` in any browser (double-click it). It follows your device's light/dark setting.

## The two concepts compared (Oct 17, 2026)

- **Look A, clean modern:** flat sans-serif, bars for HP and load, colored chips, numbers dominant. Fastest to read; can feel like a work app.
- **Look B, light old-school:** serif type, double-ruled dividers, plain-language labels ("turn the fourteenth", "the party acts"), HP pips. Most flavor; pips fail above ~20 HP and serif reads slower at a glance.

## Decision: hybrid

Look A's structure, bars and numbers; Look B's serif names, ruled dividers and a few flavored labels, offered as an optional "Classic touch" theme. Legibility wins every tie.

These are concept wireframes, not final designs. R0 includes a clickable mockup built from them.
