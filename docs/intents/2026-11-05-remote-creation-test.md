# Intent: players make characters remotely, as a mini table test

Author: Francis · Date: 2026-11-05 · Status: planned

## Problem
The first table test is still far off, and character creation is the part of the app players meet first and alone. Nobody but Francis has used it.

## Outcome
In Francis's words: "even before table test … ask players to create characters in the app remotely and provide feedback. That will be a mini-table test." Each player joins a campaign from home, makes a character on their own device, and says what was unclear, slow or wrong.

## Touches
Creation (05 Characters; 06 Character creation, guided), joining a campaign by link, the GM's approval of waiting characters, the phone layout; `docs/testing.md` (a short checklist for the players); 08 (where it sits before table test 1).

## Constraints
After the M5b gate passes (creation is part of it). Players use the live site and a real campaign on the private packs; nothing from the packs leaves the app. No new feature is built for it unless a ruling says so.

## Out of scope
Play after creation (expeditions, fights, money). A formal survey tool.

## Open questions
All answered by Francis, 2026-11-05: as soon as creation is ready and he has tested it himself; both settings if the players can spare the time; feedback by seven questions in the group chat and a short call; he wants to learn ease of use, enjoyability, look and feel, and anything we missed. He expects players to ask for custom choices and material from other sources: the plan says what exists and how each request is recorded.
