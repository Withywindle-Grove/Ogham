# Intent: levels of zoom, from a fight to the world

Author: Francis, passing on a player's idea · Date: 2026-11-10 · Status: draft

Taken down by the design agent during the pause of the M5b gate rewalk, in the order it was said. Nothing is designed or built from it before the gate passes. It grew out of the downtime and factions feedback (`2026-11-10-players-feedback.md`, P-2 and P-3).

## Problem
The app has a view for a fight (coming, M6) and a view for an expedition, and the downtime view asked for in P-2. The player sees them as one thing at different distances, and wants to move between them, each showing what the party knows at that distance.

## Outcome
In the player's words, numbered as given:

1. "Perhaps a 3rd action but not combat preset, for stealth/exploration, more like traditional dungeon view."
2. "Perhaps what I really mean is 3 levels of zoom."
3. "Combat is closest zoom level, but consider split party scenarios or people ran out of the room."
4. "(May need to be able to track multiple simultaneous combats.)"
5. "Explore, second level of zoom: your traditional dungeon map, perhaps a village but not entire city, but perhaps a neighborhood of city."
6. "City/region view."
7. "And perhaps a 4th, full zoom out, world map."
8. "Especially good for travel decisions."
9. "Known location tracking."
10. "Note tagging for locations."
11. "Each zoom level can have a side bar of known information thereabout, e.g. city view: known factions, important NPCs, etc."
12. "Perhaps these things are individual objects in your table with a number of facts that the party can learn and discover, which would then be displayed once known."

So: four distances (a fight; a site such as a dungeon, a village or a neighbourhood; a city or region; the world), each with what the party knows there beside it, and every place, person and faction a thing with facts that show once learned.

## Touches
What exists or is planned at each distance: **a fight** (M6: several fights can exist at once, one run at a time and the others paused where they were; a split party already has a group per place, each with its own budget, prompts and pieces); **a site** (M5 and M5b: the place card with its pieces, each shown to players by the GM; "What we know"; the dungeon and settlement modes; M8b: two-layer locations, the location browser); **a region** (M5b: wilderness travel on the region pack's roads and places; the hex-map and point-to-point travel design session on the after-gate list); **the world** (nothing yet). **Facts learned one by one** (12) is how a place's pieces already work; P-3 asks the same of people and factions. Notes on places: the Notes session on the after-gate list.

## Constraints
- The prime directive: fewer taps, eyes on the table. The brief says the app doesn't aid narration "except by revealing room descriptions and visual aids (maps, pictures, handouts)". A map as a visual aid fits; a tactical map the table plays on is a different product and would need Francis's ruling.
- Hidden means hidden: an unknown place, person or fact never reaches a player's device.
- Rules-agnostic: maps, places and factions come from packs or the GM, never from the app.
- Not before the M5b gate passes.

(To be set when it is designed.)

## Open questions
- **Ruled by Francis, 2026-11-10:** "No virtual tabletop for now. Though a map for exploration and dungeon exploration showing where the party is, is fine and desired. A sort of 'mini map' in a sense." So: a map that shows where the party is, in the wilderness and in a dungeon; nothing the table plays tactics on (no tokens moved in a fight, no measuring).
- ⚑ Is a zoom level a **map** (a picture with things placed on it) or a **level of information** (the same screen showing what matters at that distance), or a map where the pack has one and a list where it doesn't?
- ⚑ Whether "zoom" replaces the modes the app already has (a fight, the dungeon turn, the wilderness day, the settlement day, downtime) or is a way to move between them.
- ⚑ Several fights at once (4): M6 rules one fight run at a time with the others paused. Is that enough for a split party, or must two run side by side?
- ⚑ The side bar (11) on a phone, where there is no side.
- ⚑ Who tags notes on a place (10): each player privately, the party together, the GM.
- ⚑ Which milestones: most of it lands in M8b (adventures) and the hex-map session; the world view is new.
