# Intent: patrons and expanded cleric creation

Author: Francis (written down by the design agent from his words, 2026-11-07) · Date: 2026-11-07 · Status: draft; the core is ruled (`design/rulings/m5b-patrons-2026-11-07.md`), the session covers the rest

## Problem
Two books the group plays with, the daemonology add-on and the zine, each give clerics a patron (a god, or a demon lord for a chaotic cleric) in the same shape: holy garb, strictures, weapon restrictions, prayer, alternative spells, a mark of servitude. The app's cleric creation knows nothing of it, so a player picks a class and the patron's whole page stays in the book.

## Outcome
With "Expanded cleric creation" switched on, a cleric picks a patron at creation and the app applies what the patron changes: what they wear and wield, which spells they cast, and the mark they carry; strictures and prayer are there to read. The same for any class the pack flags. The GM sees a patron the alignment doesn't fit as an exception to allow or refuse.

## Touches
07 (a `patrons` kind; the option; the classes it applies to), 05 creation (a Patron step), 06 (the step's mockup; the sheet's identity block), the equip warning and attack penalty (the patron's lists replace the class's), the spell step (the patron's list), 11 (a G-case). Private packs: daemonology and the zine (Pack Creator writes the entries in the packs' own words).

## The session (a paper dry run, `design-session` skill)
Scenario: a player makes a chaotic cleric in a campaign with the add-on attached and the option on; a second player a lawful cleric on the zine's gods; a third tries a patron their alignment forbids. Steps: the option in set-up; Class; alignment; the Patron step and its entry sheet; Spells with the patron's list; Gear with the garb and the restrictions; the review and the GM's card; the sheet; a weapon the patron forbids equipped in play.

Open for the session: the add-on's other optional rules (held, Pack Creator's table in 10); changing patron in play; the book's other parts (races, classes, monsters, locations, items, a downtime activity, spells, summoning, cults) after Pack Creator's inventory.

## Where the zine's gods differ from the add-on's demon lords (Pack Creator)
1. Followers' alignment: per god in the zine; always Chaotic in the add-on. 2. Who follows: worshippers in general (zine) vs any class casting clerical spells (add-on). 3. Weapon restrictions: the add-on only, clerics only. 4. A holy symbol: the zine only. 5. The deity's own alignment and depiction (zine) vs a stat block and a plane (add-on). 6. Spells: a swap list in both ("level N, replaces X"). 7. A note only ("favours non-reversed spells"). 8. Garb, prayer, strictures and marks tiered by spell level in both; garb priced in both. 9. Add-on extras: a cult and a plane per lord; familiar and summoning tables keyed by lord. 10. **Key distinction (Francis, 2026-11-07):** the add-on offers patrons for player characters and, separately, for NPCs (the cults and their leaders); the two are different things in the book and stay separate in the pack: a `patrons` entry for what a player character follows, the cult (a faction, G-b) for the NPC side; the zine's gods are for player characters only.

## Proposed `patrons` shape (Pack Creator, invented values; for the session, not registered)
`id, name, title, option ("expanded-cleric-creation"), blurb, description, alignment, followers {classes, alignments, note}, holySymbol, holyGarb [{atSpellLevel, text, itemIds, costGp}], strictures, prayer, weaponsAllowed {classes, ids, note}, equipment {forbidden, required, note}, spells {extends, swap [{level, replaces, with}]}, marks [{atSpellLevel, text}], realm, factionId, statBlockId, source`. The app applies `weaponsAllowed`, `equipment` and `spells`; the rest it shows. Proposed G-cases: G-a patrons; G-b factions (cults); G-c rituals; G-d study and research as downtime activities; G-e a familiar as a companion.
