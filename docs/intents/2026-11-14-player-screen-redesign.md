# Intent: the Player screen redesigned for the person playing

Author: Francis (written down by the lead, in his words where quoted) · Date: 2026-11-14 · Status: draft

## Problem
Francis made a level-2 character and opened the Player screen on his iPad (dark). His words:
- "Saving throw is missing die icon simplify explanation to just what it takes to pass the check and what die to [roll]." The Saves section prints the rulebook's whole paragraph.
- "Did we do a design reskin here?" It had not been restyled: only character creation carries the book's type so far.
- "Do a style redesign from the perspective of the end user, a novice to moderate experienced roleplayer who needs important information close at hand based on the frequency used but also needs cues about how to play his character narratively."

Who feels it: every player, on the one screen they look at all session. Today the screen gives the same weight to what is used every few minutes (hit points, the weapon in hand, armour class, saves) and what is used once a session (experience); it explains rolls in the rulebook's sentences; and it says almost nothing about who the character is.

## Outcome
A player who has played a handful of sessions finds what they need for the next thing that happens without scrolling or reading a rule: how hurt they are, what they hit with and what to roll, what to tell the GM, what to roll to save. Things used rarely are still there, further down or folded. And the screen keeps the character in front of the player as a person: the few things that say how to play them, drawn from what the player chose and wrote.

## Touches
- The Player screen and the full sheet (05, the sheet's sections and their order: the sheet-order rulings of 2026-11-04 and 2026-11-05; 06, the screen and its components).
- The app-wide restyle that follows the December mockups (ts-2): for this screen the redesign and the restyle are one pass, built once (po-2).
- The Saves line already ruled (sv-1).
- No pack changes expected: the cues come from what packs and characters already carry (kindred and class one-liners, alignment, background and other trait picks, the Details tables' results, motto and backstory, languages).

## Constraints
- **No LLM in the app** (hard rule 1): every cue is the pack's own words or the player's.
- **Rules-agnostic** (hard rule 2): nothing assumes a system's saves, scores or classes; a character with none of a thing shows nothing for it.
- **Calm UI** (hard rule 7) and the prime directive: fewer taps, large text, no line that explains what the screen already shows; one accent button; never a disabled button.
- **Hidden means hidden**: nothing of another player's character or the GM's reaches this screen that does not today.
- **It removes nothing a player can do or read today**; things move, fold or shrink.
- Mockups first: nothing is built before Francis approves the pictures.
- Timing: first play is Saturday 5 December 2026; whether this is built before it is his to say once he has seen the pictures and a size.

## Out of scope
The Scene, Pack and Magic tabs; the GM's screens; character creation; fights.

## Open questions
- ⚑ Which cues: who the character is (looks, manner, background, alignment, motto), how their kindred and class are played (the pack's own one-liners), or both.
- ⚑ Built before 5 December, or with the app-wide restyle.
- ⚑ "Missing the rule" (his note on the same screenshot): a dividing rule under the section headings, the full rule staying reachable once the paragraph goes, or something else.
