# Intent: the homebrew tables pack

Author: Francis (written down by the design agent from his words, 2026-11-07) · Date: 2026-11-07 · Status: draft

## Problem
The classic-advanced setting has no tables for who a character is (names, looks, manner, wants), so its creation ends at the numbers; the fae-woodland setting shows how much those tables add at the table. Francis wants his own, for his game, in a place he can keep adding to. (P23, ruled (b) on 2026-11-06.)

## Outcome
A homebrew pack that is a holding zone for any table Francis wants in his game, meant to grow. It starts with the creation tables, used the way fae-woodland uses its own but for the classic-advanced setting: one table of each type for every race in the core pack and in the zine overlay. The tables are for the story only: no rule, number or modifier comes from any row.

**The flavour (Francis, 2026-11-07):** late-medieval to early-Renaissance Europe, France, Italy, England, Spain and Germany, abstracted so nothing is a direct match (no real place, house or person; names that sound of those tongues without being them). Also, from earlier times: Byzantine, Gallic, Frankish and Vandal, for a different race or a different reach of the setting.

**The voice (Francis, 2026-11-07, from the Human batches):** whimsical, and "describe, don't tell": a row gives the observable detail, never its reason or backstory ("Hair cropped short, except one long lock kept long for personal reasons", not "…kept for luck"); a clause, not a sentence; the player supplies the why. Variety between races: a motif (a lost home, a broken nose) may recur in a few lists, never in every race's. Names are invented, playful, fantastical compounds carrying the race's flavour (the Gnome batch set the register, 2026-11-07; Human, Dwarf, Elf and Half-elf names were redone to it, auto-approved; Halfling stood). The more fantastical the race, the more fantastical its names and traits, inspired by where such races appear (OSR books, film, literature) in our own words; the European flavours are a seasoning there, the base for the near-human races. Names come as two sets, noble and common, where the race has a nobility (half-elves have one set); surnames are one shared list per race (Human keeps its two, approved first).

Rows written by the pack session, drawing inspiration from the source material and from popular fiction and game supplements; the words are the pack's own (07's IP rule: no row copied from a book, inspiration not reproduction).
