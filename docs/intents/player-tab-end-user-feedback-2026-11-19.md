# Intent: the Player tab, feedback from one of the players, 19 November 2026

Author: Francis, passing on one of his players' messages · Date: 2026-11-19 · Status: accepted

Collected in chat by the designer on 2026-11-19, message by message, in the order he sent them. The player's messages are told here in other words, with no name and no direct quote; nothing is sorted or decided here. He asked that nothing be processed until all of it was in, "then we can compare whats in flight and what to add".

- **Who is speaking.** The messages are the player's, passed on by Francis. Where he marks his own view he says "my take"; the framing lines (the first and the last) are his.
- **Pictures** (7) are not in the repo: they are screenshots of his live campaign and show a private pack's names. Each is described here in neutral words.
- **The screen** in the pictures is the Player tab as live on 2026-11-19, before page M1's changes are built (the heading "Full character sheet" and its Edit are still there). Pictures 6 and 7 are the Pack tab.

## His framing, before the first message

"Note some of this feedback may be similar to what's already been given by myself and we may have fixes being built. please hold processing until I"ve added it all, then we can compare whats in flight and what to add"

## Message 1: Details, and Abilities and Traits

**Picture 1** (dark, the right column's foot): the heading "Full character sheet" with Edit and the quill; then four rows with chevrons: "Abilities" over a line naming four abilities "and 4 more"; "Traits" over a line naming four traits "and 3 more"; "Magic" over a line with two spell lists and their counts; "Notes" over "No notes yet".

**Picture 2** (dark): the heading "Details" with a chevron at the right, and three labelled lines under it (Head, Face, Body, each with a few words).

**What the player said, in other words:** these two sections felt folded up too far. Details reads well when open, but the player would like to put the sections in their own order, choosing which show when folded. Abilities and Traits sitting two taps deep is hard going: the player wants to see every trait and ability at once (anything they can act on), each with its name and a short description on hover.

**Francis's take** (his own words): "my take at minimum we need to add pinning to abilities and traits so that they can stay expanded and ordered selectively. players also want the ability to reorder their own screen and have it saved to their view."

## Message 2: the weapon in hand

**Picture 3** (a screenshot of a chat message, dark): the weapon in hand's block. "Staff", "In hand" under it, the stacked Attack button at the right, and one line of two rolls, each with its die's glyph: "d20 +1 to hit", "d4 +1 damage". Above it, the player's chat message praising the block.

**What the player said, in other words:** this block works very well, and the player would like the same treatment for Abilities, Traits and Magic.

## Message 3: the Movement box

**Picture 4** (dark): the heading "Movement" and one box under it: "90" in bold, then "Movement, 30 ft in a fight". The box's lower half is empty.

**What the player said, in other words:** this box feels wrong. Perhaps join it with the burden meter and its status in one box on the left, and put a small inventory panel on the right showing the three to five heaviest items, or something like it.

## Message 4: the same box

**What the player said, in other words:** the movement box's contents would fit above the burden line ("Lightly Burdened"), all in one box.

## Message 5: the space under the headings

**Picture 5** (dark, the right column from its top): "Scores" with its roll line at the right, and two rows of three score boxes, five with a modifier under the figure and one with none; "Saves" with its roll line and five boxes; "Movement" with the box of picture 4 at the left and, at the right, a second box: "Lightly burdened", a bar, "540 of 1,600 coins' weight. Small gear counts as 80". The space between "Scores" and its boxes is plainly larger than the space under "Saves" and under "Movement".

**What the player said, in other words:** amused by the uneven spacing, the player asked whether the larger gap above the Scores table, compared with Saves and Movement, is on purpose.

## Message 6: the Pack tab's ⓘ

**Picture 6** (dark, the Pack tab): one line: "In the backpack" in small capitals, an ⓘ, "7 items".

**Picture 7** (the same line after a tap on the ⓘ, which shows a focus box): "In the backpack", the ⓘ, then "holds things; doesn't add capacity" in bold; "7 items" has dropped to the next line.

**What the player said, in other words:** the player laughed at tapping the ⓘ and getting only that short line of text.

## His closing line

"that's all. ignore pack feedback for now"

So message 6 is set aside by him: it is kept here and not taken up at this sitting.
