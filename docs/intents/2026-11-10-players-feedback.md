# Intent: players' feedback (10 November): the portrait, downtime at a glance, factions and people

Author: Francis, passing on his players' feedback · Date: 2026-11-10 · Status: draft

Taken down by the design agent during the pause of the M5b gate rewalk. One entry per piece of feedback; the players' own messages are told here in other words, with no name and no direct quote. Nothing is designed or built from it before the gate passes. The scars idea from the same conversation has its own file (`2026-11-10-scars-chronicle.md`).

## Problem
Players using the character screen on a wide screen (the play column on the left, the full sheet on the right) want more of the character's face and a clearer top of the column.

## Outcome
- **P-1 A larger portrait.** A player asked for a larger portrait on the character screen, as wide as the column, with the title, class and level, and kindred moved below it instead of beside it. Today the portrait is a small round picture at the top left of the play column, with the title line ("<title>, <class> <level>, <kindred>") and the one-line description beside it, above the hit points. From a player's screenshot of a classic-advanced character, marked up in red: a box around the portrait, the title line and the hit points.
- The same screenshot has arrows drawn down the page. **Answered by the player (relayed by Francis):** read it from top left to bottom right: the picture grows to fill the box, everything below it moves down, and the text that was beside the picture sits just under it. Nothing moves between columns.
- **The player's own caveats:** for a thief the column holds little, so there is room; the player wasn't sure whether a caster's spells sit in that column, which would crowd it. (They don't: a caster's spells are on the Magic tab since mp-20, so the column stays as light for a caster.) And the player suggested **a way to enlarge the portrait when wanted, rather than making it large by default.**

- **P-2 Downtime at a glance.** A ready-made downtime view: the important places the party knows, where each character is, and what each is doing. A view for the time between adventures.
- **P-3 Factions and people.** A player asked for a tracker of factions and the people the party meets, with how they relate. Francis, on what it means: **for the players, a persistent look-up** ("factions and NPCs will need a persistent look up, is what the player is asking"): the people and factions the party has met, with how they stand, there to consult at any time, not only inside an adventure. **For the GM, the living world:** "we'll want to also do a tracker of what the faction agendas are and how they're proceeding". So two layers on the same people and factions: what the party knows (shown to players), and each faction's agenda and its progress (the GM's alone until revealed).
- **Ruled by Francis:** "GM and player both need their own reference screen for that": one screen for the players (what the party knows) and one for the GM (the same people and factions with their agendas and progress), not one screen with parts hidden.
- ⚑ for P-3 (to settle at its design session): what "relations" covers (a faction toward the party, faction to faction, a person to a faction); how an agenda advances (by the GM's hand, by the calendar, by a roll at downtime); whether the party keeps its own notes on each.

- **P-4 Quests and rumours.** A player wondered about tracking quests and rumours separately, or whether that is simply a kind of note. Rumours are already in the adventures milestone (M8b, from the pack); what is new is the party tracking them and its quests: what was heard, from whom, what was done about it. ⚑ A kind of note with a state (open, done, false) and links to the places, people and factions it names, or its own list; to settle at the Notes session.

## Touches
For P-2 and P-3: the roadmap already has NPCs and factions "with Introduce and status" in M8b (adventures) and downtime in M7, with its second stage waiting for a design session (G-178 … G-188); organizations in M9. What neither names: relations between factions, people and the party, and one downtime view of who is where doing what. For P-1: 06 (the Player screen and the play column beside the full sheet; the portrait component); 05 (portraits). The players' standard of 2026-11-07 (mockup `design/mockups/players/01-player-pack-sheet-standard.html`).

## Constraints
Calm UI: the hit points and the figures a player glances at stay reachable without scrolling past a large picture on a phone; a character with no portrait must not leave a hole. Not before the M5b gate passes.

## Out of scope
Changing what the Player screen holds (ruled 2026-10-26).

- **Ruled by Francis in the same conversation:** "this is for tablet only", so the phone keeps today's small portrait; and with no portrait the layout closes up, with no empty block ("I agree no portrait should resize").

## Open questions
- ⚑ The picture's shape at full width (round, a square with soft corners, a banner) and how a small uploaded picture is treated.
- ⚑ Default or toggle: the large portrait for everyone, or the small one by default with a way to enlarge it (the player's own suggestion). Design's first thought: tap the portrait to enlarge it, remembered on the device, so each player chooses and nobody's figures are pushed down unasked.
