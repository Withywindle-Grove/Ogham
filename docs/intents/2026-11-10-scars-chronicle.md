# Intent: a chronicle of scars

Author: Francis · Date: 2026-11-10 · Status: draft

Taken down by the design agent during the pause of the M5b gate rewalk, from Francis's words and his players' feedback.

## Problem
Characters change over a campaign through grievous injuries: a score goes down, a foot or an eye is lost, someone is hideously burned. Today the app can record the number (a player submits a score change, the GM approves it) and one free line of "Scars and marks" in Details. It can't keep the story with the number, it can't give an injury an effect of its own, and there is no history to read back. In his words: "given these tend to go down due to grievous injuries, perhaps an ability to add features/abilities as time goes by to chronicle the accumulated scars (this is a great homebrew pack addition we'd need modeling for)".

## Outcome
A character carries a dated list of what has marked them. Each entry says what happened and when in the campaign. Some entries carry an effect of their own ("players have lost a foot or an eye before, or become hideously burned"); others are "narrative explanations for a lost stat", the words kept with the change they explain. A homebrew pack can supply a table of injuries; a typed one works the same way.

## Touches
05 (Characters: traits and features gained in play; sheet edits approved by the GM); 06 (the full sheet, the Details ledger's "Scars and marks", the breakdown sheets where an effect would show as a named part); 07 (a pack kind or table for injuries, each with or without an effect); 04 (an entry on the character with its date, source and effect); the log. Private packs: a homebrew pack to hold the table. Near neighbours on the after-gate list: statuses that change figures (gate rewalk F25: a condition's modifier field doesn't exist yet) and what a magic item's bonus does to a figure (mw-1).

## Constraints
- Ruled by Francis in the same conversation: **it depends on the scar**: an effect is optional on each entry.
- **Who adds one:** the GM adds, "with the rare exception of a character intentionally maiming themself"; so allow both: the GM gives one directly; a player may propose one, which waits for the GM's approval as a score edit does.
- Everything through commands, logged, with Undo; nothing erased (hard rules 3 and 4). Rules-agnostic: the app carries no injury table of its own; effects come from the pack or are typed.
- Not before the M5b gate passes.

## Out of scope
Healing or regrowing (a scar that ends); injuries rolled automatically from damage in a fight (M6 decides what a fight prompts); XP or level loss.

## Open questions
- ⚑ What an effect may change: a score, a save, movement, an attack figure, a skill, or only a note for the table to apply.
- ⚑ Whether a scar's effect and a status's modifier are one mechanism (F25 proposes a modifier field for conditions; a scar is a condition that doesn't end).
- ⚑ Where it reads back: its own section on the full sheet, the Details ledger, or both; and whether other players see it.
- ⚑ The pack's shape: a roll table whose rows are injuries, or a list the GM picks from, or both.
- ⚑ Which milestone.
