# 08 — Roadmap & Timeline

## How to read this timeline

- It assumes **about 30 hours a week on average** (re-forecast 2026-10-25; was 6), for a novice building with Claude Code, including M5b, M8b, the design system work and table test 1.
- Hours will vary week to week. The dates are a **forecast, not a contract**. They flex.
- Each milestone has a Google Calendar date with alerts 1 week and 1 day before.
- A weekly check-in (Sundays, 5:47pm ET) asks how many hours went in and what got done. If the plan drifts a week or more either way, it proposes new dates for every remaining milestone and moves them only after you say yes.
- Each milestone ends with a **gate**: you try it, approve it, and it's committed before the next one starts.
- **Gate workflow** (ruled 2026-10-25): During a gate, Claude Code collects Francis's feedback without acting on it until the gate is finished or he explicitly says to act on an item, then hands over one grouped list (ruled · needs a ruling · technical) for the design chat. Meanwhile it may plan the next milestone (plan plus ⚑ list) and build engine-only pieces (no UI, no migrations) on a separate branch, merged only after the gate passes and its fixes are in.

## Scope checks

There's no scope freeze. Any new feature or change to an approved design gets a scope check before anyone builds it:

> **Scope check — [name]**
> **What:** one line
> **Now:** goes in [milestone] · cost [days] · pushes back [what] · chance of redoing work [low/med/high]
> **Later:** goes in [milestone or R2] · cost then [days] · what we lose by waiting
> **Signals:** [rules of thumb that apply]
> **Recommendation:** [Now / Later / R2 / Drop] because …
> **Options:** A Now · B Later · C R2 · D Drop

Rules of thumb: touches foundations (data model, permissions, event log, pack format) → usually cheaper now · screen or layout only → usually fine later · changes what the table test can measure → lean before the test · pushes back the next gate by more than a week → flag it and offer a smaller version · rare at the table → lean later.

**Silent choices (standing rule, 2026-10-31).** Where the rulings are silent and an agent had to choose, a choice that is already built and harmless counts as ruled unless Francis objects at the gate. The agent lists each such choice in its status report (and the plan's ⚑ list says "chosen: …"), so they are reviewed in one place, not one by one.

Technical checks (no change to behaviour or screens) are ruled in Claude Code. Anything that would change 05, 06 or 08 goes to the design Project. Every ruling is logged in 09 with a "Scope:" prefix.
