### 1a (branch `dec-1a-patrons`, 2026-11-11)
**The shape and the lead's rulings for 1a** (relayed to the Builder by the lead, 2026-11-11; added here by the Builder as told):

- A swap's `with` may name a spell on another class's list in another attached pack: the cleric casts it as a cleric spell at the swap's `level`, whatever list it came from; it need not be on a cleric list.
- A swap, or a follower class, whose pack isn't attached is skipped and shown as text ("pack not attached"), never a failure.
