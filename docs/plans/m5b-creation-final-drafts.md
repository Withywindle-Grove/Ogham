## What this step delivers

### A · The shell and the pieces (every screen; build first, others start from its branch)
1. **The app bar:** ← at the top left on every screen (an icon button, "Back"), the title centred, ⋯ at the right. Leave moves into ⋯. On step 1 the footer's Back reads **Leave** and does what Leave does (cw-13). No "‹" or "›" anywhere in creation.
2. **The Roll row** (new inventory row): the die's glyph, the instruction in bold at body size, never a caption. One per thing to roll, placed above its field or list.
3. **The die glyphs** (new inventory row): the solids' projections as SVG, outline 2 px, inner edges 1 px, nothing inside; d4 tetrahedron, d6 cube, d8 octahedron, d10 ten-sider, d12 dodecahedron, d20 icosahedron, d100 as two d10; each die its colour from 06's palette (d4 info, d6 ink, d8 accent, d10 lit-light, d12 danger, d20 warning). Paths are in `docs/design/mockups/creation/final/final.js` (`DIE_O`, `DIE_I`).
4. **The warning sign** (replaces the plain ▲ everywhere): a triangle with "!" in the warn colour, with its words. Marks closed rows, needs-approval, over-limit, offline.
5. **The average-value stepper:** prefilled with the average rounded to a value the dice can make, stepping by the dice's multiplier; the value in lighter weight with the line "Average value, replace with your roll" until **touched** (a per-field flag, never a comparison of values); once touched, full weight and the field's caption. The review and the GM's card read the flag ("the average, not rolled").
6. **Help me choose:** a full-width button; open, a full-width box that grows to its text, closed by a ✕ at its top right. No "Close help" button.
7. **The sheet** (the Dialog row becomes Sheet; "pop-up" means this): slides over the dimmed page from the bottom on a phone, anchored to its button on a tablet; ✕ or a tap outside closes it. Used for every entry, table, the kit's roll sequence, Start over, Continue, a failed send.
8. **The accent follows the ask:** one accent button per screen, on the action that moves the player forward now. A step with one ask: the (default) Pick until picked, then Next. A step with several asks: Next.
9. **No middle dot in creation** (Francis, 2026-11-07, 3:20 AM: creation now; the app-wide sweep is its own pull request after creation lands, 829 lines in 144 files per the Builder, with a picture batch): a colon after a count ("9 items: leather, spear"), figures as a sentence ("120 rolled, 2 sp spent, 119 gp 8 sp left"), a second line where there is room, commas inside a line. A test that no creation screen renders " · ".
10. **Tablets:** two columns only where the second has a job (Race and Class: the entry; Who they are: the open table or the liege's entry, else Derived values; Spells, Languages, Weapon skills: the entry, else "You speak" / "Yours so far"; Gear and the shop: coin, load, yours so far); otherwise one column widened, tiles three across, the review's cards two across. Below 700 px the right column drops under the left and folds.
11. **The phone's navigation** outside creation (mockup 17): a bottom tab bar (Player, Scene, Pack, Log; icons crown, campfire, tied sack, open book; the current one bold with an accent mark above; a badge for what waits) and a left drawer (≡; the campaign's name and gear; groups "This campaign", "Your characters", "As the GM", "Elsewhere"; the person at the foot; icons: hills and a tower for Places, scales for Rules, library shelf for the setting's entries, round table for Table view, sealed letter for Invite, two banners for Your campaigns). The tablet keeps its rail. **Its own slice (E), after creation.**

### B · Scores and Adjust (final draft 1)

### C · Kindred, Class, Who they are (final drafts 2, 3)
- Pick rows: the name opens the entry as a sheet (source page, blurb, the figures that matter, Pick at the foot); Pick picks and moves on where it is the only ask; a closed row shows the sign and why and, tapped, explains and offers **Ask the GM to allow it** (the pick is taken, flagged; the GM's card calls it out).
- Who they are: the name field with its placeholder; one row per name table with **Open** (the pack's own, then "Optional, from <pack>"); the table as a sheet with the Roll row, one tab per part of the name, the rows; **no typed-number box**, the row is the tap; a tapped row fills that part. Outlook as pick rows (closed ones with the sign). "Derived values" folded (cw-7), each figure with its source in words.

### D · Magic, Languages, Weapon skills, Gear, Details, the review (final drafts 4–7)
- Given things are marked **Given** with no button. Several groups on a step: Pick stays, Next confirms. One count per step, in the footer.
- Languages: "You speak" (given), "Pick N more" with the die's rows numbered in their blurbs and the left-out number as a closed row with its reason, then "Or choose one of these"; a row needing approval carries the sign on its own line.
- Weapon skills: "Pick N", counted; a skill names its variants ("Also the silver knife").
- Gear: the coin's Roll row, the average stepper (100, step 10); **"To choose (pick N)"**: kits (the first Pick accent until one is taken), the keepsake's Roll row and table, the shop (its own screen); "Yours already" folded by group; the load line with its bar. A kit with rolls: Pick opens the roll sequence as a sheet, one roll per step, a roll made twice as one step with two taps; afterwards the row shows what it gave with "(rolled)" and **Change**; the keepsake shows its result with Change. **Coin outside the dice's range: warn** (the sign and the limit on the row; Next explains if tapped; an exception on the GM's card), never clamp.
- Details: optional; Body as average steppers in years / feet and inches / pounds, one Roll row ("Roll for each at the table, or type them") and each card naming its dice ("1d6 + 18, years"); the describing tables one row each with Open, the rest folded as "N more tables"; each table as a sheet with a typed field at the foot; a filled row shows its result with Change.
- The review: one card per step with Change at its top right; the name card and gear as label-left rows; scores on one line with the trade noted; hit points with the sum; magic by source; Given folded with the load line; Derived values folded; **every exception called out** with the sign; the phone footer stacked (Send to your GM full width with the accent; Save and leave and Start over side by side). A step opened from the review says "From the review" and its footer reads **Back to review** on both buttons while nothing changed. **Start over** asks once in a sheet (the one confirm in creation).
- The GM's card: exceptions first, each with the pack's reason and **Allow it / Take it off**; then the character as rows; coin as "rolled, spent, left"; the full sheet one tap away; Send back or Approve.

### F · The states (final draft 8)
Loading as skeleton rows with a line; offline as a top line with the sign, Next plain and explaining; a failed send as a sheet (Try again, Save and leave); Continue where you left off as a sheet naming what was kept and when; a step with nothing to ask skipped, and saying why if reached by Back.
