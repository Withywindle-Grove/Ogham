# Plan: players make characters remotely (a mini table test)

Written by the design agent, 2026-11-05. Intent: `docs/intents/2026-11-05-remote-creation-test.md`. Ruled by Francis the same day (when, settings, what to learn, and the feedback proposal). **Nothing is built for it.**

## When and what

| | |
|---|---|
| When | As soon as creation is ready (after the M5b gate) **and Francis has tested it himself** |
| Settings | Both: classic-advanced and fae-woodland, one character in each if the player can spare the time; one if not |
| Where | The live site, a real campaign per setting on the private packs; players join by link on their own device |
| What Francis wants to learn | Ease of use · enjoyability · look and feel · anything we missed |

## How it runs

1. **Francis sends only the join link and one sentence.** No instructions and no guide: guided creation is meant to stand on its own, so needing help is a finding.
2. **One ask while they create:** "Note the first place you hesitated or guessed."
3. **Seven questions straight after**, answered by voice note or message in the group's usual chat:
   1. How long did it take, and on what device?
   2. Which step was easiest, and which hardest?
   3. Ease, 1 to 5, and why?
   4. Did you enjoy it, 1 to 5, and why?
   5. The look: one word, and anything hard to read?
   6. Anything missing or wrong for your character?
   7. Was there anything you wanted to choose, or bring in from another book or source, that the app didn't offer? What, and from where?
4. **A 15-minute group call** once everyone has finished, for what the answers raise.
5. **Francis approves the waiting characters** as GM (that is part of the test: what the GM sees).
6. **The designer turns the answers into one grouped list** (bugs · wording · design questions; ruled · needs a ruling · technical), as for a gate.

## Expect: custom choices and new sources (Francis, 2026-11-05)

Players will ask to make choices the packs don't list and to pull in material from other books. Be ready, don't improvise:

**Checked against the app by the builder, 2026-11-05 (main c2269459).** What a player can and can't do today:

| They ask for | What exists today | What Francis says |
|---|---|---|
| A language, a background or trade, or a weapon skill the list doesn't have | **Other…** on those three steps only. The GM's approval card lists them under "Typed by <player>" with **Add to homebrew** on each (a typed weapon skill becomes a homebrew weapon for the GM to fill in) | "Type it under Other; I'll see it when I approve you." |
| An item that isn't listed | **Not in creation:** the Gear step has no one-off item. After approval the player can make one from the Pack ("Not listed? Make a one-off item"), which waits for the GM | "Finish the character; add it from your Pack afterwards and I'll approve it." |
| A listed choice that misses a requirement or a soft limit (a class their kindred rarely takes, a score need, an alignment) | It can be picked and shows "Needs the GM's special approval"; the GM sees the missed requirement on the card, and approving the character approves it. **There is no field to say why** | "Pick it, and tell me why in the chat." |
| A choice the rules forbid outright, or scores over the range | Can't be picked or entered | "Tell me; if I agree I'll change it on my side." |
| A class, kindred or spell that isn't in the packs | **Can't be typed.** The GM can add single entries (Setting rules › + New homebrew entry: class, race, spell, item…); a new spell shows at creation only once it is on a class's list. A whole book becomes a private pack through the pack session | "Tell me the book and the page; I'll add it, or get it converted, and you can change the character after." |
| A character that doesn't follow the steps | **Freeform** ("Type in what you rolled"). A Freeform character has no class or kindred from the packs, so nothing it types reaches Add to homebrew and nothing is flagged | "Use Freeform only if nothing else fits, and tell me what you changed." |

**What the test will likely show is missing** (to rule after it, not before): somewhere to tell the GM why or to ask for something ("a note to your GM" on the review); a one-off item during creation; Other… on class, kindred and spells.

Every such request is written down (question 7) and comes back in the designer's list as: **possible today** (say how) · **needs homebrew or a pack** (to the pack session) · **needs a ruling** (a design question). The table above is the builder's reading of the app, not the rulings'.
