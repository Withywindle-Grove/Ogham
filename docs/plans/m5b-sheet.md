# Plan: the full character sheet (sr-1 … sr-10)

Ruling: `docs/design/rulings/m5b-sheet-review-2026-11-04.md`. Review: `docs/design/notes/sheet-review-2026-11-03.md`. Mockups: `docs/design/mockups/sheet/01-sheet-review.html`, `02-sheet-extras.html`. M5b, before the gate. Test it on the seeded campaigns (`npm run seed:test`, docs/testing.md).

## Build order (each its own PR, from main)
1. **Combat, movement, skills** (sr-1, sr-2, sr-8): the four captioned tiles; one Movement section from the travel speed, the rest folded under Speed; class skills out of the Combat grid into Skills (two groups, the pack's notation, a fold after 4); the Player screen's skill names; Attack's breakdown with the to-hit row under descending AC. 5–7 turns.
2. **Traits and advancement** (sr-3 as amended by `design/rulings/m5b-traits-chips-2026-11-04.md` tc-1 … tc-4, sr-5): the traits as **wrapped chips** by source (a small heading per group; pips, "· drawback" and "· lv N" inside the chip; each opens its entry; a group folds after 12 with "+ N more"; one component for the Player panel and the full sheet; layout test at 375 px with a long name and 13 traits); Advancement with the XP bar, the prime bonus and its reason, the title. 4–6 turns.
3. **Magic** (sr-4): the Magic section with spells-a-day pips and a tab per kind of magic (book, prepared, glamours, runes, knacks), rows opening entries. 4–6 turns.
4. **Order and gating** (sr-6, sr-7): two columns on a tablet, stacked with one-line summaries on a phone; fields shown only when they apply (with the options-gates reader). 3–5 turns. **Added by Design from #446's screenshots (2026-11-05):** at 834 px portrait the four Combat tiles never run into the next column (they wrap two by two when they can't fit); no caption line starts with a separator dot (today "· NIM +1", "· missile +1" and "1d8 ·" wrap that way at 834 and 375): a caption breaks at its separator and drops it; the layout test covers both at 834 and 375.
5. **Pack load** (sr-9): a bar per band, the band that sets the speed named, tiny items as a group. 2–3 turns. **Ruled 2026-11-05 (`design/rulings/m5b-pack-load-words-2026-11-05.md`):** counts say "items"; the bands are Equipped and Stowed here and on the Pack tab (a pack's own names win); groups fold in place; a tiny item's weight stays as built (pl-1, pl-2). **Design's review of #479:** invented figures only in specimens and tests (no setting's slot counts, speeds or coin limits); armour mode in two plain lines; thousands commas; coin mode's heading gives the tier and the speed.
6. **`sheetFields` in pack:check** (sr-10): read the list, report printed fields with no home. 1–2 turns.

**The Pack tab's folded Tiny section** (06, Pack summary; SC-5-2): ruled 2026-11-05 (Francis): before the gate if a builder slot is free without taking from the gate chain, else after; the builder has the slot, so it follows order 5 as its own small pull request, with pl-1's words, its Pack tab pictures to Design before merge.

**The M5b gate waits for orders 3–6 (Francis, 2026-11-05).** Sequence: order 6 now (scripts only); orders 3 and 5 in parallel once order 2 (#446) merges; order 4 last and alone, since it moves every section. Order 3 uses order 2's use pips, never a second pip component. Order 5 starts from the two band bars area-2 PR 3 (#433) built on the Pack tab: one bar component for the Pack tab and the sheet, not a copy. Each order's pictures go to Design before it merges; gate check 64 flips when order 4 lands.

## Tests (purpose first)
- No full sheet shows a raw "Movement: <mode>" box or a bare Attack number.
- A thief's skills appear under Skills in the thief's group, not under Combat.
- A character with no magic has no Magic section; a magician's spells-a-day shows pips, not a list.
- Liege, moon sign and weapon proficiencies show only when they apply (the seeded options-on and defaults campaigns).
- With descending AC, Attack's breakdown has the to-hit row; with ascending, it doesn't.
- pack:check lists a printed field with no home in an invented fixture.

## Scope checks
- Cast or Prepare on the full sheet (M7).
- A fourth player tab (M7, sr-4).

**Build order 3, Magic** (`m5b-sheet-3-magic`, 2026-11-05; sr-4; stacked on #446):
1. **A kind of magic is a spell list.** The tabs are Spell book (a `book` caster's own list, and anything in a book caster's book that's on no list), Prepared (a caster with slots, or anything prepared), then every other list holding a spell of the character's book, **named as the pack names the list** ("Glamours", "Bogling knacks", "Hymns"), not the ruling's short words (Runes, Knacks): the app never names a kind itself. A spell on no list, for a caster without a book, sits under "Other magic". A known-magic class (neither `book` nor `divine`, e.g. glamours) always shows its own list's tab, empty or not; a divine caster's list shows only once it holds spells (they pray for any spell, so "Hymns · 0" would mislead).
2. **Who has a Magic section:** spells in the book (First spells writes the kindred's glamours there too), anything prepared, a spell slot at this level, a class that casts from this level (`fromLevel`), or a kindred with `firstSpells`. A divine caster below its `fromLevel` with nothing has none.
3. **Spells a day are pips with no spent ones:** nothing records a cast spell until M7's Cast, so every pip is filled (`UsePips` with nothing spent). Only levels with slots show (a level with none is left out, not "Level 4 —"); a screen reader hears the list "Spells a day", then each level as "Level 1, 3 a day" (Design, on #482). With the section showing, the raw "Spell slots 3, 2" box (the figure no field shows, or a layout field filled with `spellSlots`) is left out of the sheet.
4. **The tabs are the Button row** (the pressed one in the Selected wash, tf-4), not the mockup's underlined text tabs: an approved component that wraps at 375 px; a lone kind shows as a heading instead of one button. A new tab look would be a new component (Design's call).
