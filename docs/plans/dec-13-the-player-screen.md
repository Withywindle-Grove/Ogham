# Plan: the Player screen for the person playing, built once

Written by the lead on 2026-11-15, the day Francis ruled it to the front of the line (ord-1: "Revamped player screen goes to the front of the line"). It had rulings and approved frames since 14 November and no plan: the lead's gap, found when Francis looked at his live screen.

It covers the Player screen (a character's own screen: `src/app/PlayerScreen.tsx`) on a tablet and a phone. The figure's sheets (tap a figure for where it comes from) are `docs/plans/dec-player-figure-sheet.md`, built and live; not repeated here. The Pack tab's rows are `docs/plans/dec-6-the-whole-pack-tab.md`; the players' Scene is `docs/plans/dec-9-the-players-scene.md`.

**Status: RELEASED to the Builder on 2026-11-15, every row.** C8 (pins reaching spells and traits) has Francis's yes of the same day (pin-2): asked "A player may pin a spell or a trait of their own character to At hand; it adds a record and removes nothing.", he answered "Yes".

## How it is built
- **Whole, once (po-4).** Rows a person can see are built on a held branch, `dec-13-player-stack`, from main, and go live together after Francis has seen one set of as-built pictures (tablet and phone, light and dark, a plain character and a messy one).
- **Mockups first.** Every row names its frame and the approval's id and date. Pictures of record: branch `design-december-mockups`, page `docs/design/mockups/december/14-player-screen-for-the-player.html`, frames P1 (tablet) and P2 (phone) at 4303bd32, approved by ps-9 on 2026-11-14 ("Approve both"); the icons, page 20, frame IC1, approved by ic-3 on 2026-11-14.
- **Everything ruled for this screen** is gathered in `docs/design/notes/player-screen-ruled-so-far-2026-11-14.md` (nt-1): read it whole before building; where it and this plan differ, stop and ask.
- **Parts are hosted, not rebuilt.** The full sheet's parts (scores, saves, movement, traits, skills, languages) are the listed components placed in the new order; a variant is named in 06's inventory, never copied.
- **In the book's type** and on the shared stylesheet (ms-1, pq-1). A row's button is the stacked button (sb-2, stk-2). Targets 44 px with 8 px between (t44-1). One accent a screen: this screen has none unless a frame draws one.
- **A pop-up opened from inside a sheet keeps its own form** (`SheetScope.Provider value={false}`, with a layout check).
- **First play.** Not needed for the night itself (fights are on paper; the figure sheets are live); it is what players hold, so it is built first.
