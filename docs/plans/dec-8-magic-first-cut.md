Written by the lead on 2026-11-12 from the Builder's sizing of the same day. Magic in play is ruled (`docs/design/rulings/m5c-magic-in-play-2026-11-05.md`, mp-1 to mp-21; `m5c-magic-mockups-2026-11-10.md`, mm-1 to mm-5), its mockups were approved by Francis on 2026-11-10, and its full build is planned in `m5c-1-magic-state.md` to `m5c-4-prepare-and-rest.md` (about 30 to 44 builder turns). This plan carves the part the first night needs, about **10 to 13 turns**; the rest of those four plans stands for after the 5th. **The cut is put to Francis before any of it is built.**

## What the first night gets
A caster sees what is ready, casts with one tap and an Undo, chooses for tomorrow, and gets spells back at a rest; a character's limited-use abilities refresh by the pack's `uses.per`.

Already in the app, which is why the cut is small: each character's prepared list and spent uses are stored; the Player screen lists prepared spells by level; abilities show their uses as dots from the pack's `uses`.

## Left to the table on 5 December
| Left out | Why |
|---|---|
| Timers, lights and conditions started by a cast; dice durations | The slice with the most unknowns (7–10 turns); the GM starts a light or a timer by hand today |
| The morning window, its prompt and the GM's line | A convenience on top of Prepare: choosing for tomorrow happens on Prepare and the rest's tick makes it ready |
| Scrolls, Give a spell, found spells | Read at the table from the item or the book |
| Turning's table | Looked up in the Rules browser |
| Conjured creatures; "spell lost" | Both need fights |
| The reversed form at casting or memorising | Said at the table; the cast still marks the slot |
| Casting blocked by gear or a status | The GM rules it |
| The fourteen ability shapes `uses` cannot carry (a power of its own inside an ability, a use a week, a use a target); weekly, yearly and once-ever refills | Counted at the table; the GM resets dots by hand. A format ruling is owed after the 5th |

## Departures
**Piece A** (`dec-8-a-magic-state`, 2026-11-13; migration `20261113140000_dec_8a_magic_state.sql`):
1. **The cast mark is a count per spell and level, not a mark per slot** (`character_play.spells_cast`, keyed `"<level>:<spell id>"`). It is what mm-1's dots read (one row a spell, a dot a casting), and "casting marks one slot of a spell taken twice, not both" holds. It is still written only through a database function (`cast_spell`, the row locked), so two devices can't both spend the last casting.
2. **Nothing chosen reads as "the same again".** `chosen` is null until someone chooses; the next rest then brings back today's ready spells (mm-5's "selected by default"). Piece D reads `nextReady` / `afterRest` for this.
3. **The GM's set goes through a database function too** (`set_spells`, GM only; with no casts given it keeps today's, trimmed inside the function against the locked row, so a cast made meanwhile on another device isn't lost), so the app's GM-only rule has a database twin and the permission table can test "a player can't set their own spells the GM's way". Who reads or writes `character_play` is unchanged.
4. **Undo a cast is its own command** (`character.uncastSpell`, the same function with -1) rather than the log's row undo, so an Undo never rolls back another cast made on another device in between.
5. **The log line names the spell only when the reader's screen knows its name** (`LogNames.spells`, optional); until B fills it, a cast's line says "a level 1 spell". B fills it.
6. **The old `character.prepare` stays as it is** (it writes today's ready list, as the Player screen's present Prepare does); C moves Prepare to `character.chooseSpells`, and the Player screen's section goes with B. Nothing a person sees changes in A.
