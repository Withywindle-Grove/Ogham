## How it is built
- **Mockups first.** The pictures of record are frames of `docs/design/mockups/december/42-save-bonuses-in-the-number.html`, **approved 2026-11-25**: 42-29, 42-28's third picture, 42-25 and 42-26, with the sheets of 42-19 and 42-20. 42-22, 42-23, 42-24 and 42-27 are kept as first drawn; where they differ, 42-29 wins (on 42-19 the ⓘ beside "Saves" and the "with Bog folk" captions are replaced).
- **The frames are drawn by hand, or typed onto the real screen in a browser.** They show what the build looks like, never how to build it: build in each part's own stylesheet, tokens only.
- **Parts are changed, never copied.** The saves are one piece, `SaveTiles` (`src/app/FigureSheets.tsx`), placed by the sheet (`src/app/CharacterSheet.tsx`) for the Player tab and the full sheet's page alike. `src/app/player/PlayerVitals.tsx` holds no save and no row here edits it.
- **One counting, read everywhere.** Row 1's function is the only place a save's number is worked out; the tile, the heading's line and the sheet read its result and do no sums of their own.
- **Test characters** (all invented, added to `src/testing/layoutP1Character.ts` as `p1=` values): `saves` (Brannoch as page 42 draws him: Fever 13 with "11 vs venom", Wisps 12 counted, +1 against magic from Lore), `savesmessy` (two conditions on one save, the long condition, a counted bonus and one at times on one save), `saveslow` (low rolls succeed), `savesstop` (level 12: the stops at both ends).
- **Cases ruled in words, not drawn** (rulings 6, 7, 8, 9 and 11; the full sheet's page and the GM's view of it, ruling 12): the builder builds them as ruled and shows each one's built screen in its pull request's text; Francis approves them from those pictures.
- **Every pull request with a screen** shows the built screen beside its frame, at 375, 1024 and 1180 px, light and dark, and brings the Code column of 06 (Save row, Breakdown sheet, Roll row, Stat) up to date.
- **Tests.** Each batch adds its own layout spec file (named below). Each test's purpose is the plain sentence in its row.
- **No migration, no stored data changes, no new command.**
