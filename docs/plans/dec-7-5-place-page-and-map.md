## What exists (the Builder's facts, from main, 2026-11-13)
- `assets` has kind `map`, with visibility gm / all / users (default gm). Only the GM writes one; the GM always reads; a member reads when visibility is `all` or names them. Files sit in the private bucket `campaign-files` at `<campaign>/<asset id>`, with rules mirroring the table.
- A place already has a picture the GM adds from their device, "hidden until you show it", with its eye (m5b). That is the pattern this plan reuses.
- The importer leaves a pack's `images` out with a warning. Nothing creates a map asset; nothing links one to a place.
- `docs/07-content-packs.md` §9: a pack's `images` name each file, its type (`map`), title, alt, the locations it belongs to; the files stay with the GM (a folder picked at import), and a reveal copies into app storage.

## The approach (the lead's lean; ⚑ 1)
**A copy per campaign, added by the GM from their device**, exactly as a place's picture is today: the pack says which map belongs to which place and what the file is called; the GM picks the file once; the app stores a resized copy as that campaign's map asset, the GM's alone until its eye is opened. No new rule about who may read what: sharing is the asset's existing `all` visibility. The other route (the pack's pictures stored once at pack level, read by every campaign that attaches it) needs a new place to store files and a new read rule, and is left for later.

## Confirmed by the Builder from the code (2026-11-13, night)
- **The upload reuses; its command does not.** The device's shrink (`shrinkImage(file, max)`) is reused as it stands with a larger maximum. `place.addPicture` is fixed to a place's picture and the GM's eyes: a map takes a command of its own, or a kind on that one.
- **The tie is new.** A place's picture is a piece (`place_pieces.asset_id`); a map is not a piece. A map needs its own link from a place (a column or a link table): one add-only migration.
- **Sharing needs no new read rule.** A map asset with visibility `all` is already readable, row and file, by every member of the campaign. The picture's own eye is a per-visit showing of a piece, not this; so the map's eye (5m-3) is a new command that sets the asset's visibility, under the read rules as they are. ⚑ 3 below falls away.
- **An eye on a spot (5s) is 1½ turns the smallest way**: a town already shows a place in town through a key in the place's own record and a read for players; a region's spots can take the same road (a new kind there, the check, the read returning spots): one add-only migration. It widens what players read (a spot's number and name once its eye is open, before any of its pieces), so it needs Francis's yes in plain words. Until it is built, "Spots here" lists the spots with no eye and no count (page 11, frame 8).
