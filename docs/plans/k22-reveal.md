## What K22 changes, in one paragraph

Today an item from the setting's rules that should start unidentified carries its true name and its whole visible entry onto the line, and the app shows them: a player's own sheet of a cursed weapon reads "Magic bonus -1" (the driver's finding of 2026-11-21: `weapon.magicBonus` is neither `gmOnly` nor in `private.hidden_keys`, so the line's copy and the entry both carry it, and `describeEntry` prints it, `src/app/ItemSheet.tsx:184`), and the line's `name` is the entry's (`copy_item_details`, `supabase/migrations/20261024230000_m4_review_fixes.sql:177-178`). The GM reveals a hidden field with one button per field and "Reveal the count", with no Undo and the log saying only "revealed more about …". After K22: the database keeps an unidentified item's truth off every player's device until the GM tells it; the GM edits what players see from a row on his item sheet; "Reveal…" opens a pop-up of what is still hidden, one row per fact, a tap tells it at once with Undo; the player's sheet and log follow, and an undone tell leaves the players' log.
