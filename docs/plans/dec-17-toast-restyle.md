## Honest notes

- **Not run:** the app. Read from the source and from page 43's pictures.
- **Not drawn:** anything moving (the drain, the hold, the fade are still pictures); Larger text; an error toast with a button (the app has none).
- **Fragile:** touch and hover together. A finger lifted can leave a hover that never ends on some tablets; the tests above ask for it by name, and it wants a real iPad **and a phone** before the merge (tr-15 leans on the hold: with the 6-second top, holding is the only way to finish reading the six-line error, about 8 to 9 seconds of words).
- **Nothing on the toast says that holding stops it** (tr-14: a held toast looks like a running one). His rulings; the manual check on a tablet and a phone looks at whether a player finds the hold.
- **A test that must change, not only be added to:** `toastWrap.spec.ts` asserts today that the long toast's button drops under at 1180 px and that the bar spans the full width. Both were approved as built on 13 November (tw-1) and are changed by tr-5 and tr-6.
- **Costs build time for little:** the reduced-motion fade is a second animation that must also obey the pause. If it proves awkward, the builder stops and asks; it does not drop tr-4 or tr-10 quietly.
- **Costs build time:** the error's seconds (tr-8). The unit test environment lays nothing out, so the line count is proved twice: the sum in a unit test, the measuring in the layout check.
- **The redrawn 43H has had no second critique of its own drawing.** Francis approved the recommendations it is drawn to, then the redrawn pictures (25 Nov). Home Two re-checked those (35/40); the three frames redrawn to his picks of 26 Nov have had none since.
- **The disc narrows the words by 4 px,** so the garb swap's errors are three and six lines at 375 px, not the two and five the critique counted: 6 seconds each since tr-15.
- **Line numbers** will drift.
